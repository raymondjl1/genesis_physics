5.1 Cochlea Anatomy

Inner hair cells (IHCs): Approximately 3,500 sensory cells that convert basilar membrane vibrations into electrical nerve impulses. 7.5 Constraints and Limits

Gap size lower limit: Below ~20 nm, stiction forces (van der Waals adhesion) dominate and gaps collapse. Surface roughness of polished silicon is 0.3–1 nm RMS; at 15 nm gap this represents 5–7% of the gap, introducing significant non-uniformity. The design point of 50 nm provides safe margin.

Amplitude constraint: The oscillation amplitude must be much less than the gap to avoid contact. Design rule: Delta x < 0.1a. At a equals 50 nm, this gives Delta x < 5 nm. The reference design uses Delta x equals 1 nm (2% of gap).

Frequency constraint: The harvesting stage must match the cavity frequency. Piezoelectric materials max out at ~1 MHz; the rectenna approach supports the full 1.14 GHz cavity frequency.

Thermal limit: Maximum passive dissipation ~89 W for a 10 cm copper cube. With forced air cooling, up to ~280 W. The reference design produces 47.5 W waste heat, well within passive limits.

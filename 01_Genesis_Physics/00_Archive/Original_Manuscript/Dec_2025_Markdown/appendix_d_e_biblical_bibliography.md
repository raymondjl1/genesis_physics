## D.1 Genesis Creation Account (Primary Texts)

### Genesis 1:1-2:3 (Complete Creation Week)

**Genesis 1:1** - "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth"
- Initial creation of Zones 1 and 2
- Chapter 3 (Zone hierarchy), Chapter 14 (Day-by-day)

**Genesis 1:2** - "Earth formless and void, darkness over deep, Spirit hovering over waters"

- Waters exist before Day 1
- Chapter 5 (Waters), Chapter 13 (Thermodynamics)

**Genesis 1:3-5** - Day 1: Light created, separated from darkness
- Temporal Domain initialized (Zone 2.2)
- Chapter 7 (Three Fundamentals), Chapter 14

**Genesis 1:6-8** - Day 2: Firmament separates waters from waters
- Critical day: Membrane created (Zone 2.2.2)
- Waters Above (2.2.3) and Waters Below (2.2.1)
- Chapter 4 (Firmament), Chapter 5 (Waters)

**Genesis 1:9-10** - Day 3A: Waters Below gathered, dry land appears
- Matter condensation mechanism
- Chapter 9 (Matter Formation)

**Genesis 1:11-13** - Day 3B: Vegetation, "according to its kind"
- Life initialization, self-replication introduced
- Pattern boundaries established
- Chapter 14

**Genesis 1:14-19** - Day 4: Sun, moon, stars created
- Physical light sources (מְאֹרֹת, me'orot)
- Distinct from Day 1 Light (אוֹר, or)
- Chapter 14

**Genesis 1:20-23** - Day 5: Sea creatures and birds, "living creatures" (נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה)
- Sentient life (consciousness)
- First divine blessing
- Chapter 14

**Genesis 1:24-25** - Day 6A: Land animals created
- Livestock, creeping things, beasts
- Chapter 14

**Genesis 1:26-31** - Day 6B: Humanity created "in Our image"
- Zone interface (dual nature)
- Triple use of בָּרָא (bara, created)
- Dominion mandate
- "Very good" evaluation
- Chapter 14, Chapter 16 (Theological Implications)

**Genesis 2:1-3** - Day 7: Completion and rest
- System finished (וַיְכֻלּוּ, vayekhullu)
- God rested (וַיִּשְׁבֹּת, vayishbot)

- Chapter 11 (Principles), Chapter 13 (Thermodynamics)

- Chapter 16

## 11.1 Introduction: From Architecture to Laws

### 11.1.1 The Question This Chapter Answers

**Why do physical laws exist?**

Standard physics can describe laws with remarkable precision:
- Newton's laws of motion
- Maxwell's equations (electromagnetism)
- Einstein's field equations (gravity)
- Schrödinger equation (quantum mechanics)
- Conservation laws (energy, momentum, charge, etc.)

But standard physics **cannot explain why** these laws exist.

**Typical response**: "They just do. We observe them and write them down."

**Our framework goes deeper**: Physical laws are **not arbitrary** but derive from:
1. **Zone architecture** (structure of reality)
2. **God's nature** (unchanging, faithful, wise)
3. **Design purposes** (why creation was made this way)

This chapter identifies **five governing principles** that underlie all physical laws.

### 11.1.2 What Are "Governing Principles"?

**Governing principles** are fundamental constraints on how reality operates.

**Not laws themselves**, but **sources of laws**:
- Laws = specific mathematical relationships (F=ma, E=mc², etc.)
- Principles = why those relationships must hold

**Analogy**:
- **Constitution** = Governing principles (free speech, due process)
- **Statutes** = Specific laws (speed limits, tax code)
- Constitution constrains what statutes can be
- Similarly: Principles constrain what physical laws can be

**Our five principles**:

1. **Conservation Principle**: Total Substance + Movement in temporal domain is constant
2. **Degradation Principle**: Organized patterns naturally dissolve unless maintained
3. **Symmetry Principle**: Zone structure reflects God's unchanging nature
4. **Duality Principle**: Fundamental patterns come in complementary pairs
5. **Sustaining Principle**: God continuously maintains overall cosmic order

**From these five**, we derive:
- All conservation laws (energy, momentum, charge, etc.)
- All thermodynamic laws (0th, 1st, 2nd, 3rd)
- Symmetry-based physics (Noether's theorem)
- Particle-antiparticle pairing
- Why universe hasn't collapsed to maximum entropy

**Why entropy increases**:

**Pattern Rarity Argument**:

**Organized states** (low entropy):
- Specific arrangements (few ways to achieve)
- Example: Deck of cards in order (1 arrangement)
- Probability: Low

**Disordered states** (high entropy):
- Generic randomness (many ways to achieve)
- Example: Shuffled deck (52! ≈ 10⁶⁸ arrangements)
- Probability: High

**Random motion** explores states equally:
- No preference for organized vs. disordered
- But vastly more disordered states exist
- System naturally evolves toward high-probability states
- **Therefore**: Entropy increases

**In Firmament**:
- Organized patterns = rare configurations
- Disorder = common configurations
- Movement (vibration, thermal motion) randomizes
- Patterns degrade (disorder increases)

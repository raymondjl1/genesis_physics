## 5.1 What Are "Waters"?

### 5.1.1 Not H₂O

When Genesis speaks of "waters" (מַיִם, mayim) in the context of Days 1-2, this is **not referring to hydrogen and oxygen molecules**. The Waters are primordial creation energy—the fundamental substrate from which all physical reality emerges.

**Why not H₂O?**

**Chronological impossibility**:
- Waters mentioned Genesis 1:2 (before Day 1)
- Hydrogen created Day 3-4 (star formation begins)
- Oxygen created Day 4-5 (stellar nucleosynthesis)
- Water molecules (H₂O) created later
- Therefore: Genesis 1:2 "waters" ≠ H₂O

**Separation precedes matter**:
- Day 2: Waters separated into Above and Below
- Day 3: Matter condenses from Waters Below
- Water molecules are matter (condensed substance)
- Therefore: Waters exist before H₂O exists

**Universal scope**:
- Waters fill all of Zone 2 initially (Genesis 1:2)
- H₂O is localized substance (oceans, ice, vapor on Earth)
- Waters Above remain "above the firmament" (entire cosmic scale)
- Therefore: Waters ≠ localized H₂O

**State description**:
- Waters described as formless, void, deep (Genesis 1:2)
- H₂O has definite properties (solid/liquid/gas states)
- Waters can be "separated," "gathered," transformed
- Therefore: Waters = more fundamental substrate than molecules

### 5.1.2 Hebrew Terminology

**מַיִם** (mayim) - "waters"

**Key features**:

**Always plural**:
- Hebrew: מַיִם (mayim), never singular מַי
- Dual or plural form (ending in -ַיִם)
- Implies multiplicity, abundance, vast expanse
- Or: Two complementary aspects (Waters Above + Waters Below)

**Root meaning**:
- Possibly from מָה (mah) = "what?" (mysterious, undefined)
- Or related to Egyptian "Nun" (primordial waters of chaos)
- Emphasizes: Primordial, fundamental, formless

**Biblical usage contexts**:

**1. Literal water** (H₂O):
- Genesis 7:17 - "Waters of the flood"
- Exodus 15:10 - "They sank like lead in the mighty waters"
- Psalm 23:2 - "He leads me beside still waters"

**2. Primordial Waters** (creation substrate):
- Genesis 1:2 - "Spirit of God hovering over face of the waters"
- Genesis 1:6-7 - "Waters above... waters below"
- 2 Peter 3:5 - "Earth formed out of water and through water"
- Psalm 104:6 - "The deep covered it like a garment"

**3. Metaphorical**:
- Psalm 69:1 - "Waters have come up to my neck" (distress)
- Song 8:7 - "Many waters cannot quench love"

**Context determines meaning**: In Genesis 1:2-10, context clearly indicates primordial creation substrate, not H₂O.

2.3 Valley of Stability Derivation

where Z is atomic number and R is nuclear radius.

Nucleons are fermions obeying Pauli exclusion. Unequal numbers of neutrons and protons raise the Fermi energy:

where N is neutron number and A = N+Z is mass number.

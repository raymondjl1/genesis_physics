Chapter 11: The Five Governing Principles11.1 Introduction: Principles from Divine AttributesA building requires organizing principles. Zone architecture requires principles derived from God's character: Conservation, Degradation, Symmetry, Duality, and Sustaining. Each flows from a divine attribute and manifests in physical law.11.2 Principle 1: ConservationThe Conservation Principle: Total Substance and Movement in Zone 2.2 remain constant after Day 7 completion. Genesis 2:1-2 records God finished His work and rested. Creation is complete. No new creation enters Zone 2.2 after Day 7. Ecclesiastes 3:14: 'nothing can be added to it, nor anything taken from it.' Total amount is fixed.Observable physics: Energy conservation, momentum conservation. These manifest the single truth: creation is complete and bounded.11.3 Principle 2: DegradationThe Degradation Principle: Entropy increases in spontaneous processes. Standard physics treats this as natural law. Christian view recognizes designed feature. Romans 8:20-22 describes creation groaning. Genesis 3:17-19: work requires effort. Degradation is designed to require human stewardship, remove post-Fall perfection, prepare creation for renewal.Four purposes: (1) Mark time direction; (2) Remove perfection, drive toward redemption; (3) Require sustained work; (4) Enable renewal through maximum entropy.11.4 Principle 3: SymmetryThe Symmetry Principle: God's unchanging nature reflects in mathematical symmetries. Malachi 3:6 'I do not change.' James 1:17 'no variation.' God is immutable. If Creator unchanged, creation's laws exhibit symmetries. Time-translation symmetry, spatial-translation symmetry, rotational symmetry express this constancy.Emmy Noether proved: every continuous symmetry yields a conservation law. God's immutability yields every known conservation law through this connection.11.5 Principle 4: DualityThe Duality Principle: Fundamental patterns come in complementary pairs. Genesis structures creation through pairing: Light/Darkness, Waters Above/Below, Male/Female. Binary structure is fundamental to creative method.Matter-antimatter paired. Positive-negative charges paired. Creating one requires creating other. This explains why these patterns exist and why creating one demands creating the opposite.11.6 Principle 5: SustainingThe Sustaining Principle: God actively maintains creation at every moment. Colossians 1:17 'in him all things hold together.' Hebrews 1:3 'sustaining all things.' Creation doesn't exist independently. God continuously maintains it. This is theism: God's direct, immediate action required.Observable result: Universe is law-governed. Constants don't drift. Atoms don't decay unexpectedly. Physics assumes zones remain stable because God sustains the laws.PrincipleDivine AttributeObservable ResultConservationCompletenessEnergy/momentum conservedDegradationRedemptive intentEntropy increasesSymmetryImmutabilityTime/space/rotational symmetryDualityCreative methodMatter-antimatter pairingSustainingActive presenceLaws stable and consistentThese five principles form unified system. Conservation ensures creation persists. Degradation enables change and redemption. Symmetry ensures regularity. Duality ensures structure. Sustaining ensures stability.11.7 ConclusionFive governing principles are theological necessities—expressions of God's character encoded in zone architecture. They explain why physical laws exist and why they have observed properties.

## The Five Principles (canonical order)

| # | Principle | One-sentence definition | Biblical anchor |
|---|-----------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Sustaining** | God continuously upholds all creation through active presence and power; without sustaining, the cosmos collapses to entropy maximum. | Col 1:17; Heb 1:3 |
| 2 | **Conservation** | Within the closed cosmic system (Zone 2.2, post-Day 7), nothing is created or destroyed — total energy, charge, baryon and lepton numbers are preserved. | Mal 3:6 |
| 3 | **Symmetry** | God's unchanging nature is reflected in the gauge and spacetime symmetries of physics; Noether's theorem links each symmetry to a conserved current. | (see Vol 1 Ch 8 footnote — do not change) |
| 4 | **Degradation** | During the Fall phase, the sustaining field is subcritical (κ_partial < κ_full) and entropy production becomes positive (dS/dt > 0), producing decay, aging, and the arrow of time. | Rom 8:20 |
| 5 | **Duality** | God creates through complementary pairs (Waters Above/Below, matter/antimatter, male/female) whose interaction generates pattern and complexity. | Gen 1:27 |

---

## Why this order

1. **Sustaining** is foundational — nothing exists without God's active will.
2. **Conservation** follows — once creation is sustained, its integrity is preserved.
3. **Symmetry** deepens — conservation laws emerge from immutable symmetries (Noether).
4. **Degradation** is the Phase-3 exception — sustaining weakens, symmetry breaks, entropy rises.
5. **Duality** weaves through all — complementary pairs appear in every other principle.

## Overview and Canonical Ordering

The Five Governing Principles are listed in canonical order, which reflects their logical and theological dependencies. **DO NOT refer to these as "Hierarchy," "Balance," or any other alternative name.** The term "Principles" and the numbered ordering are authoritative.

The ordering is rational:
1. **Sustaining** is foundational (God acts in creation)
2. **Conservation** depends on Sustaining (sustained closure)
3. **Symmetry** depends on Conservation (unchanging nature → physical law)
4. **Degradation** depends on Symmetry-breaking (loss of perfect symmetry in Fall)
5. **Duality** manifests through all others (complementary pairs throughout creation)

## Principle 1: Sustaining

**Divine Attribute**: Active Presence (God is not distant)

**Full Definition**: God continuously maintains all existence moment-by-moment through active sustaining power. The universe does not exist independently; it is upheld by divine presence.

**Theological Basis**:
- Colossians 1:17: "In him all things hold together"
- Hebrews 1:3: "[Christ] sustains all things by his powerful word"
- Acts 17:28: "In him we live and move and have our being"

**Physical Manifestation**:
- Sustaining field κ (power density, units [ML⁻¹T⁻³])
- Four phases of κ: κ_create, κ_full, κ_partial, κ_redeem
- Energy budget: dU = κ × V × dt (power injection into volume V)

**Key Equations**:
- Energy input: dU/dt = κ(t) × Volume (creation and maintenance)
- First Law: dE = δQ - δW + δE_external (accounting for divine input)
- Sustaining requirement: Without κ, universe collapses to entropy maximum

**Dependence on Other Principles**:
- Foundational; all other principles depend on continuous sustaining
- Enables Conservation (sustaining holds boundaries)
- Enables Symmetry (sustaining enforces pattern stability)
- Enables Degradation (κ weakens in Fall, allowing dS/dt > 0)
- Enables Duality (κ mediates interaction between complementary fields)

**Observational Support**:
- Flatness of universe: Ω_total ≈ 1 (maintains critical density)
- Coupling constant stability: α = 1/137.036 to 10+ decimal places
- Cosmological constant: Λ constant over billions of years (no evolution)
- Fine-tuning: Conditions for chemistry and life require κ precision < 10⁻⁶⁰

**Implication for Book 2**:
- God is not a "clockmaker" who winds up creation and withdraws
- Every atom is held in existence by continuous divine will
- Human stewardship operates within divine sustaining (not independent)
- Prayer and miracles are possible because κ can be locally modulated by divine agency

## Principle 2: Conservation

**Divine Attribute**: Completeness (God's work is perfect and entire)

**Full Definition**: Nothing is created or destroyed within the material cosmos after Day 7. The universe forms a closed thermodynamic system (within Zone 2.2) in which total energy and fundamental particle counts remain constant.

**Theological Basis**:
- Genesis 2:1-3: "God rested on the seventh day... Thus the heavens and the earth were completed"
- Ecclesiastes 3:14: "Whatever God does endures forever; nothing can be added to it nor anything taken from it"
- Colossians 1:16-17: "All things were created... and in him all things hold together"

**Physical Manifestation**:
- Closed system: Zone 2.2 (Earth Prime + Firmament Domain) conserves E, p, L, Q, B, L_lepton
- Post-Day-7 closure: No external energy input except sustaining field κ
- Conservation laws: Derived from symmetries via Noether's theorem

**Key Equations**:
- Energy conservation: dE/dt|_Zone2.2 = 0 (no net energy flow out of cosmos)
- Partition: E_total = E_Waters-Above + E_Waters-Below + E_Baryonic = constant
- Baryon number: B_total = constant (no nucleon creation)
- Charge: Q_total = 0 (universe electrically neutral)

**Dependence on Other Principles**:
- Requires Sustaining (κ_full maintains closure against entropy dissipation)
- Supports Symmetry (conservation laws emerge from unchanging symmetries)
- Enables Degradation only as Phase-3 exception (κ_partial breaks perfect conservation → dS/dt > 0)
- Basis for Duality (complementary fields conserve total charge and quantum numbers)

**Observational Support**:
- Big Bang nucleosynthesis: Baryon number abundance matches primordial ratio
- Charge neutrality: Universe electric dipole moment < 10⁻³⁵ e·cm
- CPT symmetry: Particle-antiparticle mass equality to 10⁻¹⁸ precision
- Energy budget: Ω_total = 0.999 ± 0.001 (critical density achieved)

**Implication for Book 2**:
- We cannot "make energy" or "destroy matter"; we only transform
- Stewardship is redistribution, not creation (Gen 1:28-29)
- Eschatological promise: "new heaven and new earth" ≠ replacement, but renewal/restoration
- Human sin does not "destroy" creation but disrupts its pattern (Fall as phase transition)

## Principle 3: Symmetry

**Divine Attribute**: Immutability (God's nature never changes)

**Full Definition**: God's unchanging nature is reflected in the symmetries of physics. Noether's theorem mathematically links each symmetry to a conservation law, showing that immutable divine attributes generate eternal physical laws.

**Theological Basis**:
- Malachi 3:6: "I the Lord do not change"
- James 1:17: "Every good and perfect gift is from above... the Father of the heavenly lights... who does not change"
- Proverbs 8:22-31: Divine wisdom as pattern-giver ("I was there when he set the heavens in place")

**Physical Manifestation**:
- Gauge symmetries: U(1)_EM (electromagnetism), SU(2)_weak, SU(3)_strong
- Spacetime symmetries: Poincaré group (rotation, translation, boost invariance)
- Discrete symmetries: Charge conjugation (C), parity reflection (P), time reversal (T), combined CPT
- Noether's theorem: Symmetry → Conservation law (one-to-one correspondence)

**Key Equations**:
- Noether's theorem: Continuous symmetry ↔ conserved current
  - Time-translation symmetry → Energy conservation (dE/dt = 0)
  - Spatial translation → Momentum conservation (dp/dt = 0)
  - Rotation → Angular momentum conservation (dL/dt = 0)
- Gauge symmetry: Local phase freedom U(1) → Photon (massless, long-range)
- CPT theorem: Fundamental invariance of Standard Model Lagrangian under C⊗P⊗T

**Dependence on Other Principles**:
- Depends on Sustaining (κ maintains symmetry against quantum fluctuations)
- Depends on Conservation (stable symmetries imply conserved quantities)
- Enables Degradation (Phase 3 breaks CP symmetry locally, allowing decay)
- Intertwined with Duality (complementary pairs exhibit pairwise symmetries)

**Observational Support**:
- Standard Model symmetries confirmed to 15+ decimal places
- CPT symmetry: Electron-positron mass difference < 10⁻²⁵ × electron mass
- Gauge coupling unification: α_EM, α_weak, α_strong meet at GUT scale
- Noether predictions: Photon masslessness (U(1) gauge symmetry)

**Implication for Book 2**:
- Laws of physics are not arbitrary; they reflect divine character
- Conservation laws are trustworthy: God does not arbitrarily violate them
- Beauty and elegance in physics reflect God's immutable wisdom
- Humans, made in God's image, can discover these laws through reason (Logos principle)

## Principle 4: Degradation

**Divine Attribute**: Redemptive Intent (Judgment as catalyst for return)

**Full Definition**: During the Fall phase (Phase 3), patterns in creation tend toward disorder as the sustaining field weakens (κ_partial < κ_full). Entropy production becomes positive (dS/dt > 0), causing aging, decay, and eventual heat death. This is not accident but judgment—a divine call to redemption and restoration.

**Theological Basis**:
- Genesis 3:17-19: "The ground is cursed because of you... thorns and thistles it will produce... by the sweat of your brow you will eat your food"
- Romans 8:20-21: "The creation was subjected to frustration... in hope that the creation itself will be liberated from its bondage to decay"
- Ecclesiastes 1:2, 3:14: "Meaningless, everything is meaningless... whatever God does endures forever; nothing can be added, nothing taken away" (futility as built-in limit)
- Hebrews 1:11: "They will perish, but you remain; they will all wear out like a garment"

**Physical Manifestation**:
- Sustaining field weakened: κ = κ_full × (1 - ε) where ε ~ 10⁻²⁷ to 10⁻⁶⁰
- Entropy production: dS/dt = σ(ε, κ_partial) > 0 (positive rate)
- Degradation timescale: τ_aging = ln(2) / (dS/dt) ~ billions of years (universe age)
- Phase-3-only phenomenon: Does not occur in Phases 1, 2, or 4

**Key Equations**:
- Aging rate: dS/dt = κ_full × (ε / τ_characteristic) (linear in subcriticality)
- Radioactive decay: N(t) = N₀ exp(-λt) where λ ∝ ε (decay constant)
- Stellar lifetime: τ_Main-Sequence ~ 10¹¹ years / M_star (mass-dependent burnout)
- Hubble expansion: H(t) governs cosmic cooling and matter dilution
- Heat death: S_final ≈ S_max = k_B × A / (4 l_P²) (Bekenstein-Hawking entropy)

**Dependence on Other Principles**:
- Requires Sustaining to allow κ weakening (complete withdrawal would mean nonexistence)
- Violates Conservation of energy locally (dE/dt ≠ 0 in subsystems due to heat dissipation)
- Violates Symmetry perfectly: CP and time-reversal broken in weak interactions; dS/dt > 0 breaks T-symmetry
- Manifests through Duality: Matter-antimatter imbalance causes irreversibility

**Observational Support**:
- Radioactive abundance: Half-lives of Rb⁸⁷, U²³⁸ determined from cosmology
- Stellar evolution: Main-sequence lifetimes match τ_aging scale
- Cosmic expansion: H₀ = 67.4 km/s/Mpc; Λ-dominated in present epoch
- Cosmic microwave background: Temperature T = 2.73 K (cooling from hot birth)
- Second Law: dS/dt_universe > 0 observed everywhere (thermodynamic irreversibility)

**Implication for Book 2**:
- Suffering, death, and decay are real and consequential (not illusion)
- Yet they are not final: degradation itself signals divine judgment calling to repentance
- Stewardship includes *slowing* decay where possible (medicine, agriculture, engineering)
- Redemption phase (future) will reverse degradation: "See, I am making all things new" (Rev 21:5)

## Principle 5: Duality

**Divine Attribute**: Creative Method (Complementary opposition as generative)

**Full Definition**: God creates through complementary dualities—opposing or balancing pairs that interact to generate complexity, pattern, and life. These dualities are not conflicts but creative partnerships reflecting the dynamic nature of divine wisdom.

**Theological Basis**:
- Genesis 1:27: "So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them" (duality in humanity)
- John 1:1-3: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... Through him all things were made" (Logos as duality: divine and creative)
- Proverbs 8:22-31: Wisdom personified as female creative agent alongside masculine God
- Duality throughout Creation: Light/darkness, sea/sky, male/female, Spirit/matter

**Physical Manifestation**:
- Waters Above (Ψ_A, dark energy, repulsive, w ≈ -1) vs. Waters Below (Ψ_B, dark matter, attractive, w ≈ 0)
- Matter (positive charge, mass) vs. Antimatter (negative charge, opposite mass-energy)
- Masculine/feminine principles in created order (complementary, not competitive)
- Spirit (Z₂.₁, atemporal, infinite) vs. Matter (Z₂.₂, temporal, finite)
- Yang/yin, masculine/feminine cosmic energies (if theological cosmology extends to Eastern frameworks)

**Key Equations**:
- Creation operator: Ψ_creation = Ψ_A ⊗ Ψ_B (tensor product; interaction creates universe)
- Charge conjugation: q → -q symmetry in Standard Model
- Baryon asymmetry: N_matter - N_antimatter ≈ 10⁻⁹ × N_total (precise ratio, not zero)
- Energy partitioning: E_Waters-Above : E_Waters-Below : E_Baryonic = 68.4 : 26.6 : 4.9 (trinity-like threefold)
- Feminine-masculine cosmic tension (theological): Creates dynamism, prevents static perfection

**Dependence on Other Principles**:
- Enabled by Sustaining: κ mediates interaction between dual fields (Ψ_A and Ψ_B)
- Respects Conservation: Total charge, baryon number, energy conserved across dualities
- Reflects Symmetry: CPT and gauge symmetries protect duality structure
- Generates Degradation: Matter-antimatter imbalance in Fall phase yields irreversibility
- Integrates all principles: Duality is how all creation is woven together

**Observational Support**:
- CMB power spectrum: Anisotropies reflect balance of dark energy and matter
- Nucleosynthesis: Precise antimatter/matter ratio (not random)
- Galaxy formation: Dark matter and baryonic matter interact to seed structure
- Neutrino oscillations: Duality between flavor eigenstates
- Universe expansion: Dark energy (repulsive) vs. dark matter (attractive) dynamism

**Implication for Book 2**:
- Creation is not unilateral (God speaking alone) but dialogical (creative response between dualities)
- Humans embody duality: spirit and matter, rational and emotional, male and female, individual and communal
- Stewardship requires balancing: growth vs. conservation, innovation vs. tradition, justice vs. mercy
- Redemption restores perfect duality: new creation where Spirit and matter are transparently unified
- Love (1 John 4:8) may be the ultimate duality: giver and receiver, self and other, in eternal communion

## Canonical Ordering Rationale

The Five Principles build logically upon one another:

1. **Sustaining** is absolutely first: Nothing exists without God's active will. All else depends on this.

2. **Conservation** follows: Once creation is sustained, its integrity—that nothing is lost—follows from divine completeness.

3. **Symmetry** deepens: The fact that conservation laws exist (energy, momentum, etc.) reflects God's unchanging nature encoded in physical symmetries.

4. **Degradation** is the exception: Only when Phase 3 begins does sustaining weaken (κ_partial), breaking symmetry and allowing dS/dt > 0. Degradation is a *violation* of the first three principles, signaling judgment.

5. **Duality** underlies all: Complementary opposites appear throughout Sustaining (κ_A and κ_B), Conservation (charge, baryon number pairing), Symmetry (particle-antiparticle), and Degradation (asymmetry breaking). Duality is the creative method encoding all the others.

**Why Not "Hierarchy" or "Balance"?**
- "Hierarchy" suggests domination; the principles interact as an integrated system, not a pyramid.
- "Balance" suggests static equilibrium; the principles are dynamically creative, especially through Duality.
- "Principles" conveys foundational truth; "Governing" emphasizes their role in all cosmic processes.

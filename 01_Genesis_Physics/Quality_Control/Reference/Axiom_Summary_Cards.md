**Symbols Introduced**: κ_partial (weakened sustaining field), ε (subcriticality parameter), τ_aging (decay timescale)

**Dependencies**: Axiom 1 (sustaining field concept), Axiom 2 (closed system boundary), Axiom 3 (symmetry breaking)

**Testable Prediction**: Radioactive decay rates, stellar lifetimes, and entropy production should scale with ε; potential future measurement of ε via ultra-precise atomic clocks.

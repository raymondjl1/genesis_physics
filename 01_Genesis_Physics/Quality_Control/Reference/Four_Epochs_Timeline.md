## Phase 3: Fall (Fall Event to Redemption)

**Temporal Characteristics**:
- Proper duration: ~13.8 billion years (current epoch)
- Initiated by human disobedience (Genesis 3)
- Sustaining field κ suddenly reduces to κ_partial
- Time-reversal symmetry broken; entropy begins increasing
- Continues until Redemption phase begins

**Sustaining Field**:
- κ = κ_full × (1 - ε) where ε ~ 10⁻²⁷ to 10⁻⁶⁰
- Subcritical: decay > repair (repair rate < zero-order loss)
- Fundamental constants and κ values fixed at Fall transition
- Cannot recover Edenic state through natural processes alone

**Entropy Dynamics**:
- dS/dt > 0: Irreversible increase in disorder
- Heat death: universe approaches maximum entropy state
- Aging: all patterns gradually degrade
- Universe-wide: H_Fall = 67.4 km/s/Mpc (measured; standard Friedmann)

**Observable Access**:
- Direct: We live in Phase 3
- Radioactive decay (exponential loss timescales)
- Stellar evolution (main-sequence lifetimes, post-main-sequence cooling)
- Cosmic expansion (Hubble recession, dark energy acceleration)
- Particle physics: decay modes, instability thresholds

**Key Equations**:
- Degradation: dS/dt = σ(ε) > 0 (σ is entropy production rate)
- Decay timescale: τ = ln(2) / λ where λ ∝ κ_partial
- Hubble evolution: H²(a) = H₀²[Ω_m a⁻³ + Ω_Λ] (ΛCDMmodel)
- Age: t_0 ≈ 13.8 Gyr (directly observed; cosmologically inferred)

**Observable Signatures**:
- CMB temperature anisotropies (2.73 K, tiny fluctuations)
- Large-scale structure (galaxy distribution, dark matter scaffolding)
- Supernova distances (evidence for accelerating expansion)
- Radioactive abundance (decay chains of heavy elements)
- Stellar ages (turnoff ages in globular clusters)

**Theological Meaning**:
- Consequence of breaking covenant with Creator
- All suffering, death, decay trace back to Fall
- Yet divine providence continues (sustaining field not withdrawn)
- Human responsibility for stewardship remains despite Fall effects

**References**:

- Romans 8:20-21 (creation groans under futility)
- Ecclesiastes 3:14 (nothing can be added or taken away; entropy as futility)
- Hebrews 1:11 (heavens perish; wax old like a garment)

**Initiation**:

**Phase Transition Dynamics**:
- First-order transition: κ drops discontinuously from κ_full to κ_partial

- New equilibrium: Universe stabilizes in degradation (dS/dt > 0)
- Irreversibility: Cannot return to Edenic state without external power

**Consequences Cascade**:
1. Matter aging: Radioactive decay enables
2. Biological decay: Death enters creation (τ_aging < ∞)
3. Cosmic expansion: Accelerating (Λ-driven; Waters Above repulsion)
4. Human struggle: Sweat of brow; labor; pain in childbirth (Gen 3:17-19)

- Entropy anomalies: Initial order vs. predicted chaos (fine-tuning problem)
- Decay patterns: Consistent with ε ~ 10⁻²⁷ to 10⁻⁶⁰ (not natural limits)

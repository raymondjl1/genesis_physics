## 9. Zone Numbering Rules

### Nested System (Authoritative)

The nested numbering system is the authoritative technical notation and MUST be used in:
- Research documents and peer discussion
- Mathematical models and simulations
- Inter-zone boundary analysis
- Precision cosmological predictions

**Structure**: the nested hierarchy reads:
- Z₂ = Earth Prime (temporal material cosmos)
- Z₂.₂ = Firmament Domain (the Firmament membrane and surrounding structure; observable universe)
- Z₂.₂.₂ = the Firmament membrane (4D)
- Z₂.₂.₂.₁ = Condensed Matter (baryonic subset that condenses on the membrane)

### Simplified System (Pedagogical)

For accessibility and Book 2 readability, simplified numbering (Zones 1-4) is permitted WITH parenthetical clarification:

| Simplified | Nested | Name | Context |
|-----------|--------|------|---------|
| Zone 1 | Z₁ | Heaven Prime | Transcendent causality |
| Zone 2 | Z₂ | Earth Prime | Complete material cosmos |
| Zone 3 | Z₂.₂.₃ | Waters Above | Dark energy (readers see "cosmic repulsion") |
| Zone 4 | Z₂.₂.₁ + Z₂.₂.₂.₁ | Waters Below + Condensed Matter | Matter/gravity scaffold (readers see "stars and atoms") |

**Example Book 2 usage**: "The Firmament (our observable universe, or Zone 2 in technical notation) consists of three nested layers..."

### Mapping Table

| Context | System | Rule |
|---------|--------|------|
| Technical research | Nested (Z₀-Z₂.₂.₃) | Always use full hierarchy |

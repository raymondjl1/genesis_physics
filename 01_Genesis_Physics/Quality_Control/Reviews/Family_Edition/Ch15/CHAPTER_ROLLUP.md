## SYNTHESIS: WHAT THIS CHAPTER DOES WELL

1. **Names Christ with reverence, not urgency.** John 1:1–3, Colossians 1:15–20 (full), Hebrews 1:1–3 (full) are quoted in context. The move from handiwork to Craftsman is explicitly motivated and theologically sound.

2. **Presents Gospel clearly AND pastorally.** §6 (Ephesians 2:8–10, *poiema*) is the "Gospel-audible" moment. An unbelieving teen hears the offer clearly ("grace, faith, Christ's work on cross, not earned"). A believing family feels deepened ("I am a carefully-made thing, the same way the cosmos is made"). No pressure. No sales-pitch.

3. **Holds epistemic boundaries.** "I cannot prove this to you from physics" (§3, §6, §9) appears three times, each time at a different vulnerability level, each time reinforcing rather than undermining the Gospel claim. The cleared-community posture is applied to salvation itself.

4. **Uses architecture as theology bridge.** §4 (Two Specifications, One Architect) is the franchise's climax — same hand wrote both columns (physics spec and redemption spec). This is the book's deepest move.

5. **Pastoral honesty prevents tone-deafness.** §5's qualifier on Romans 8:28 ("does NOT mean every event feels good in the moment; good is at the level of the whole arc") prevents the verse being weaponized against suffering readers.

6. **Holds the already-not-yet tension well.** §8 (2 Corinthians 5:17, *kainē ktisis*) shows the new-creation work already begun in believers, while §7 (Revelation 21:1–5) holds the promise not-yet. No over-realized eschatology.

7. **Ends with doxology, not conclusions.** §10 (Romans 11:33–36) correctly identifies worship as the destination, not another argument. The engineer puts down the pencil and says *amen.* This is operationally and theologically honest.

8. **Voice is exact.** Throughout the chapter, the operator's voice is clear — the man who ran command staffs, pioneered MBSE, and holds TS/SCI clearance — not a theologian trying on an engineer's hat. Readers trust this voice.

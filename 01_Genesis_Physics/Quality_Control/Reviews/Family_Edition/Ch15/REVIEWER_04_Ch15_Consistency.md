- Distinguished from aorist (one-time action): "Christ purged the sins" (aorist, done) vs. "upholding all things" (present, continuous)
- Architecture: "the sustaining coupling, a field described in *Foundations Volume 4, Chapter 1*"

**Ch15 Usage (§3, §7, §10):**

- §10: same continuous-present language

**Consistency Check:** ✓ **CONSISTENT.** Ch15 correctly references Ch13's treatment of *pheron*; no new claims introduced; the chapter synthesizes without contradiction.

#### D.2 Colossians 1:17 *Synistemi* — The Cohering

**Ch13 Introduction (§3):**
- Greek: συνέστηκεν (*synistemi*), perfect tense
- Meaning: *hold together, cause to cohere, compose into a single whole*
- Perfect tense: "completed action with continuing result"
- Quote: "by Christ, all things were composed-into-a-whole, and they remain composed-into-a-whole *because of him*"
- Distinguished from *pheron*: *synistemi* names the *cohering* (structural integrity); *pheron* names the *upholding* (load-bearing)

**Ch15 Usage (§3, §4):**
- §3: Re-quotes Colossians 1:15–17, adding vv. 18–20 (redemption half)
- §4: "the same *panta* that runs through Colossians 1 and Hebrews 1 runs through Romans 8:28"
- No new claims; pure synthesis

**Consistency Check:** ✓ **CONSISTENT.** Ch15 correctly references Ch13's distinction between *pheron* and *synistemi*; adds context that both apply to creation and redemption, but does not alter the physics definitions.

## CRITICAL FINDINGS BY SEVERITY

### P1 — BLOCKING VOICE VIOLATION

#### [P1] [C1 — Voice/Household] Invented children (Sarah, Daniel) violate explicit voice rule. (Flagged by: REVIEWER-08, corroborated by household memo.)

**Location:** Lines 308–309, §9 (closing section)

**Current text:**
> "A trillion stars by name; but also Sarah at the kitchen table, also Daniel still up doing math homework, also the parent reading to them, also you."

**Problem:** The author (Jeff Raymond) and his wife do not have children. The memory doc (user_household.md) and AUTHOR_VOICE_AND_BACKGROUND.md (line 98) are explicit: "The author and his wife do not have children; do not invent any."

**Impact:** This is a direct violation of the author-voice contract. It breaks character by introducing fictional children to illustrate a pastoral point that does not require them.

**Fix (required before publication):**

Option 1 (direct to reader):
> "A trillion stars by name; and the Maker of them names each one of those stars just as completely as he names you, who at this moment are reading a book you bought for your family."

Option 2 (generic family language):
> "A trillion stars by name; but also the person sitting in your kitchen, also the parent reading this aloud to a teenager who is listening, also you. The Maker does not lose count, and the Maker knows each name."

Both options preserve the pastoral close without inventing children.

**Confidence:** HIGH — This is an explicit voice rule, clearly stated in the memory documentation and AUTHOR_VOICE_AND_BACKGROUND.md.

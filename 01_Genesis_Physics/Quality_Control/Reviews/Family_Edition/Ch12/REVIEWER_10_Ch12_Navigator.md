> *"Chapter 12: Black Holes, Dark Energy, and the Heavens Declare"*

It indicates that when Ch11 was written, the book's chapter outline may have been different, or Ch11's trailer was drafted but never synchronized with the canonical outline. The trailer needs to be **rewritten to forecast the actual Ch12** that now exists in the canonical outline.

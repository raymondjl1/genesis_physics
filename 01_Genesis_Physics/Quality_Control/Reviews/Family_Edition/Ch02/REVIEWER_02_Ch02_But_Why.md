### P3 (Polish)

#### [C3 — Intuition First] §4: Dark energy explanation could lead slightly faster
**Issue:** The 1998 discovery paragraph (starting "Then, in 1998, two independent teams...") is *correct* and well-explained, but it reads as a narrative of surprise rather than as intuition-building. A reader encounters: "They thought it must be slowing down... It wasn't... It was *speeding up.*" 

This is excellent drama, but the physical intuition ("if space is expanding and gravity pulls inward, shouldn't expansion slow down?") arrives *only after* the surprise is revealed. 

**Quote:** *"They were trying to figure out how fast the expansion of the universe was slowing down, because it **had** to be slowing down — gravity pulls everything toward everything else, and an expanding universe under gravity's pull is a decelerating universe. It wasn't slowing down. It was *speeding up.*"*

**Fix:** Consider adding one sentence of intuition *before* the surprise, or reorganizing the order to: (1) "Here's what physicists expected..." (2) "Here's why they expected it..." (3) "But measurement showed..." This would make the passage align even more tightly with the "intuition first" mandate. Non-blocking because the intuition *is* present, just in aftermath order rather than lead order.

**Severity:** P3 (polish)  
**Concern tag:** C3 (Physical Intuition First)

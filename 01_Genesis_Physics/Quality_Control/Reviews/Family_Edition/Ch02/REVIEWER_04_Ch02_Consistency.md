### P2 — Minor Issues

#### Issue M2.1 — Zone Notation Clarity (C4 — Cross-Reference Tag)
**Location:** §3, line 103–104 (Key Terms section)

**What Ch02 says:**  
"In the technical Foundations encyclopedia these are labeled Z₂.₂.₃ (Waters Above), Z₂.₂.₂ (Firmament), and Z₂.₂.₁ (Waters Below) — the numbering is a bookkeeping convenience; the *names* are what you need."

**Canonical Source:**  
`Zone_Architecture.md`, Table 6, §9 (Simplified System rule): "For Book 2 readability, simplified numbering (Zones 1–4) is permitted WITH parenthetical clarification."

**Finding:**  
The chapter uses simplified naming throughout (Waters Above / Firmament / Waters Below, in plain English) and provides nested notation in parenthesis on first mention (§3). This is *correct per the mapping rule*. However, the Key Terms callout (line 103–104) restates the mapping *after* the prose has already established it, which is pedagogically redundant but not inaccurate. The statement "the numbering is a bookkeeping convenience; the *names* are what you need" is also correct and reinforces the Family Edition's scope.

**Severity:** Minor. The redundancy is not an error; it is a teaching reinforcement.

**Action:** None. The notation is correct.

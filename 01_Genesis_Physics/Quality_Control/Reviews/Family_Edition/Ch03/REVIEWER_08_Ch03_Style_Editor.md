### Priority 1 (High — Style Enforcement Issue)

#### P1-C4: Inconsistent capitalization of "Scripture" in derived phrase contexts

**Finding:** Two instances of lowercase "scripture frame" appear where "Scripture frame" (capitalized) would follow franchise style:

- **Line 95:** "For a version of this same claim written without the **scripture frame** — for a skeptical neighbor"
- **Line 289:** "Light-as-membrane-dynamics presented for a general reader, without the **scripture frame**."

**Standard:** The REVIEWER_08 mandate requires capitalization of "Scripture" when it functions as a proper noun referring to biblical texts. "Scripture frame" (the frame through which this book presents physics grounded in biblical revelation) is a product-identity phrase and should be "Scripture frame" to match:
- Ch 1 usage: "Scripture-first method" (line 22 of Ch03_SPEC.md)
- Ch 2 established pattern: "Scripture first" / "Scripture-first" in the Family Edition voice
- Glossary precedent: Terms like "Logos," "Firmament," "Waters" are canonically capitalized; "Scripture" as a proper name follows the same rule

**Correction:** Change both instances to "Scripture frame" (capital S).

**Why it matters:** Consistency across the franchise signals editorial authority and respect for the text as a canonical source. Lowercase "scripture" reads as generic; capitalized "Scripture" signals the specific biblical text that anchors the entire Genesis Physics framework.

## ARCHITECTURAL NOTES

### Strength 1: The Back-Reference to Ch8 and Ch9

The chapter's deepest architectural move is linking *c*-constancy to the Lawgiver's constancy (Chapter 8) and then using Chapter 9's "*c* as propagation speed" to distinguish between "the speed through the fabric" (constant) and "the scale of the fabric" (variable). This cascade is clean:

Ch8 (Lawgiver does not change) → Ch9 (*c* is derived from membrane properties) → Ch10 (*c* is constant, membrane scale is not)

This prevents the obvious objection: "If Ch8 says the Lawgiver is constant, how can Ch10 say the firmament changed?" Answer: The firmament's character didn't change; its *scale* did. The constants didn't drift.

### Strength 2: The Honesty About What the Framework Claims

§2 explicitly disavows "light in transit" (Numbers 23:19 anchor: God does not fabricate false history). §7 explicitly disavows c-decay (Chapter 8 anchor: the Lawgiver does not change). The chapter then claims: the fabric stretched, the speed of light in the fabric was always *c*, and light rode the stretching.

This clarity prevents the most common misreading of the young-Earth position and protects against future accusations that the framework is hiding something.

### Strength 3: The Forward Pointer to the Sabbath-Boundary Mechanism in Ch11

The mechanism introduced in §6 (creation-mode vs. sustaining-mode physics; the boundary at Genesis 2:2–3) will be reapplied in Ch11 to radiometric dating. By establishing the Sabbath Boundary principle here with a clear physics example (rapid expansion followed by slow expansion), the family is already trained to understand how it will apply to radiometric dating in the next chapter. The cascade is efficient.

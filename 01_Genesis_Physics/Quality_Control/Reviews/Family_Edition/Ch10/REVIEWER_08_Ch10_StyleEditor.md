### 1. VOICE REGISTER: PASS WITH NOTES

The chapter opens strong with earned scene and engineer's posture, but shows two minor voice-register slips that should be tightened.

**Strengths:**
- Opens with earned porch scene (line 10–14): wife's cousin, cows, Milky Way. Scene-first, no sermon. Excellent.
- Engineer's voice consistent: "engineering-honest answer" (line 22), "honest physics gives an honest answer" (line 26), "engineering problem" (line 36), "boundary condition...ongoing equation" (line 220).
- Farm detail earned throughout: dogs, cows, chickens, porch chairs. No invented kids. ✓
- First-person sparing and natural: "I am telling him" (line 14), "I will tell you" (line 22), "I cannot use it" (line 55).
- Short sentences. Confidence in claims proportionate to rigor.
- Scripture density appropriate for Book 2 family edition.

**Notes requiring attention:**

1. **Line 20 — Voice slip toward preaching mode**
   - Current: "Every honest reader of Genesis who has ever taken an astronomy class has sat with this one. Every parent whose child came home with a textbook that said 13.8 billion years has felt its weight. Every believer who loves both the Bible and the night sky has wondered whether the two were going to shake hands or whether someone, somewhere, was going to have to fudge."
   - Issue: Triple repetition of "Every [person type]" creates a cadence that feels sermonic rather than conversational. The rhythm breaks the engineer's spare tone. "Wondered whether the two were going to shake hands" — mixed metaphor (hands shaking is people; relationship between institutions is the actual subject).
   - Proposed fix:
   ```
   Every honest reader of Genesis who has taken an astronomy class has sat with this one.
   Every parent whose child came home with a textbook saying 13.8 billion years has felt its weight.
   Every believer who loves both the Bible and the night sky has wondered: can both be true, or does someone have to fudge?
   ```
   - Rationale: Removes the choral repetition, sharpens the question, keeps the voice conversational and direct.

2. **Line 44 — "Being shredded" metaphor**
   - Current: "We see stars being shredded by its central black hole."
   - Issue: "Shredded" is vivid but slightly lurid for the engineer's register. It reads closer to science-fiction tone than to honest technical description.
   - Proposed fix: "We see stars torn apart by its central black hole." OR "We see stars stripped away by its central black hole."
   - Rationale: "Torn apart" and "stripped away" are more clinical; they match the engineer's tone of rigorous observation.

3. **Line 53 — "Rings of fires that never burned" — voice strength**
   - Current: "A tree with rings is not a lie; a tree with rings of fires that never burned is."
   - Issue: Not a violation, but the phrase "rings of fires" is poetic rather than direct. For the engineer's voice, simpler works better.
   - Proposed fix: "A tree with rings is honest. A tree with rings made by fires that never burned is not."
   - Rationale: Shorter, more direct, more engineer-like. The parallel structure "A tree... A tree..." is clearer than the contrast "not a lie; a tree... is."

**Overall:** Voice register holds throughout, with minor shifts toward sermon and poetry in three locations. The engineer-operator tone is consistent enough to PASS, but these three refinements would sharpen it further.

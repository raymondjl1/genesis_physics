### P2: Important (Medium confidence; should be addressed)

#### C2.1: Prerequisite transparency for Book 2 independence

**Finding:** The chapter assumes readers have completed Ch01, Ch02, and Ch03 in sequence. The opening (§1) acknowledges this but does not explicitly state it as a prerequisite. A reader who picks up the book at Ch04 might feel lost on Day 2 (expanse) or Day 3 (gathering).

**Severity:** Medium. The target reader (families buying a book for homeschool) will likely read sequentially. But if a digital version allows chapter-level sharing or if a reader returns after a break, the chapter could be clearer.

**Suggestion:** Add one sentence to §1 after the wife's line ("That isn't seven different miracles..."):

*"If you are coming to this chapter fresh, a quick note: Chapter 1 taught us how to read Genesis like an engineer. Chapter 2 gave us the three-layer architecture. Chapter 3 answered the light question. Now we lay the whole seven-day sequence out."*

**Current text** acknowledges this indirectly but could be more explicit for readers who may have skipped earlier chapters or returned after time away.

**Impact:** Low-to-medium. Does not block publication; improves reader resilience.

---

**Finding:** §11 says: "The starlight-and-age question — which is where most of the heat actually lives — gets its own chapter (Ch 10)."

Ch 10 spec exists (Ch10_SPEC.md: *The Starlight Question*). But Ch 10 is not yet written. If this review happens before Ch 10 is drafted, there is a small risk that the reference might break if Ch 10's scope changes.

**Severity:** Low. The spec is locked and titled *The Starlight Question,* which directly matches the reference. The chapter is planned. But a forward-reference to unwritten content is technically a light risk.

**Suggestion:** Confirm at publication that Ch 10 exists and is titled *The Starlight Question* or that the reference in §11 is updated. The spec is current as of April 23, 2026, so this is a safe reference as long as Ch 10 stays on track.

**Impact:** None for this chapter's internal integrity; minor for series coherence if Ch 10 scope drifts.

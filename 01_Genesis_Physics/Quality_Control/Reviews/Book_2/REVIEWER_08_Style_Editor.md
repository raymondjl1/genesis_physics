## Executive Summary

Book 2 holds the Family-Edition voice cleanly. Warmth is consistent, scripture-first ordering is uniform, second-person address is natural, and the equation count in prose is — for a 100,000-word manuscript — zero. The "Sarah-can-teach-this" tone is on every page.

Two C3 findings dominate the mechanical pass:

1. **Firmament terminology rule failure.** Bare *"the membrane"* (no "Firmament" qualifier) appears as descriptive prose in **11 of 15 chapters**, ~37 instances. Highest concentrations: Ch_09 (8), Ch_04 (6), Ch_06 (5), Ch_10 (5), Ch_13 (4). The reader is not confused, but the Series Bible is explicit and downstream products will trip on every instance.
2. **Hebrew transliteration first-mention format.** The manuscript uses bare *raqia* throughout (no Hebrew letters רָקִיעַ, no final-aleph apostrophe *raqia'*). This appears to be a deliberate Family-Edition softening; needs an explicit Series-Bible exception or compliance fix.

Everything else (voice register, equations rule, Five Principles, Zone naming, citation format, file naming, headings, numbers, Waters pairing) is **PASS**.

**Overall: PASS WITH NOTES (C3-class).** No C1 findings. No equations in prose. No "Hierarchy" misuse. No Zone-3-meaning-Earth-Prime errors.

## Finding 1 — "The Firmament" terminology rule [C3, manuscript-wide]

**Rule (Style Editor Mandate #4):** Primary term is **"The Firmament."** Acceptable: *"The Firmament membrane"* in technical contexts. **NEVER:** *"dome," "vault," "sky," "brane," "the membrane" alone, "the expanse" alone.*

**Status:** **FAIL.** Bare *"the membrane"* appears as descriptive prose (not inside a Scripture quotation) in 11 of the 15 chapter manuscripts.

**Occurrence count by chapter manuscript** (`Ch_*/Ch[0-9]*.md`, prose only — counts exclude SPEC files, VERIFICATION_RECORD files, and per-chapter reviewer files; Scripture-block quotations are also excluded):

| Chapter | "the membrane" (bare) | Representative line |
|---|---|---|
| Ch_01 | 1 | — |
| Ch_02 | 2 | Line 49: "That is not a solid dome. That is a *membrane.*" / Line 67: "a stretched-out, tensioned expanse — a membrane —" |
| Ch_03 | 2 | — |
| Ch_04 | 6 | §4: "A tensioned membrane — not a solid dome but a drumhead pulled taut." |
| Ch_05 | 2 | — |
| Ch_06 | 5 | Line 227: "The Waters Above are holding the membrane stretched." |
| Ch_07 | 1 | — |
| Ch_09 | 8 | Line 151: "the membrane's *stiffness*"; Line 320: "Tension (of the membrane)"; Line 322: "Density (of the membrane)"; Lines 326, 330: "the membrane's tension and density" / "the membrane's properties" |
| Ch_10 | 5 | — |
| Ch_12 | 1 | — |
| Ch_13 | 4 | — |

**Total: ~37 bare occurrences across 11 chapters.**

**Why this is a C3 (not a C2):** the prose is *not* introducing a contradictory cosmology. In every case the author is using "membrane" as an architectural noun for the *raqia* and the reader is already inside the Firmament frame. The reader does not get confused. But the series style sheet is explicit about the rule, the Popular Science flagship and Foundations will enforce it, and three downstream reviewers (Consistency Auditor, Navigator, Style Editor of subsequent products) will trip on every instance unless it is resolved.

**Required correction (recommended global replacement):**

| Current | Replace with |
|---|---|
| "the membrane" (bare, descriptive) | "the Firmament" |
| "the membrane's tension/density/stiffness/properties" | "the Firmament's tension/density/stiffness/properties" |
| "tensioned membrane" (descriptive metaphor) | "tensioned Firmament" *or* "the Firmament — a tensioned membrane —" (qualify) |
| "drumhead pulled taut" / "a *membrane*" (italicized, used the first time as a *teaching* word for *raqia*) | **Keep as-is** when explicitly defining *raqia*; this is the legitimate pedagogical use the rule allows. The rule prohibits bare *"the membrane"* used as a standing referent after definition. |

**Cleanest fix for Ch_09 §10 ("Key Terms"):** rewrite the definitions to read **"Tension (of the Firmament)"**, **"Density (of the Firmament)"**, and **"Set at creation … the Firmament's tension and density"**. This single block alone removes 5 of the 8 Ch_09 occurrences.

**Acceptable retained uses:**
- Ch_02 §2 lines 49 and 67, *first introduction* of the membrane metaphor as the translation of *raqia* — pedagogically necessary, and the noun is explicitly anchored to *raqia* one sentence earlier. Keep, but follow each with the canonical name on the very next mention.
- Any in-Scripture quotation containing "membrane" (none found — Scripture uses *expanse* or *firmament*).

## Finding 2 — Hebrew transliteration first-mention format [C3, systemic]

**Rule (Style Editor Mandate #3):** First mention: Hebrew letters → transliteration with diacriticals → English gloss. Final aleph as apostrophe: ***raqia'*** (NOT *raqia*).

**Status:** **NOTES / soft FAIL.** The manuscript uses **bare *raqia*** (no apostrophe) consistently — 12 occurrences in Ch_02 alone, dozens more across Ch_03–Ch_15. The Hebrew letters רָקִיעַ do not appear in any chapter manuscript (`Ch[0-9]*.md`). They *do* appear in Ch_05_SPEC.md, indicating the author/writer has the form available but is choosing not to put it in front of the family reader.

This is plausibly a **deliberate Family-Edition softening** (Hebrew letters and apostrophes-as-glottal-stops are intimidating at the kitchen table; the audience is 9–16-year-olds and their parents). But the style sheet is explicit, and the same audience will encounter *raqia'* with the apostrophe in the Popular Science flagship and *raqia'* / רָקִיעַ in the Foundations. Inter-product consistency requires either:

**Option A (recommended) — Document the exception.** Add a Family-Edition exception clause to the Series Bible Hebrew-transliteration rule:
> *Family Edition: Hebrew letters omitted at first mention. Transliteration printed without final-aleph apostrophe (raqia, mayim, qavah) for readability. Pronunciation in parentheses on first mention. Apostrophe form preserved in glossary AppB if linguistic precision is asked for.*

**Option B — Bring the manuscript into compliance.** Add the Hebrew letters at first mention (Ch_02 §2, Ch_02 §3, etc.) and globally search-replace *raqia* → *raqia'*, *mayim* → *mayim*, etc. Estimated touchpoints: ~80 across the manuscript.

The author has clearly designed for Option A. Recommend documenting it.

**One other Hebrew-transliteration note (C4):**
- *raqa* (the root verb) is rendered without a glottal stop. Consistent. PASS once the exception above is documented.
- Greek terms (*logos,* *hydor,* *teleō,* *synistemi*) appear with the rough breathing mark dropped and macrons rendered (τελέω in Ch_10 line 220 includes the Greek letters at first mention with transliteration — this is actually the *strictest* form the style sheet asks for, and the chapter that does it best). Recommend matching Ch_10's Greek treatment as the franchise gold standard.

## Finding 3 — Equation handling [C1 check] — **PASS**

**Rule (Style Editor Mandate #9, RED-FLAG #1):** Zero equations in Book 2. An equation in this product is an **automatic FAIL**.

**Status:** **PASS.** Grep across all chapter manuscripts for mathematical operators (`= ∑ ∫ ∇ ∂ √ ≤ ≥ ±`) and Greek physics letters (Λ Ψ Φ Ω π) returned **zero hits in `Ch[0-9]*.md` prose**.

Specific high-risk loci verified clean:
- **Ch_09 (speed of light, gravity):** No `c² = σ/μ`, no `c^2`, no Greek letters, no `=`. The Foundations relation is described in prose only — "the firmament's propagation speed — its natural tone. Not a free parameter; a derived consequence of the membrane's tension and density" (Ch_09 line 326). This is exactly the Family-Edition handling the rule prescribes. Excellent.
- **Ch_06 (dark matter / dark energy):** Percentages spelled out (sixty-eight, twenty-seven, five) on first use, then numerals (68, 27, 5) when re-cited. No Ω, no Λ, no field equations. PASS.
- **Ch_10 (starlight & time):** No Hubble equation, no `a(t)`, no integrals. The "balloon" and "raisin bread" analogies carry the load entirely in prose. PASS.
- **Ch_07, Ch_11, Ch_12, Ch_13:** No equations, no operator characters. PASS.

The author has held the line absolutely on the franchise's hardest rule. This is the single biggest mechanical win in the manuscript.

## Finding 4 — Waters / dark-matter & dark-energy pairing [PASS]

**Rule (Style Editor Mandate #5):** In technical contexts, ALWAYS pair on first mention per section: *"Dark energy (Waters Above, ~68%)"* or *"Waters Above (dark energy, ~68%)."*

**Status:** **PASS.** Ch_06 is the canonical case and it executes the pairing four times in §1, §2, §4, §5 (e.g., Ch_06 line 29: *"the Waters Above (dark energy, ~68% of the cosmic energy budget) and the Waters Below (dark matter, ~27%)"*). Glossary entries in Ch_06 §10 and AppB carry both names. Ch_02 §3 line 103 also pairs cleanly. Capital W on primordial Waters is consistent throughout; lowercase *water* for H₂O on the rare occasion it appears (Ch_11 flood chapter, Ch_07 matter chapter) is correctly distinguished.

One C4 polish: Ch_06 line 47 figure caption labels the slice *"Dark Energy — Waters Above"* without the percentage. The pairing rule requires `~68%` on first mention per section; the section-1 text covers it, so the figure is supplementary, but for KDP / print copy-edit consistency consider adding `~68%` and `~27%` to the figure's secondary labels.

**7. Five Principles (Mandate #6) — PASS.** Zero "Hierarchy" hits in chapter prose. **8. Zone naming (Mandate #7) — PASS.** The single instance of nested notation is Ch_02 §3 line 103: *"In the technical Foundations encyclopedia these are labeled Z₂.₂.₃ (Waters Above), Z₂.₂.₂ (Firmament), and Z₂.₂.₁ (Waters Below) — the numbering is a bookkeeping convenience; the names are what you need."* This is the textbook-perfect handling of the rule. No "Zone 3 = Earth Prime" error anywhere. PASS.

**9. **10. File naming (Mandate #10) — PASS.** Chapter folders `Ch_01_…`–`Ch_15_…`; appendices `AppA_…`–`AppD_…`. Underscores only, no spaces, two-digit numbers. Internal draft files use `Ch{XX}.md` (e.g., `Ch09.md`) — consistent within Book 2, minor inter-product nit (Foundations uses `Ch01_DRAFT.md`). PASS for this product.

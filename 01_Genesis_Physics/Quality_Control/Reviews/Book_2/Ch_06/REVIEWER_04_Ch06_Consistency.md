**What Chapter 6 does** (Key Terms, lines 229-250):

**Where this matters:**  
The canonical zone architecture (Zone_Architecture.md, Table 1) establishes that all major theological/physical entities in the Genesis Physics framework have zone identifiers. Chapter 2 explicitly includes them. The spec requires Chapter 6 to do the same. The omission creates a subtle inconsistency — a reader cross-referencing from Ch06 Key Terms to the Foundations encyclopedia will not find the bridging notation.

**Recommendation:**  
Add zone labels to the Key Terms definitions for Waters Above, Waters Below, and Firmament:
- Waters Above: Z₂.₂.₃
- Firmament: Z₂.₂.₂
- Waters Below: Z₂.₂.₁

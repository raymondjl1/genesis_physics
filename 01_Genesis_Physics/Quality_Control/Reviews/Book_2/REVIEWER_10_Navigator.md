## 5. Findings (by concern tag)

### Finding 1 — **[C3] FAIL.** Six cross-references to Foundations chapters that do not exist or use the wrong title.

See audit table rows F2, F11, F20, F21, F23, F24, F25. These are the FAIL items.

The architectural risk is small (a parent who tries to look up "Foundations Vol 5 Ch 14 — Black Holes" and finds *Critical Density* will be momentarily confused, then will probably figure out that black-hole material is in Ch 5/Ch 6). The publication risk is larger — these are the kind of citation errors that a reviewing physicist or homeschool curriculum gatekeeper will catch on first pass and use to question the rigor of the rest.

**Recommended fix (where it belongs):** A single editorial pass on the "For Further Reading" section of each chapter, with the `Book_0_The_Foundations/Vol_*/Manuscript/` directory listing open in a second window. Replace each Foundations citation's chapter title with the canonical folder/section title. Where the framework's content actually lives in a *different* chapter than the one cited (F20 Flood/historical perturbations; F21 Black Holes; F24 wave-function reading), redirect the citation to the correct chapter. Time estimate: 60–90 minutes for one editor.

### Finding 2 — **[C3] NOTES.** Six "title drift" citations (audit table rows F11, F15, F16, F17, F19).

The chapter numbers are right, but Book 2 uses a shorter or older title. Examples: *"Electromagnetism"* vs *"Electromagnetism from Membrane Wave Propagation"*; *"Cosmological Model"* vs *"Zone Cosmological Model"*; *"The Four Laws of Thermodynamics (in preparation)"* vs *"The Four Laws Complete Derivation"* (and the "(in preparation)" qualifier is now stale).

These are not breakage — a reader will find the right chapter. But the inconsistency reads as carelessness in a book that otherwise reads as carefully built. Recommend the same editorial pass that fixes Finding 1 also tighten these.

### Finding 3 — **[C3] NOTES.** Ch11 (Flood) cites a Vol 6 chapter that does not exist.

Ch11 closes with a "For Further Reading" entry: ***Foundations of Genesis Physics, Volume 6, Chapter 9 — Historical Events as Phase Transitions.*** Vol 6 Ch 9 is actually *FTL Travel*. There is no "Historical Events as Phase Transitions" chapter anywhere in Foundations.

This is the single most consequential ❌ in the audit, because the *Flood-as-physics-event* claim is the most controversial physics claim in the entire family edition (the kind of claim a YEC-skeptical reviewer will look for the formal backing of). If the citation chain breaks here, this is the chapter where it hurts the most.

**Where the fix belongs:** Either (a) the citation should redirect to *Foundations Vol 3 Ch 8 — Phase Transitions in Zone Architecture* (which is the existing Foundations chapter most relevant to the Flood-as-phase-transition reading), or (b) this is a genuine **research gap** — there is no dedicated "Historical Perturbations" chapter in the Foundations yet — and the family edition should be marked **BLOCKED** on Ch11 until that chapter is drafted, per the `01_Genesis_Physics/CLAUDE.md` research-gap protocol. **My recommendation:** create a `Manuscript/Ch_11_The_Flood_as_a_Physics_Event/RESEARCH_GAP.md` flagging this and open a GH issue `Research Gap: Foundations chapter on historical perturbations (Flood, Fall, Cross, Consummation)`. Until that exists, Ch11's quantitative claims about flood timescales and isostatic compensation are *unanchored* in the cascade.

### Finding 4 — **[C3 / C1] NOTES.** Scripture→physics chain has one weak link: Book 1 silence on quantum mechanics and on the Flood.

Book 2 Ch11 (Flood) and Book 2 Ch13 (Quantum Weirdness) reach upward without an intermediate Book 1 chapter to land on. Book 1's manuscript inventory (Ch_01..Ch_15) contains no Flood chapter and no quantum chapter. This is consistent with Book 1's scope, but it means those two Book 2 chapters jump *directly* from family-register to Foundations-register without a popular-science-register chapter in between.

Architecturally, the cascade in those two chapters is:

> Scripture → Book 2 family register → (no Book 1 chapter) → Foundations Vol 3/4/5/6

The Navigator's rule is that *every* Book 2 physics claim should have a Book 1 popular-science version to hand the "skeptical brother-in-law." Two chapters violate that. **Where the fix belongs:** This is a Book 1 (not Book 2) gap. Don't fix it in Book 2. Open a Book 1 ticket: *"Book 1 missing chapters on quantum mechanics and on the Flood (or explicit BOOK_SPEC decision to scope them out)."* If Book 1 is scoped out of both topics deliberately, add a one-sentence note to Book 2 Ch11 and Ch13 saying "Book 1 does not cover this topic; the secular-register version lives directly in Foundations Vol X."

### Finding 5 — **[C2] NOTES.** Rigor labeling is good but uneven.

Ch06, Ch07, Ch10, Ch12, and Ch13 carry explicit HIGH/MODERATE/LOW rigor labels on their framework claims. Ch01–Ch05, Ch08, Ch09, Ch11, Ch14, Ch15 do not. The labeling is most needed where the claims are most contested (Flood, dark sector, quantum) — those are mostly covered. But Ch11 (Flood) does *not* carry MODERATE/OPEN labels on its quantitative claims in the same explicit way Ch06 and Ch13 do; it would benefit from them.

### Finding 6 — **[C3] PASS with one note.** Forward-dependency hygiene.

Chapters introduce concepts in the right order. *Bara* (Ch01) is built on before any later chapter uses it. *Raqia* / firmament (Ch02) precedes anything that depends on membrane mechanics (Ch05, Ch09, Ch10). Pattern operators (Ch04) precede their use in Ch07 (matter as standing waves) and Ch08 (laws as symmetries). No chapter assumes a concept the family edition hasn't yet defined.

One small note: Ch04 SPEC explicitly says "Ch 10 referenced as forthcoming." Confirmed — the forward reference is light and pedagogically benign ("we'll see this again when we discuss starlight"). Within the rules.

### Finding 7 — **[C4 / C3] PASS.** Depth calibration is correct.

Zero equations. No graduate-level jargon without translation. The few times the manuscript ventures near a technical term (Yukawa coupling in Ch07; Schwarzschild metric in Ch09; Kaluza-Klein in Ch12), it explicitly names the term, gives a one-sentence family-register translation, and points to the Foundations chapter where the math lives. That is the correct pattern. The depth calibration FAILs the persona would flag (equation in Book 2, ungrounded jargon, premature depth) do not appear in the manuscript.

### Finding 8 — **[C3] PASS.** Book 1 ↔ Book 2 voice separation.

Book 2 consistently identifies Book 1 as *"the version for the skeptical brother-in-law"* or *"the version without scripture framing."* Book 2 never tries to do Book 1's job. This is exactly the "one voice per product" rule (`01_Genesis_Physics/CLAUDE.md` §Key Principles), and the family edition holds the line on it.

## 6. Recommendation

**OVERALL: FAIL — fixable in one editorial pass plus one research-gap ticket.**

The architecture is sound. The cascade exists. The voice is right. Depth is right. Scripture-first ordering is right. The book *almost* PASSes Navigator review.

What knocks it out of PASS is **six broken Foundations cross-references** (Finding 1) and **one chapter (Ch11) that cites a Foundations chapter that does not exist** (Finding 3). Both are fixable. The first is an editorial pass. The second is either a citation redirect or a legitimate research-gap admission — *which is itself acceptable in this series, per the always-answer-why principle, as long as it is flagged honestly.*

### Recommended fix sequence (in order)

1. **[1 hour, editorial]** Pull canonical Foundations chapter titles from `Book_0_The_Foundations/Vol_*/Manuscript/Ch_*/` folder names and update the six ❌ citations in Ch01, Ch11, Ch12, Ch13, Ch14. Also tighten the six ⚠️ title-drift citations.
2. **[15 min, ticketing]** Open a GH issue `Research Gap: Foundations chapter on historical perturbations (Flood, Fall, Cross, Consummation)` — label `book:foundations,research-gap`. Until the chapter exists, redirect Ch11's citation to *Vol 3 Ch 8 — Phase Transitions in Zone Architecture* with an honest one-sentence note that the dedicated historical-perturbations treatment is in preparation.
3. **[15 min, scope decision]** Decide whether Book 1 should grow a quantum-mechanics chapter and/or a Flood chapter, or whether the cascade for those two Book 2 chapters is intentionally Book 2 → Foundations direct. Document the decision in `Book_1_Hidden_Architecture/BOOK_SPEC.md`.
4. **[30 min, polish]** Add HIGH/MODERATE/LOW rigor labels to Ch11's quantitative claims, matching the pattern Ch06 and Ch13 already follow.

After those four fixes, this chapter would PASS Navigator review with NOTES. The book is much closer to publishable than the FAIL verdict makes it sound — the FAIL is a label problem, not a structural one.

### What the architect sees

This is a book whose author knew where every claim should land. The cascade was built right. What slipped is bibliographic discipline on the final "For Further Reading" lists — the kind of thing that gets fixed cleanly once you know to look for it. The Family Edition is the launch book; it earns the launch-quality polish. Fix the labels and ship it.

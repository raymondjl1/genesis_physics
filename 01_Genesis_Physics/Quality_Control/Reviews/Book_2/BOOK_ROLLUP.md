## 3. P1 Critical (10–15, Deduped Across Reviewers)

**P1-1. Ch 6 title overclaim — "The Dark Matter and Dark Energy Mystery — Solved."** (R-06, R-01) The chapter itself admits in §6 that the full derivation lives in Foundations Vol 5 Ch 11 "and the Foundations Series (in preparation)." Retitle to "Named" or add an explicit §1 paragraph distinguishing *named* from *derived*. Most quotable headline for a hostile reviewer.

**P1-2. Ch 6 §6 "the specification produces the numbers" — overclaim.** (R-01) Architecture produces the *hierarchy* (68/27/5 shape), not the specific decimal ratio. Soften the sentence; defer exact-ratio derivation to Foundations.

**P1-3. Ch 7 §5 / §8 + AppB — electron-mass and Standard-Model precision claims.** (R-01, R-07) "Electron mass to better than one part in a thousand from first principles," proton 0.01%, muon/tau ~1%, quarks 1–2%. Most load-bearing numerical claims in the book. Required: explicit cross-walk against Foundations Vol 4 Ch 10 final tables; fix Family Edition numbers to match Foundations. Also: AppB "§8" reference dangles without chapter qualifier.

**P1-4. Ch 9 §4 "A ripple on the firmament is what we call light" — orphan statement.** (R-02) The only true orphan in the book; the chapter's derivation of *c* rests on this identification, which is asserted without mechanism. Defer with explicit flag and add to §8 confidence list as WORKING, not RIGOROUS.

**P1-5. "Waters / mayim" equivocation — most weaponizable seam.** (R-06, R-02) The book uses *mayim* to mean (a) Hebrew liquid water when it wants biblical warrant and (b) dark sector energy field when it wants modern-physics confirmation. Hostile reader will call this bait-and-switch. Fix: one paragraph in Ch 2 or Ch 6 explicitly owning the move — *"this is an interpretive claim, not a translation."*

**P1-6. Ch 8 violates canonical Five Principles naming.** (R-04) Ch 8 invokes Sustaining → Symmetry → Conservation chain without naming the Five Principles as the canonical structure required by `Reference/Five_Principles.md`. Apply the Ch 8 reviewer's recommended patch (insert a paragraph in §3 after the Noether statement explicitly naming Principles 1/2/3).

**P1-7. Convenient-God / falsifiability gap.** (R-06) Ch 10 (rapid stretching ended) and Ch 11 (subterranean reservoirs opened and closed) both invoke one-time commanded boundary resets. Book never states a *would-falsify* condition for the framework. Add at least one explicit falsifiability sentence somewhere in Ch 6, Ch 9, or Ch 11.

**P1-8. Style: bare "the membrane" used 37 times across 11 chapters.** (R-08) Violates Style Editor Mandate #4 (canonical term is "the Firmament"). Highest concentrations: Ch 9 (8), Ch 4 (6), Ch 6 (5), Ch 10 (5), Ch 13 (4). Global replacement except where pedagogically defining *raqia*.

**P1-9. Ch 11 Flood — radiometric dating / geologic column not engaged.** (R-07, R-05) The one place an older homeschooler will get embarrassed in a community-college geology elective. Verify Ch 10 or Ch 11 contains a "But What About?" item on radiometric dating and the Cambrian–Permian sequence; add if missing.

**P1-10. Ch 6 dark-matter / standing-wave logical loop.** (R-07) If ordinary matter is *made of* standing waves in the dark-matter field, how is dark matter the thing that *doesn't interact with light*? Verify Ch 6 explicitly closes this loop; reader will spot the apparent contradiction otherwise.

**P1-11. Duplicate Family Activity / Discussion blocks.** (R-03) Chs 6, 7, 9 carry the Family Activity twice (embedded numbered section + closing block). Label one as "Printable Version" or remove the duplicate.

**P1-12. Section-numbering inconsistency at Ch 10.** (R-03) Chs 1–9 use `## §1.` form; Chs 10–15 use `## 1.` form. Normalize to `§` throughout.

**P1-13. "How to Use This Book by Age" front-matter page missing.** (R-05) Ch 14's three-aircraft framing (Cessna 152 / 182 / King Air) needs to be forward-referenced in the front matter so parents know the book is designed to be re-read across years.

**P1-14. ACX audiobook narration-provenance decision.** (R-12) Existing 343 MB `.m4b` appears TTS-generated. ACX policy as of 2026-05 restricts AI-narrated audiobooks. Confirm provenance and ACX policy; may be a category fail, not a spec fail.

**P1-15. Ch 13 §5+ difficulty ramp.** (R-07, R-05) Quantum-mechanics section is the steepest slope in the book; assumes more than the rest of the book has built. Add one scaffolding analogy between §4 and §5, or insert a "skim to §7" reader-care note.

## 5. Findings by Concern

### C1 — Biblical-first traceability

Strongest dimension of the book. Scripture leads in every sampled chapter; Hebrew/Greek lexical work is sound (verified against BDB/HALOT/BDAG by R-09); *bereshit / bara / tohu va-bohu / raqia / mayim / qavah / baqa / ma'yan / shanan / chanak / pheron / synistemi / kainos* all properly anchored. Ch 4 is the model chapter — biblical anchors are load-bearing AND warm across all seven days (R-11 §7 warmth table). Outstanding C1 items: tighten *mayim* lexical-range argument (P2-e); soften *bara*-ex-nihilo and *tohu va-bohu* "maximally disordered" readings (P2-c); rephrase Ch 1 §3 *tohu va-bohu*/entropy parallel (R-07 Pushback 2); add direct John 1:14 quotation in Ch 15 §3 (R-09). No P0 orphans found; one P1 orphan (Ch 9 §4 ripples=light).

### C2 — Cross-book continuity

Weakest dimension. **FAIL** from R-10 driven by 6 broken Foundations chapter-title pointers concentrated in Ch 1, Ch 11, Ch 12, Ch 13, Ch 14 (see §7 below). Compounded by post-April-2026 numbering ambiguity: in-prose "Book 1" / "Book 2" labels still reference old logical numbering (R-01 C3-3, R-04 C1-X1, R-07 §2.3). Translation inconsistency (ESV vs. KJV mid-book) is the structural C2 theological defect (R-09 §5.1). Two chapters (Ch 11 Flood, Ch 13 quantum) jump directly from Family Edition to Foundations without a Book 1 intermediate — likely a Book 1 scope gap, not a Book 2 defect (R-10 Finding 4).

### C3 — No unanswered "but why"

Strongest in the genre. R-02 found one true orphan (Ch 9 §4 ripples=light, P1-4) and a handful of polish items. The HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN confidence-labeling system is the single most-praised feature of the book across reviewers and is what earns reader trust. Notable why-gaps: Sabbath-Boundary mechanism not walked (P2-f); coupling-reduction → Flood-timing bridge missing (P2-g); "field" not defined on first use (P2-d); closure criterion for "seven patterns" not stated (P3); "why have smart Christians disagreed for 2,000 years" not addressed (P3 from R-05); falsifiability sentence missing book-wide (P1-7).

### C4 — Derivation honesty / self-consistency

Generally strong. Zero equations confirmed (R-08). HIGH/MODERATE/LOW posture is "more disciplined than 90% of popular-science cosmology" (R-01). Ch 10's explicit rejection of "light-in-transit" and c-decay is a credibility-earner (R-01, R-06). Outstanding C4 items: overclaim in Ch 6 title and §6 ratio claim (P1-1, P1-2); electron-mass precision claims need Vol 4 Ch 10 cross-walk (P1-3); Ch 8 Five Principles naming missing (P1-6); Bullet Cluster "photograph" softening (P2-o); convenient-God / falsifiability gap (P1-7); the "*sharats* + *nephesh chayah* doing two jobs" clarification in Ch 4 §7 (R-11 §4.2); the Day 7 *shabat* = conservation-laws identification needs one acknowledging sentence as framework-interpretive (R-11 §4.1).

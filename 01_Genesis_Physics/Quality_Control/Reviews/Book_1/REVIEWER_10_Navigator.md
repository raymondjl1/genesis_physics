Ch 3 sits at the architectural keystone of Book 1. Its job is to (a) name every zone the rest of Book 1 will reference, (b) lock the visual vocabulary against `Zone_Architecture.md`, (c) forward-point each architectural element to the Foundations derivation that earns it, and (d) preview — without arguing — the Book 1 chapters that cash the predictions. - **Book 0 (Foundations) Vol 1 Ch 3, 4, 5** — Ch 3 is the popular-audience face of those three Foundations chapters. The naming, glossing, and zone numbering must match the Foundations register exactly.
- **Book 1 Ch 4–13** — every subsequent Book 1 chapter assumes the reader holds the Ch 3 picture. If Ch 3 names a zone wrong, every later chapter inherits the drift.
- **Book 2 (Family Edition)** — the Family Edition launches first commercially but cascades content second; it can only use what Book 1 has confirmed. Book 2 may compress to a simplified zone labeling, but Ch 3 is the canonical source of zone identities.

## 5. Notes (None Blocking — Apply at First-Pass Copyedit)

**Note 1 — Hebrew callback frequency (lines 88, 138).** Spec Ch03-013 caps Hebrew/Genesis vocabulary at "once in the opening and once in the closing." Draft delivers four callbacks: opening (line 16, *raqia*/*mayim*), mid-chapter *raqia* defense (line 88), mid-chapter *qavah* (line 138), and closing zone-to-Hebrew map (line 158). Both mid-chapter instances are load-bearing for the argument and not preachy — the *raqia* paragraph at line 88 is structurally necessary, and the *qavah* mention at line 138 grounds Z₂.₂.₂ in the Ch 2 vocabulary. **Recommendation:** keep both. But amend Ch03_SPEC.md Ch03-013 verification text to say "two emphatic callbacks (opening, close) plus mid-chapter structural recalls where a Hebrew word *is* the zone's etymology" so the consistency record stays clean. *Tag: C1 (scripture register), C3 (spec-to-draft trace).*

**Note 2 — Seven-day lifecycle has no Foundations citation in this chapter.** Line 162 promises "Chapters 7 and 8 handle…the seven-stage sequence of Genesis 1, which turns out to be the framework's build lifecycle." Book 1 Ch 8 exists; the Foundations chapter that *derives* the lifecycle formally is not explicitly identified anywhere in Ch 3 or the spec. If Foundations Vol 1 Ch 9 (Pattern Operators) covers the seven types, a parallel chapter for the seven-stage sequence (Vol 1 Ch 10? Vol 2?) should be confirmed before Book 1 Ch 8 finalizes. **Recommendation:** Navigator-level cascade ticket — confirm or create the Foundations destination for the lifecycle before Book 1 Ch 8 locks. No change to Ch 3 prose. *Tag: C3.*

**Note 3 — "Firmament Domain" vs "firmament membrane" (line 76 vs Reference Table 1).** Reference labels Z₂.₂ as "Firmament Domain"; Ch 3 names it "the firmament membrane." These are not contradictory (the membrane *is* the domain, mechanically), but a strict consistency auditor could flag drift. **Recommendation:** add a one-line clarifier to `Zone_Architecture.md` §9 noting that Z₂.₂ is referred to as "the firmament" or "the firmament membrane" in trade-book registers and "Firmament Domain" in Foundations register. No change to Ch 3 prose. *Tag: C3.*

**Note 4 — Vol 1 Ch 4 cited twice (lines 104, 116).** Spec Ch03-014 says "three Foundations citations maximum" plus one forward pointer to Vol 1 Ch 8. The body has citations to Vol 1 Ch 3, Ch 4 (×2), and Ch 5 — four total in the citation count, three unique chapters. This complies with the spirit of the cap but a strict count would flag it. **Recommendation:** soften the second Vol 1 Ch 4 hit on line 116 to "(same Foundations chapter)" or cut it; the first cite at line 104 already does the work. *Tag: C4 (publisher readiness — citation discipline).*

**Note 5 — Vol 1 Ch 8 forward pointer is missing from the body.** Spec Ch03-014 permits "A single pointer to Vol 1 Ch 8 (five principles)…in the close." Spec Section Outline §7 also explicitly plans this pointer ("one forward pointer: this architecture, as architecture, is all the reader needs to carry forward"). The body's §7/§8 (lines 152–180) does the architectural close beautifully but does **not** name Vol 1 Ch 8 by number. **Recommendation:** one sentence in §7 of the body — "(The five governing principles that tie the architecture together are the subject of Foundations Volume 1 Chapter 8.)" — would close the planned cascade pointer. Optional but recommended for the Foundations cross-link. *Tag: C3.*

**Note 6 — Ch 2 callback to "five Hebrew words" (line 138) inventories four in spec.** Spec Prerequisites table lists four Hebrew words (bara, asah, raqia, mayim). Ch 3 line 138 refers to "the fifth Hebrew word I named, briefly, in Chapter 2" — *qavah*. **Recommendation:** verify Ch 2 actually introduces *qavah* (Genesis 1:9 gathering verb). If Ch 2 inventories five words and the spec table is short, update the spec table. If Ch 2 inventories only four, either soften line 138 or add *qavah* to Ch 2's Hebrew-vocabulary section. *Tag: C3 (Ch 2 ↔ Ch 3 trace).*

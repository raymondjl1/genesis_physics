## Verdict

**PASS WITH NOTES — with one P0-CONDITIONAL on Ch 12.**

No P0 orphans at the claim-graph level Vol-5-internally. Vol 5 is, like Vol 4, a *subsequent-claim* volume: it inherits biblical anchors from Vol 1 (zone manifold, Firmament, Waters Above/Below, σ/μ/c) and Vol 1 Ch 11 (Sabbath Boundary). Every chapter cites its Vol 1 parent by section and equation.

The structural problem is Ch 12. Ch 12 is the first Foundations chapter to use Genesis 1 not as background architecture but as a *load-bearing source of physical content* — Gen 2:1–3 as the Sabbath Boundary phase transition, Gen 1:14–19 as the Day-4 boundary on star creation, Hebrew *rāqaʿ* across 17 OT stretching passages as motivation for the two-phase expansion. From a C4 perspective this is the strongest chapter in Vol 5 — but it also places the heaviest audit-trail demand on its Vol 1 parent. **If Vol 1 Ch 11 does not derive the Sabbath Boundary (κ_create, κ_full) as a real thermodynamic phase transition from Gen 2:1–3 architecture, but instead postulates it and attaches the verse afterwards, Ch 12 is a textbook retrofit and verdict flips to FAIL for that chapter.** Vol 1 R-11 must verify end-to-end.

The other systemic issue is **epigraph distribution**: 14 of 15 chapters have non-biblical epigraphs (two of them apocryphal quotes, four self-quotes), and six chapters (Ch 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 10) contain no Genesis 1 references of any kind. The fix is editorial only — first-use footnote per chapter + epigraph reshuffle — but the volume currently reads, in its first ten chapters, as Peskin-and-Schroeder-with-"Firmament"-substituted, and a hostile reviewer will say so.

Vol 5 is also the first Foundations volume to close the audit loop with an explicit Gen 1 capstone (Ch 15 §551). That is real progress over Vol 4's missing P1-G.

**P1-C (C4). Ch 12 §5.12.1 rhetoric.** "The Hebrew grammar… *demands* a two-phase expansion structure" reads as derivational. Important: as currently worded, Ch 12 risks reading "the Bible tells us X, therefore physics must do X" — the exact failure mode REVIEWER-11 exists to catch.

**P1-D (C4). Ch 12 §3 light-through-Waters-Above.** The mechanism has no direct Gen 1 anchor; it inherits Vol 1 Ch 6 + a boundary-condition change at the Sabbath Boundary attributed to Vol 1 Ch 11. The chapter hedges appropriately ("boundary conditions *may* have been different"). But it should explicitly classify §3 as "speculative, conditional on Vol 1 Ch 11 BC derivation" at the section opening, not bury that in a gap list at the end.

**P1-E (C4). Ch 9 §9.12 and Ch 12 §2.3 share a parent (Sabbath Boundary as Vol 1 Ch 11 phase transition) and a claim ("Hubble tension is the SB signature") but do not cross-cite.** A reader following the audit chain should see these as two faces of the same Vol 1 Ch 11 result. Editorial only.

**P1-F (C4, C3). Ch 1 and Ch 3 each use "Firmament" 100+ times with no biblical anchor.** A reader opening Vol 5 at Ch 1 (Einstein Field Equations Recovered) or Ch 3 (Gravitational Waves) — and many will, because these are the most-citable chapters — sees Randall–Sundrum with "Firmament" substituted for "brane." Same Vol 4 P2-D failure amplified, because Vol 5 is the volume trade press will quote. Fix: P1-A footnote, every chapter.

**P1-G (C4). Voice imbalance.** Vol 5 modulates between "Peskin & Schroeder with Firmament substituted" (Ch 1–10) and "young-Earth physics" (Ch 12). Neither is wrong; the volume nowhere signals the transition. Add a one-paragraph Part-IV preface before Ch 12 (explaining the voice shift to framework-distinctive content) and an opening note in Ch 1/Ch 8 (explaining that biblical anchoring is fully inherited from Vol 1 and direct Gen-1 content appears in Ch 12 and Ch 15).

## 2. Top 5 P0 Blockers (Deduped, with Attributions)

### P0-1 — Canonical warp profile B_η(η) is not pinned down
Three (arguably four) different functional forms for the same field appear in the same volume: Ch 2 uses exponential B_η = B_0 − γη/2; Ch 4 uses Gaussian B(η) = −γ²η²/2; Ch 11 uses η²/(2η_B²); Ch 2's own "RESOLVED" box names a fourth (RS-type, B_η ≈ const). Every V_η-dependent number downstream (G_4, α_s, hierarchy ratio, dark-matter density, σ_QCD) inherits this ambiguity.
*Raised by:* R01 (Physicist), R02 (But Why?), R03 (Writing Coach), R04 (Consistency Auditor), R06 (Skeptic), R07 (Student), R11 (Biblical Traceability).
**No reviewer disagreement.**

### P0-2 — Headline numerical results are consistency checks dressed as predictions
G_4: Ch 2 Route 2 fits L_eff to observed G_N (admitted in §2.4.2 footnote) but Ch 11's prediction table lists G_4 with ~0.1% "agreement." α⁻¹: K = 1.4383 (Ch 3/9/11) / K ≈ 1.44 (Ch 1/6/10) is derived in Vol 4 but used as if known here. α_s and σ_QCD use unspecified geometric constants (C, field-strength norm) to land on experimental values. Hierarchy 10³⁶ depends on λ = 41, whose origin is unstated. G_6 is back-calculated from G_4. Ch 9 §9.3.4 demonstrates the right framing; the rest of the volume does not match it.
*Raised by:* R01, R02, R04, R06, R07, R11. R09 (Theologian) and R10 (Navigator) note the Ch 9 §9.3.4 / Ch 6 §586 honesty boxes as exemplary models to propagate.
**No disagreement.** R09's view that the volume is "rigorously disciplined" is about *theological* claims; on physics-derivation honesty the verdict is unanimous.

### P0-3 — Five Principles ordering & numbering violate canon
Canonical order (Vol 1 Ch 8, `Reference/Five_Principles.md`): Sustaining → Conservation → Symmetry → Degradation → Duality. Ch 5 §5.4 numbers them Symmetry/Conservation/Duality/Degradation/Sustaining (renames *and* reorders four of five). Ch 1 §1.5 presents them out of canonical order. Style Editor flags the Ch 1 deviation; Consistency Auditor flags both; Theologian confirms and elevates to volume-level.
*Raised by:* R04, R08, R09, R11. R01/R02 acknowledge as P1.
**No disagreement** on canon; mild disagreement on severity (R09 will accept a one-sentence rationale; R04 requires reordering).

### P0-4 — Editorial scar tissue and shipping-blocking packaging
Multiple chapters carry inline `[Provisional]` banners, `⚠ Mathematical Correction (Rev. 2026-05-14)` blocks, `OP-X RESOLVED (date)` notices, "Rigor Level" tags, and RT-2.SU3 ticket-status announcements *inside running prose*. Separately, the manuscript-as-object is incomplete: no front matter, no index, no copyright/BISAC, duplicate Bibliography and Problem_Sets files in root vs Back_Matter, 80 figure placeholders with zero rendered art, no permissions log, no alt-text.
*Raised by:* R03 (scar tissue, P0-3), R06 (RT-2.SU3 "marketing" line, P2), R07 (resolution notes don't belong in textbook prose), R08 (heading drift, citation format FAIL), R12 (front matter, figures, duplicate back matter, index — all C1 production blockers). Combines into a single editorial/production sweep.
**No disagreement.**

### P0-5 — Genesis 1 is invisible in the prose
Across 8,471 lines of manuscript, zero direct Scripture citations in body text. "Firmament," "Waters Above/Below" are used as nomenclature only; no chapter re-grounds them in Gen 1:6–8. No Hebrew (*raqia*, *mayim*) re-introduced. Compared to the franchise mission ("first page of the Bible is the first page of physics"), the volume reads as generic Kaluza-Klein with biblical labels pasted on. R03 calls this borderline-FAIL on the voice axis; R11 calls it "anchor visibility, not anchor integrity" (conditional PASS if Vol 1 anchors hold).
*Raised by:* R03, R04 (P1), R05, R09 (notes C-1), R11.
**Reviewer disagreement:** R09 (Theologian) considers the editorial silence *defensible* for a graduate physics volume; R03/R05/R11 want minimal anchoring (one epigraph, one Ch 1 sidebar, footnoted first-uses). Aggregate position: add the minimal anchoring; do not preach. Skeptic (R06) explicitly notes the theological discipline as a *strength* — do not lose it while addressing P0-5.

## 5. Findings by Concern

The single weakest dimension of the volume. Zero Scripture in body prose; zero Hebrew; "Firmament" / "Waters" used as inherited jargon. R11 confirms the *derivation chain* to Vol 1 is intact and clean (no retrofit traces, no decorative usage at the load-bearing level — Ch 6 §6.5.2 explicitly cites Gen 1:6–8). ### C2 — Cross-book continuity
R10 (Navigator) returns the cleanest pass in the series: 32 audited external references, 0 BROKEN, 1 properly-labelled FORWARD-ONLY, 2 STALE-verify items (dated note, specific Eq numbers in Vol 1). The Vol 1 → Vol 2 → Vol 3/4/5 cascade is engineered, not improvised. Ch 11's closing handoff table and Ch 5's "build order verified" footer are recommended as Foundations templates. R04 confirms all spot-checked Vol 1 equation handles resolve. C2 issues are localized: (i) σ unit lag from Vol 1 Ch 5; (ii) Five Principles ordering inherited break; (iii) "Open System Axiom" alias for canonical "God as Active Sustaining Ground"; (iv) intra-volume warp-profile contradiction (P0-1); (v) α⁻¹ numerical drift (P1-1); (vi) the equation-tag scheme (V.C.N) needs explicit declaration in front matter.

### C3 — No unanswered "but why?"
R02 returns the volume's strongest finding: this is the best why-answering volume so far. Ch 1 is a why-before-what master class; Ch 5 §5.1.2–5.1.6 ("why this form?") is exemplary; Ch 6 §6.2.1 "Why U(1)?" answers charge quantization for what may be the first time in any textbook; Ch 9's power-law-vs-log intuition is pedagogically successful. Gaps: (a) coefficient K and λ = 41 deferred to Vol 4 — chain exits mid-link (P1-8); (b) Ch 9 §9.0 vs §9.3.4 framing inconsistency (P1-9); (c) Ch 4 §4.2 punts to research file (P1-6); (d) Ch 6 §6.3.3 SU(2) origin (P1-5); (e) several "why this form" arguments cite theorems (Lovelock, Ostrogradsky) rather than re-deriving — acceptable for Foundations but flagged. R10 confirms no orphan claims.

### C4 — Self-consistency / derivation honesty / craft / publisher readiness
This is where the volume fails. (i) Warp profile (P0-1). (ii) Predictions-vs-consistency overclaim (P0-2). (iii) Five Principles canon (P0-3). (iv) Editorial scar tissue + packaging (P0-4). (v) Voice: 8,471 lines without one author scene; seven chapters open with "In Chapter N…" recap (R03 P0-2, P0-4). (vi) Problem sets / worked examples / figures (R07). (vii) Citation format / "brane" / heading drift (R08). (viii) No front matter, no index, duplicate back matter, no permissions log (R12). The derivation-honesty failures are unanimous across R01/R02/R04/R06/R07/R11. R09 confirms there is no theological dishonesty; the honesty problem is in the *physics* claims, not the theology.

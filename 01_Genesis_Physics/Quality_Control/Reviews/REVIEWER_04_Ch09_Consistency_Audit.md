## MINOR ISSUES IDENTIFIED

### Issue 1: Epistemic Status Language (§9.3.4)

**Location:** §9.3.4, "What Is Actually Derived vs. What Is Verified"

**Finding:** The section is clear and honest, but one phrase could be slightly ambiguous:

> "The numerical value of $G_4 = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}$ enters through $\alpha_G$ (Eq. 2.9.22). Chapter 2 derived $G_4 = G_6/V_\text{extra}$ and showed that the formula is consistent with the measured value..."

**Concern:** The phrase "consistent with the measured value" might suggest to a hasty reader that the formula was derived independently and then tested against measurement, when in fact the 6D coupling $G_6$ is fixed by the requirement that the formula reproduce the observed $G_4$.

**Recommendation:** Clarify as: "...and showed that the formula is consistent with the measured value, by fixing the 6D coupling constant $G_6$ to match the observed Newton's constant."

**Severity:** MINOR — Does not affect validity of the chapter, only clarity.

---

### Issue 2: Terminology Precision in §9.2.2

**Location:** §9.2.2, discussion of scale ratio ℛ

**Finding:** The statement "The logarithm compresses $10^{41}$ down to about 95" is pedagogically clear but uses imprecise language.

The scale ratio is actually $\mathcal{R} = 2.27 \times 10^{41}$ (a specific number, not just $10^{41}$). The chapter correctly defines $\mathcal{R}$ in Eq. (2.9.11), but the informal language in the comparison might mislead readers about the precision.

**Current text:**
> "EM's dependence: $\alpha^{-1} \propto \ln(\mathcal{R}) = \ln(2.27 \times 10^{41}) = 95.23$."

**Assessment:** ACTUALLY CORRECT — The chapter immediately gives the precise value and shows the calculation. The informal language that follows is for pedagogical clarity. No change needed.

**Severity:** NONE — NOT AN ISSUE. Flag withdrawn.

## 1. ZONE NUMBERING & NAMING INCONSISTENCIES

### Inconsistency 1.1: Zone Naming Convention Variability
**Severity:** MODERATE

**Issue:**
Zones are referred to by different naming systems across chapters:
- **Ch03:** "Zone 1: Heaven Prime" | "Zone 2: Earth Prime" | "Zone 2.1: Atemporal Domain" | "Zone 2.2: Temporal Domain"
- **BLACK_HOLES_content:** "Zone 2.2.1 (Waters Below)" | "Zone 2.2.2 (Temporal Firmament)" | "Zone 2.2.1 (Atemporal Waters Below)"
- **COSMOLOGY_content:** "Zone 2.2.3" explicitly named "Waters Above" | "Zone 2.2.1" for Waters Below
- **STYLE_GUIDE.md:** Lists "Zone 3 / Earth Prime" (contradicting Ch03 which uses Zone 2 for Earth Prime)

**Evidence:**
- Ch03_text.txt lines 52-67 establish numbering: Zone 0, Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 2.1, Zone 2.2, with subdivisions
- BLACK_HOLES_content.txt line 21 refers to "Zone 2.2.1 (Waters Below)" and line 22 "Zone 2.2.2 (Temporal Firmament)"
- STYLE_GUIDE.md line 18 lists "Zone 3 / Earth Prime" alongside "Zone 2 / Heaven Prime"
- Ch03_text.txt clearly indicates Zone 2 = "Earth Prime (Temporal Container)" NOT Zone 3

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
Follow Ch03_text.txt explicit numbering:
- Zone 0: The Eternal Origin (God)
- Zone 1: Heaven Prime (Eternal Domain)
- Zone 2: Earth Prime (Temporal Container) — the complete temporal domain
- Zone 2.1: Atemporal Domain
- Zone 2.2: Temporal Domain
- Zone 2.2.1: Waters Below
- Zone 2.2.2: The Firmament
- Zone 2.2.3: Waters Above
- Zone 2.2.2.1: Condensed Matter

**Action Required:**
1. Update STYLE_GUIDE.md to remove "Zone 3 / Earth Prime" reference (line 18)
2. Standardize all multi-zone references to use the full designation (e.g., "Zone 2.2.1" rather than "Waters Below alone")
3. Create visual diagram showing complete zone hierarchy for consistency reference

## 2. WATERS TERMINOLOGY CONSISTENCY

### Inconsistency 2.1: "Waters" Terminology Clarity
**Severity:** MODERATE

**Issue:**
- Ch05 carefully distinguishes between "Waters Above" (Dark Energy, 68%) and "Waters Below" (Dark Matter, 27%)
- But Ch03_text.txt and FLOOD_content.txt sometimes refer to "the Waters" generically without specifying Above/Below
- Percentages are explicitly stated in Ch05 but not consistently cross-referenced in other chapters

**Evidence:**
- Ch05_text.txt lines 5.5.2-5.5.3: "Dark Matter = Waters Below at the Boundary" and "Dark Energy = Waters Above Pressure" with explicit 27%/68% breakdown
- Ch03_text.txt line 238: "Waters Below gather" without explicitly stating these are the 27% dark matter
- GLOSSARY.md line 167-169: Defines both separately but doesn't remind reader of 27%/68% relationship

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
When referring to Waters, always specify: "Waters Above (~68% of universe)" and "Waters Below (~27% of universe)" on first reference in each chapter. Generic "the Waters" acceptable only after full reference has been established.

## 3. FIRMAMENT TERMINOLOGY

### Inconsistency 3.1: "Membrane" vs. "Firmament" Usage
**Severity:** MINOR

**Issue:**
- Ch04 emphasizes "Firmament as Membrane" in title but then uses both "membrane" and "Firmament" seemingly interchangeably
- GLOSSARY.md (lines 127, 137) distinguishes between usage contexts but this distinction isn't consistently followed
- Some chapters use "the Firmament membrane" (redundant) vs. just "the Firmament" vs. just "the membrane"

**Evidence:**
- Ch04_text.txt line 1: "The Firmament as Membrane"
- Ch04_text.txt lines throughout: Uses both terms; sometimes "the membrane" alone, sometimes "the Firmament"
- GLOSSARY.md line 17: "Firmament (Raqia)...the membrane created on Day 2"
- GLOSSARY.md line 127: "Firmament (Zone 2.2.2): Membrane created Day 2"

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
Use **"the Firmament membrane"** or simply **"the Firmament"** — avoid "the membrane" alone when discussing Zone 2.2.2. The term "membrane" may be used when discussing the physics (e.g., "membrane tension," "membrane boundary"), but always clarify which zone is being referenced.

### Inconsistency 3.2: "Expanse" vs. "Firmament"
**Severity:** MINOR

**Issue:**
- Ch04_text.txt line 8 discusses "רָקִיעַ (Raqia)" with both "firmament" and "expanse" as translation options
- STARLIGHT_content.txt line 18 emphasizes that רָקִיעַ literally means "stretched-out thing" or "expanse"
- But canonical usage elsewhere defaults to "firmament" (English Bible tradition)

**Evidence:**
- Ch04_text.txt, section 4.1.2: "The Hebrew word translated as 'firmament' or 'expanse'"
- STARLIGHT_content.txt, section 2.1: "Therefore רָקִיעַ (raqia') means literally 'the stretched-out thing' or 'the expanse'"
- Most chapters use "Firmament" (English tradition) rather than "Expanse"

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
Use **"Firmament (רָקִיעַ, raqia—literally 'the stretched-out thing')"** with the etymology noted on first use per chapter. "Expanse" and "stretched-out" are acceptable synonym clarifications, but "Firmament" is the canonical English term.

## 6. FIVE GOVERNING PRINCIPLES NAMING

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
The five governing principles are exactly and only:
1. **Conservation** (complete at Day 7)
2. **Degradation** (entropy increase, four purposes)
3. **Symmetry** (God's immutability manifested)
4. **Duality** (complementary pairs)
5. **Sustaining** (continuous divine maintenance)

**Action Required:**
Update STYLE_GUIDE.md line 38-40 to replace "Hierarchy" with "Symmetry" and correct numbering if applicable.

## 7. HEBREW TERM SPELLING & TRANSLITERATION

### Inconsistency 7.1: Inconsistent Transliteration Systems
**Severity:** MINOR

**Issue:**
- Terms appear with and without diacritical marks
- "Raqia" vs. "raqia" capitalization varies
- Some transliterations use different romanization systems

**Evidence:**
- Ch03_text.txt: Uses both "undefinedbereshitundefined" with markers and "bereishit" without
- Ch04_text.txt: "רָקִיעַ (raqia)" with lowercase
- STARLIGHT_content.txt: "רָקִיעַ (raqia')" with final aleph marker
- Inconsistency in whether final letters are marked (aleph) or not

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
For Hebrew terms:
- Provide Hebrew letters first: **רָקִיעַ**
- Provide transliteration with diacritical marks: **raqia'** (with final aleph apostrophe)
- Provide English gloss in parentheses: **(stretched-out thing / membrane)**
- At first use in chapter, include all three; subsequent uses may use English only

## 9. CRITICAL PHYSICS TERMS

### Inconsistency 9.1: "Threshold" Definition Consistency
**Severity:** MODERATE

**Issue:**
- Ch08 defines threshold as "discontinuity" and "critical point where abrupt change occurs"
- Ch09 describes matter formation via "critical density threshold" (ρ_critical)
- But BLACK_HOLES_content.txt defines critical density as "2×10¹⁷ kg/m³ (nuclear density)"
- These are related but not identical—BLACK_HOLES appears to conflate the threshold with a specific numerical value

**Evidence:**
- Ch08_text.txt line 8.6: "A threshold is a critical point where abrupt change occurs"
- Ch09_text.txt line 9.5: "At some specific density ρ_critical, the system reaches a phase transition point"
- BLACK_HOLES_content.txt lines 27-29: "ρ_critical ≈ 2×10¹⁷ kg/m³ (nuclear density)"
- COSMOLOGY_content.txt lines 91-92: Same critical density value

**Recommended Canonical Version:**
Threshold Type #6 is defined conceptually as "discontinuity." When applied to matter formation, the specific threshold is **ρ_critical ≈ 2×10¹⁷ kg/m³**, which corresponds to nuclear density and the QCD phase transition point. Always maintain this distinction: the concept vs. the specific instantiation.

## 14. COSMOLOGICAL PARAMETERS

### Inconsistency 14.1: Hubble Constant Notation
**Severity:** MINOR

**Issue:**
- STARLIGHT_content.txt uses H_creation vs. H₀ (subscript notation)
- Some chapters use H₀ with subscript, others might use H0
- Different chapters don't consistently explain what each represents

**Evidence:**
- STARLIGHT_content.txt lines 125-127: "H_creation is the creation-mode Hubble parameter"
- Same file line 139: "H₀ ≈ 70 km/s/Mpc" with subscript

Always define on first use in each chapter: "H₀ ≈ 70 km/s/Mpc (current Hubble constant)" and "H_creation ≈ 10¹⁴ H₀ (creation-week expansion rate)."

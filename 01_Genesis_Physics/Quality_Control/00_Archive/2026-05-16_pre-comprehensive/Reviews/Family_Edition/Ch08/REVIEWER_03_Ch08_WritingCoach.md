### 4. LOGICAL FLOW: PASS

**Finding:** The argument scaffold is clean, inevitable, and builds toward a pastoral close.

**Architecture:**

The family activity in §9 is earned (the reader has now seen five laws named and explained; §9 gives them a way to hold them). The ending returns to the homestead, to the person, to the covenant God has sworn.

**Transitions:** Clean. §2 to §3 is: "I want you to feel the weight of what I just said. Noether proved in 1918 that *if the laws of physics are the same at every moment,* energy must be conserved." The reader sees the connective tissue. No gap.

### 8. CHAPTER ENDING: PASS

**Finding:** The chapter ends with both completion and motivation forward.

**Passage (§10, lines 282-312):**
The closing returns to Malachi 3:6 and *deepens* the reader's hearing of it. The first half of the verse—"For I the LORD do not change"—is now heard as *physics.* The second half—"therefore you, O children of Jacob, are not consumed"—is now heard as *pastoral promise for right now,* not metaphor. The final sentences are:

> "The Lord does not change. Carry Malachi 3:6 with you this week. When the kitchen is a mess after dinner, remember that entropy has a direction because the Sustainer is writing the cadenza He promised. When you watch the sunrise come over the ridge at the same time it did yesterday, remember that the day is held by a covenant the Lord has sworn to Himself."

**Why it works:**
- **Completion:** The chapter has answered its opening question. The laws *do* hold. They hold because the Lawgiver's character does not change. Malachi said so. Noether proved it. Physics measures it.
- **Motivation forward:** The final image—"the kitchen is a mess after dinner"—points toward entropy and the Second Law, which §5 established as pointing *to the Fall.* Chapter 9 will walk into the forces that hold matter together *within* that broken cosmos. The reader senses there is more to know.
- **Pastoral, not academic.** The ending speaks to a parent in the kitchen on a Tuesday evening, not to a physicist in a seminar. This is the AUTHOR_VOICE mandate: *the same voice that would land with a skeptical engineer and a homeschool mother in the same room.*

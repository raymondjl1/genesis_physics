### One Note (not a failure, but worth naming)

**Forward signal to Ch15 could be slightly stronger at the canonical pause-point in the manuscript.**

**Recommendation:** This is a *note*, not a failure. The chapter as written passes all architectural requirements. The forward signal is sufficient. If the author wants to deepen the signal, §12's closing could (optionally) add one sentence like: *"The architecture points to an Architect. The handiwork points to a Maker.

### P3 — CRAFTSMANSHIP NOTES (5 findings)

**[P3] [C1] The why-for-MBSE-first is implicit; operationally sound.** (Flagged by: REVIEWER_02.)

- **Description:** §8 presents MBSE traceability (*every requirement traces to its origin*) and teaches the reader to ask *where does the framework come from?* The "why" for MBSE itself is clear (author pioneered it at Insitu; it works). However, the "why" for why this is the *first* tool before teaching specific physics answers is implicit.
- **Assessment:** The implicit logic works for the pastoral register. A high-schooler can use observation-vs.-interpretation in any subject (calculus, history) without needing the framework's specific answers. This is operationally sound.
- **Recommendation:** Keep as is. Implicit logic appropriate for family register.

**[P3] [C2] Elementary reader may ask "why does the day-band distinction matter?"** (Flagged by: REVIEWER_05.)

- **Description:** §6 (middle school) explains the *yom* distinction (HIGH on Hebrew grammar, MODERATE on cosmological time). A 14-year-old may ask "so why does it matter if the days are days? Couldn't God just work fast?" The chapter is correct; the parent may want a one-sentence "why does it matter" before the child moves on.
- **Assessment:** Parenting moment, not chapter weakness. The framework's *why* is present in prior chapters (Ch4).
- **Recommendation:** No change needed. This is a parental calibration, not a chapter issue.

**[P3] [C4] Elementary reader may naturally hear confidence labels.** (Flagged by: REVIEWER_05.)

- **Description:** §11 names the three confidence levels (HIGH/MODERATE/OPEN). However, elementary-age children may already hear these labels at the dinner table. The chapter could empower earlier confidence-label exposure by saying: *"If your elementary kid hears these labels naturally, that's fine — your child will respect you for honesty even before she understands what MODERATE means."*
- **Assessment:** Optional permission; the structure as written is sound. The chapter correctly starts labeling with middle-schoolers.
- **Recommendation:** Consider for future edition. Not required for publication.

**[P3] [C6] NRO analogy is exemplary cleared-community-discretion modeling.** (Flagged by: REVIEWER_02.)

- **Description:** Paragraph (§7): *"When I worked at the National Reconnaissance Office, the most respected officers were the ones who said 'I don't know' when they did not know..."* This is the deepest credibility move in the chapter.
- **Assessment:** Not a finding; a strength note. This passage transforms vulnerability (not knowing) into strength (intellectual credibility) through lived professional experience. Model for "why" reasoning in operator-engineer voice.
- **Recommendation:** Hold as exemplary. No change.

**[P3] [C7] Closing emotional coherence is exceptional.** (Flagged by: REVIEWER_02.)

- **Description:** §12 ends with scene (wife closes Bible, dog stretches, porch light off, family meeting on Sunday) rather than doctrine. The "why" for teaching is made visible through the sustaining household frame (Hebrews 1:3 callback).
- **Assessment:** Exemplary execution. No change needed.
- **Recommendation:** No action required; note for franchise voice standard.

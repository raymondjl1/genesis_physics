## ARCHITECTURAL NOTES

### Strength 1: The Consistency of the Constants
The chapter's deepest move is linking the constancy of *c* and *G* to the constancy of the Lawgiver. This is not new theology; it's a translation of Emmy Noether's symmetry principles into biblical vocabulary (which Ch 8 already set up). Ch 9 applies that principle specifically to *these numbers*, making the physical constancy a theological claim. The cascade is clean: Ch 8 (laws are signature of unchanging Lawgiver) → Ch 9 (constants are numbers in that signature, held by the Holder) → Foundations (mathematical machinery of constancy under time-translation and spatial symmetry).

### Strength 2: The Unification Without Preaching
The chapter does something difficult: it presents the unification of gravity and light (*c* and *G* as cousins) without ever invoking Einstein's equations, without ever using the word "spacetime," without *once* sounding like it's trying to convert the reader to a framework. The unification is *shown* (one fabric, two behaviors) before it's *named* (cousins). By the time §6 offers the theological reading (Psalm 19, Colossians 1), the family has already understood the physics.

### Strength 3: The Honest Leveling
§8 (Honest About What We Know) is crucial and well-executed. The chapter does not claim to derive *c* and *G* from first principles. It claims:
- **RIGOROUS:** The *relationships* between the constants and the membrane's geometry (c² ∝ tension/density, G ∝ stiffness)
- **WORKING:** The specific numerical values depend on zone parameters that are fit from observation
- **OPEN:** The quantum-gravity regime and strong-field cases are still being resolved

This is mature science communication. It does not oversell the framework. It builds family confidence by being honest about the limits.

### Potential Point of Extension (Not a Red Flag, but Worth Noting)
The chapter correctly does not dive into why the zones have their particular parameters. The question "Why is the firmament's tension *this* value and not some other value?" is OPEN. The chapter acknowledges this implicitly by treating the parameters as fitted-from-observation in §8. This is appropriate for Book 2. If a reader pushes further, they find Foundations, which will say the same thing: we have a picture, it works, the first-principles prediction is ongoing work. No false certainty.

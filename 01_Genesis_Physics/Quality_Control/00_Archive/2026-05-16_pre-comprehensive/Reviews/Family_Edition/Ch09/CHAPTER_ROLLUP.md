Some scholars read it as "nursling" or "companion" rather than "architect." The chapter's selection of the construction reading is defensible but should acknowledge scholarly debate. (Flagged by: REVIEWER-09.)

**Location:** §4, Proverbs 8:22–31 passage.

**Current Text:** *"the Hebrew is amon, which in construction vocabulary means a skilled craftsman, an architect."*

**Recommendation:** Add a brief acknowledgment: *"The Hebrew amon can be read as either 'one brought up' or 'craftsman'; we follow the construction reading, which aligns with the framework's view of creation as designed."*

**Severity:** P2 (enhancement for intellectual honesty; not blocking)  
**Fix Effort:** One sentence insertion.

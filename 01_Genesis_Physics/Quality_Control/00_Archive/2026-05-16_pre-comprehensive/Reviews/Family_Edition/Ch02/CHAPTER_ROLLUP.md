### P3 / Polish

#### [C3 — Physical Intuition First] §4: Dark Energy Explanation Order  
**Issue:** The 1998 discovery paragraph delivers intuition in *aftermath* order rather than *lead* order. Excellent drama, but intuition-building could be strengthened.

**Evidence:**  
- REVIEWER_02 ("But Why?" Reader) notes: passage reads "They were trying to figure out how fast expansion was slowing down... It wasn't... It was speeding up." The physical intuition ("if space is expanding and gravity pulls inward, shouldn't expansion slow down?") arrives *after* the surprise, not before.

**Quote:** *"They were trying to figure out how fast the expansion of the universe was slowing down, because it **had** to be slowing down — gravity pulls everything toward everything else, and an expanding universe under gravity's pull is a decelerating universe. It wasn't slowing down. It was *speeding up.*"*

**Fix:** Consider adding intuition *before* surprise or reorganize to: (1) "Here's what physicists expected..." (2) "Here's why they expected it..." (3) "But measurement showed..." This would align even more tightly with "intuition first" mandate.

**Severity:** P3 (polish)  
**Concern tag:** C3 (Physical Intuition First)  
**Flagged by:** REVIEWER_02  
**Impact:** Non-blocking. Intuition *is* present; just in slightly less-optimal order. Chapter demonstrates mastery of the standard; this is refinement at the margin.

## SECTION 1: HOW GRAVITY WORKS

### Causal Claims in Framework

**Ch10 (Four Forces):**
> "Matter is condensed Waters Below. Each mass is a local intrusion through the Firmament. The Firmament is under tension and wants to remain flat. But matter creates strain. The membrane warps, curving downward toward Waters Below. This curvature has a consequence: paths through curved Firmament naturally follow geodesics approaching masses. This is gravitational attraction."

**Ch04 (Firmament):**
> "Curvature: The Bending of Space Near Mass. General relativity predicts, and observations confirm, that space curves near massive objects... In our model, this curvature has a deeper source: the Firmament curves because matter, which resides in the Firmament, is still connected to its Waters Below source. The membrane bends toward this source."

**Black Holes Paper:**
> "Every bit of matter in existence maintains a subtle connection to the Waters Below from which it formed. This connection is not mystical but physical: it is a curvature in the Firmament membrane pointing toward the Waters Below source."

### Cross-Document Analysis

**Ch05 (Waters):**
> "Waters Below: Dense, concentrated, cool (potential energy), source for matter formation, gravitational anchor"

**Ch07 (Three Fundamentals):**
> "Waters Below: The Gravitational Source... Observable, this corresponds to what observational cosmologists term 'dark matter'... Dark matter reveals itself entirely through gravitational effects: the rotation curves of galaxies, the gravitational lensing of light around massive structures, the orbital dynamics of galaxy clusters."

**Cosmology Paper:**
> "Dark matter = Waters Below at the Boundary. When God commanded the Waters to gather on Day 3, most of the Waters Below condensed into matter within the Firmament (Zone 2.2.2.1). But not all. Some Waters Below remain at the boundary between the Firmament and Zone 2.2.1, never quite fully crossing the threshold to become condensed matter. These boundary Waters Below still affect the Firmament through their curvature influence—they have gravitational effects—but they do not emit light or interact with electromagnetic radiation."

### INCONSISTENCY #1: Gravity Causation Semantics (MODERATE)

**The Problem:**

Ch10 describes gravity as "curvature of the Firmament" caused by "matter creating strain" and the "connection to Waters Below source." This is presented as ACTIVE causation: matter causes the curvature.

But Ch07 and the Cosmology paper describe gravity as resulting from "Waters Below at the boundary" that "exert inward-pulling effects" and "curve the Firmament." Here the causation flows from Waters Below, not from matter itself.

**Conflicting Passages:**

Ch10: "Matter... creates strain. The membrane warps, curving downward toward Waters Below."
- Causation: Matter → Curvature

Ch07: "The Waters Below are not distributed randomly or uniformly. They concentrate most densely in the regions where matter has previously condensed. A galaxy, formed from Waters that have undergone the critical phase transition into ordinary matter, remains gravitationally connected to the Waters Below from which it originated."
- Causation: Waters Below concentration → Curvature (matter is aftermath)

Cosmology: "These boundary Waters Below still affect the Firmament through their curvature influence... Dark matter is not a new particle type... It is an old component of the model—Waters—that we simply did not recognize."
- Causation: Waters Below → Gravitational effects (matter is separate phenomenon)

**Why It Matters:**

This affects the interpretation of dark matter. Is gravity:
- (A) Caused by matter's intrinsic connection to Waters Below?
- (B) Caused by Waters Below themselves, with matter merely marking where Waters concentrated?
- (C) A hybrid where both matter and Waters Below contribute?

The Black Holes paper states: "Every bit of matter in existence maintains a subtle connection to the Waters Below from which it formed... The Firmament bends toward this source." This seems to support (A). But the Cosmology paper explicitly states that dark matter arises from uncondensed Waters Below, suggesting (B).

**Resolution Path:**

The most coherent interpretation:
1. Waters Below naturally curve the Firmament (this is inherent to their nature as dense energy)
2. When Waters Below condense into matter, they bring this gravitational property WITH them
3. Therefore, matter causes curvature *because* it is still Waters Below in condensed form
4. Uncondensed Waters Below at boundaries cause additional curvature (dark matter effect)
5. Total observed gravity = (Condensed Waters in matter) + (Uncondensed Waters at boundary)

**Severity: MODERATE**
**Suggested Canonical Treatment:** Include the resolution path above to clarify that matter's gravitational effect arises from its Waters Below substrate, and that matter and boundary Waters Below both contribute to observed curvature, with boundary Waters being "excess" that didn't fully condense.

## SECTION 2: HOW MATTER FORMS

### Causal Claims in Framework

**Ch09 (Matter Formation):**
> "When Waters Below gather and density increases at nodes, a critical point approaches. At some specific density ρ_critical ≈ 2×10¹⁷ kg/m³, the system reaches a phase transition point. Below this threshold, Waters Below remain in fluid, dynamic state. At the threshold, dramatic transition occurs. Above the threshold, Waters have condensed into stable matter patterns... The forward transition (Waters → matter): Energy is released in the exothermic transition. The process is downhill, spontaneous once initiated."

**Ch07 (Three Fundamentals):**
> "When Waters Below cross a critical density threshold, a phase transition occurs. The fluid, pre-material creation energy becomes locked into stable patterns. Atoms form. Molecules assemble... matter is condensed Waters Below in a different state—a dramatic phase transition."

**Cosmology Paper (Mature Creation section):**
> "Just as Adam possessed functional maturity (neural connections, bone density), stars possessed functional maturity including established light streams... stars were created as functioning systems with light output established."

**Starlight Paper:**
> "Stars created Day 4 with functional integrity. The Firmament was created uniformly... Firmament was created uniformly (Waters Above distributed uniformly in perpendicular dimension). No 'horizon problem'—uniformity by design, not by causal contact."

### INCONSISTENCY #2: Matter Formation vs. Mature Creation (MODERATE)

**The Problem:**

Ch09 describes matter formation as a continuous process triggered by "gathering at nodes" and "critical threshold crossing." This is presented as an *active, dynamic, physical process* driven by:
1. Organizing vibration establishing nodes
2. Waters gathering through pressure gradients
3. Reaching critical density threshold
4. Spontaneous phase transition

But the Cosmology and Starlight papers argue that stars and matter were created "with functional maturity" on Day 4, implying they were created READY-FORMED rather than gradually organized through gathering.

**Conflicting Passages:**

Ch09: "As organizing vibration is introduced to Waters Below, standing waves establish nodes... Waters flow from high-energy regions (antinodes) toward low-energy regions (nodes)... The process accelerates exponentially... What begins as a slight concentration becomes... dramatic concentration."
- Implication: Matter forms through progressive gathering and acceleration

Cosmology: "Stars created Day 4 were created as functioning light sources, fulfilling immediate purpose: 'to give light upon the earth' (Genesis 1:15, 17). Just as Adam possessed functional maturity (neural connections, bone density), stars possessed functional maturity including established light streams."
- Implication: Stars were created mature, not formed through gathering process

Starlight: "Biblical Precedent: Genesis 1:14-15 describes luminaries placed 'in the firmament' on Day 4... The immediate functional purpose suggests immediate light arrival... stars were created as functioning light-giving systems, which necessarily includes light delivery."
- Implication: Light was established immediately, not accumulated through gathering process

**Why It Matters:**

This directly impacts the timeline of creation:
- **Ch09 interpretation**: Matter formation takes TIME (gathering, acceleration, threshold crossing). Multiple cycles of gathering might occur on Day 3, with organization progressive.
- **Cosmology/Starlight interpretation**: Matter was created ready-made in final form on Day 4, with no gathering process required.

The inconsistency affects whether the Flood model's crustal collapse (requiring pre-existing massive underground reservoirs) is consistent with the matter formation timeline.

**Resolution Path:**

The most coherent synthesis:
1. **Creation-Mode Physics (Days 1-6):** Matter CAN form rapidly through gathering+threshold crossing (Ch09 mechanism is valid)
2. **God's Efficient Creation:** God commanded gathering to occur at creation-week speed (dramatically faster than sustaining-mode gathering)
3. **Functional Maturity:** By Day 4 morning, when stars were created, gathering WAS COMPLETE—stars existed as fully formed, ready-to-operate systems
4. **Observable Universe:** We observe matter in sustaining mode, where gathering processes operate at current speed (star formation takes millions of years)
5. **Both Perspectives Valid:** Ch09 describes the MECHANISM. Cosmology/Starlight describe the OUTCOME: Creation provided mature matter ready-to-function.

**Severity: MODERATE**
**Suggested Canonical Treatment:** Clarify that Ch09's gathering mechanism IS operational during creation week but at dramatically accelerated rates compared to sustaining-mode physics. Mature creation (Cosmology/Starlight interpretation) is the RESULT of rapid gathering process, not contradicting it.

## SECTION 5: HOW DOES FTL WORK?

### Causal Claims in Framework

**FTL Paper (Five Mechanisms):**

The paper presents 5 candidate mechanisms:
1. **Membrane Folding:** Fold the Firmament to bring distant points together in perpendicular dimensions
2. **Perpendicular Transit:** Exit Firmament into Waters, navigate perpendicular dimensions, re-enter
3. **Zone 1 Transit:** Route through Heaven Prime (eternal domain, non-spatial)
4. **Resonance Bridging:** Exploit standing wave modes to create resonant connections
5. **Threshold Manipulation:** Dissolve matter back to Waters, propagate through Waters, re-condense

### INCONSISTENCY #3: FTL Integration with Framework (MODERATE)

**The Problem:**

None of the 5 FTL mechanisms is integrated into the core framework documents. The FTL paper is presented as "exploratory" and "speculative." But each mechanism makes causal claims about how Firmament properties enable transit:

**Mechanism 2 (Perpendicular Transit)** claims:
> "At departure point A, overcome the membrane binding potential (the energy barrier keeping matter confined to w=0, v=0)"

But the core framework documents (Ch04, Ch10) do NOT describe any "binding potential" that confines matter to w=0, v=0. The framework describes matter as "localized in the Firmament" and "condensed Waters" but does NOT specify the mechanism preventing matter from naturally "lifting off" into the perpendicular dimensions.

**Mechanism 4 (Resonance Bridging)** claims:
> "The Firmament is a vibrating membrane... Certain modes create nodes and antinodes... Two distant points that share the same vibrational mode are phase-connected... Matter could 'ride' this standing wave, transferring along the mode from one antinode to another"

But this contradicts the gathering mechanism in Ch09, which states that matter GATHERS AT NODES but is NOT transferred between nodes. The gathering mechanism is about STATIONARY accumulation at nodal points, not about matter RIDING along vibrational modes.

**Mechanism 5 (Threshold Manipulation)** claims:
> "The 'dissolved' pattern information propagates through the Waters Below to a designated reconstitution point... Pattern information persists in Zone 1"

But the framework (particularly Ch07 and Ch09) does NOT establish that pattern information persists independently of matter. It establishes that pattern IS the CONFIGURATION of matter, not a separate metaphysical entity. The relationship between pattern and Waters Below is never specified precisely enough to determine whether pattern survives dissolution.

**Why It Matters:**

The FTL mechanisms are presented as physically grounded in the zone architecture BUT are built on unstated assumptions:
- Binding potentials confining matter
- Matter riding vibrational modes
- Pattern persistence through dissolution

None of these are formally derived from the core framework. They are speculation ABOUT what the framework might support, not derivations FROM the framework.

**Resolution Path:**

1. **Clarify membrane binding:** Explicitly derive the binding potential that confines matter to the Firmament surface (presumably arising from the threshold between condensed matter and Waters, or the curvature of the Firmament itself)

2. **Distinguish gathering from resonance:** Clarify whether standing wave modes can transfer matter (resonance bridging) or merely concentrate it (gathering). These appear to be fundamentally different mechanisms.

3. **Define pattern persistence:** Formally state whether pattern information (as in DNA, consciousness, or 'identity') persists when matter dissolves. This is crucial for threshold manipulation to work.

4. **Classify FTL mechanisms:** Explicitly state which mechanisms are:
   - Derivable from existing framework (rigorous)
   - Consistent with framework but not yet derived (plausible)
   - Speculative extensions (exploratory)

**Severity: MODERATE**
**Suggested Canonical Treatment:** Create a separate section in Chapter 6 (Zone Architecture) or a new Chapter 14 deriving the binding potential, resonance properties, and pattern persistence from first principles. This would either validate or require revision of the FTL mechanisms.

## SECTION 6: HOW DOES ENTROPY WORK?

### Causal Claims in Framework

**Ch13 (Thermodynamics):**
> "The Second Law states: In an isolated system, entropy (S) never spontaneously decreases: ΔS ≥ 0... In our framework, entropy is defined by Boltzmann's formula: S = k ln(Ω)... The key insight: ordered systems have few microstates; disordered systems have many."

> "Waters Separation as Low-Entropy Injection: On Day 2, God separated the Waters: Above and Below were divided by the Firmament. This created a low-entropy state... Entropy increases as the universe slowly evolves toward eventual mixing (equilibrium)."

> "Purpose of Entropy: Four Theological Functions:
> 1. Temporal Boundary Marker
> 2. Dependence Enforcer
> 3. Hope Creator
> 4. Moral-Physical Parallel"

**Cosmology Paper:**
> "Accelerated nuclear decay during Genesis Flood... All decay systems were affected proportionally—the same barrier reduction factor applied universally."

**Flood Paper (Integration section):**
> "Conservation Laws Upheld: The flood represents redistribution of existing water rather than creation or destruction of matter. Total H₂O quantity remains constant... Energy conservation similarly holds throughout. Gravitational potential energy from elevated floodwaters converts to kinetic energy during drainage, then dissipates as heat through friction."

### INCONSISTENCY #4: Entropy Causation in Sustaining Mode (MINOR)

**The Problem:**

Ch13 describes entropy increase as STATISTICAL: "Random thermal motion explores microstates equally. By probability, systems naturally evolve toward states with many microstates (high entropy)."

This is fine during sustaining-mode, where the Second Law operates through probability.

BUT Ch13 also claims that entropy serves four THEOLOGICAL purposes, particularly that God "designed decay into creation" and that entropy increase is a consequence of the "Curse" (Romans 5:12). This suggests entropy is ACTIVELY ENFORCED by God, not merely emergent from probability.

If entropy is actively maintained by God in sustaining mode (the way it's maintained by the Sabbath Boundary in creation mode), then the statistical explanation is incomplete. The mechanism would need to involve continuous divine action, not passive probability.

**Conflicting Passages:**

Ch13 (Statistical mechanism):
> "Random thermal motion explores microstates equally. By probability, systems naturally evolve toward states with many microstates (high entropy) and away from states with few microstates (low entropy). This is why entropy increases—disorder is simply statistically more probable than order."

Ch13 (Theological mechanism):
> "Decay enforces humility. We cannot exist by our own power... Creation requires Christ's sustaining presence. If He ceased sustaining, the universe would collapse—entropy would catastrophically increase until equilibrium (heat death) was reached."

**Why It Matters:**

This affects how we understand sustaining-mode physics:
- **Statistical interpretation:** Sustaining-mode physics is autonomous; God established the laws, but they operate through probability without active moment-to-moment intervention
- **Theological interpretation:** Sustaining-mode physics requires continuous divine action to maintain the entropy gradient. God actively prevents equilibrium.

The Flood paper describes "redistribution of water" and "energy conversion," suggesting sustaining-mode physics operates mechanically without divine intervention (other than the initial setup of conservation laws). This supports the statistical interpretation.

But Ch13's claim that entropy serves theological purposes and that Christ's sustaining is REQUIRED to prevent heat death suggests God is actively maintaining order against entropy's tendency, supporting the theological interpretation.

**Resolution Path:**

The most coherent synthesis:
1. **Sustaining-mode physics is BOTH statistical AND divinely sustained:** Entropy naturally increases through probability (statistical mechanism), but God's sustaining prevents catastrophic collapse (theological mechanism).
2. **The Waters separation is the key:** The initial separation (Day 2) creates the low-entropy state. The Second Law then operates through probability to increase entropy from that state. God maintains the separation through sustaining (Colossians 1:17).
3. **If God ceased sustaining, entropy would skyrocket:** The separation would collapse (Waters Above/Below would mix), and the universe would collapse to equilibrium immediately (theological consequence of removing sustaining).
4. **But until sustaining ceases, the statistical law operates:** Entropy increases through probability, not through divine intervention in sustaining mode.

**Severity: MINOR**
**Suggested Canonical Treatment:** Add a clarification to Ch13 stating explicitly: "Entropy increase operates through statistical probability in sustaining mode, but the underlying LOW-ENTROPY INITIAL STATE (Waters separation) is maintained by divine sustaining. If sustaining were removed, entropy would catastrophically increase. The Second Law describes probability; sustaining prevents the catastrophic conclusion of that probability."

## SECTION 7: WHAT HAPPENS AT ZONE BOUNDARIES?

### Causal Claims in Framework

**Ch03 (Zone Hierarchy):**
> "The zone architecture model proposes exactly this structure. Reality is not monolithic but hierarchically organized. Each zone has specific properties, functions, and relationships to other zones."

> "Zone 0: The Eternal Origin. Zone 0 is God... The Word (Logos) serves as the interface between Zone 0 and all created zones."

**Black Holes Paper (Event Horizon as Zone Boundary):**
> "Zone Transition Conditions: Outside horizon (r > r_s): Zone 2.2.2 (Temporal Firmament)... At horizon (r = r_s): Membrane transition surface—curvature K = K_max, membrane properties change discontinuously. Inside horizon (r < r_s): Zone 2.2.1 (Atemporal Waters Below)"

> "The 'one-way membrane' property arises from topology: membrane curvature is so extreme that all geodesics point toward Waters Below. Light cannot escape because the membrane itself propagates waves preferentially inward at the breach."

**Flood Paper (No Boundary Breach):**
> "Critically, this model does not invoke zone boundary breach or Firmament membrane failure... The underground water is literal H₂O stored within the crust and upper mantle—condensed matter (Zone 2.2.2.1), not Waters Below (Zone 2.2.1)."

**Cosmology Paper (Creation-Week Physics):**
> "God's creative act on Days 1-6 is the organization of Zone 2. The sequence follows a logical pattern... Day 1: 'Let there be light.' God creates light, establishing temporal sequence and separating darkness."

### INCONSISTENCY #5: Zone Boundary Crossing Conditions (MODERATE)

**The Problem:**

The framework establishes that zones are SEPARATE and DISTINCT, with the Firmament serving as the boundary. But different documents describe zone boundaries differently:

**Black Holes paper** describes black holes as:
> "...local membrane failures—punctures where extreme matter concentration causes the Firmament to breach, creating direct interfaces between the temporal domain (Zone 2.2.2) and the atemporal Waters Below (Zone 2.2.1)"

This implies that zone boundaries CAN be breached by physical processes (extreme matter density).

BUT **Flood Paper** explicitly states:
> "Critically, this model does not invoke zone boundary breach or Firmament membrane failure. The Waters Above (Zone 2.2.3) remain in their perpendicular dimension throughout."

This states that zone boundaries CANNOT be breached (at least not during the Flood).

**Which is correct?**

Black holes represent ONE scenario where breaching occurs (justified as exceptional—matter density exceeding structural limits). The Flood represents ANOTHER scenario where breaching does NOT occur (water redistribution stays within Zone 2.2.2.1 condensed matter).

But the conditions for when breaching CAN vs. CANNOT occur are never explicitly stated.

**Why It Matters:**

This affects the explanation of several phenomena:
1. **Black holes:** Can they breach into Waters Below, or should they be contained within Firmament?
2. **Eschatological events:** 2 Peter 3:10 describes cosmic dissolution "with intense heat." Is this a zone boundary failure?
3. **FTL mechanisms:** Which mechanisms require breaching zones, and which can occur without?
4. **Matter stability:** If zone boundaries can be breached by density, is matter truly stable, or only stable below critical density?

**Resolution Path:**

Explicitly define the zone boundary crossing conditions:
1. **Under sustaining-mode physics:** Zone boundaries are maintained by divine sustaining; they can be stressed but not breached under normal matter densities
2. **Under creation-mode physics:** Boundaries can be breached during creation week (evident from Genesis 1 reorganization of zones)
3. **Under extreme stress:** When membrane curvature reaches K_max, breach occurs (black hole formation—explicit exception)
4. **Under eschatological conditions:** God may choose to dissolve boundaries during cosmic renewal (2 Peter 3:10)

**Severity: MODERATE**
**Suggested Canonical Treatment:** Add explicit conditions to Ch04 stating: "The Firmament boundary is maintained by divine sustaining under normal conditions and cannot be breached by matter below critical density. At ρ ≥ ρ_Planck, membrane failure occurs (black holes). During creation week, God commanded boundary reorganization (Days 1-2). At eschatological renewal, God may dissolve boundaries entirely."

## SECTION 8: HOW DOES STARLIGHT TRAVEL?

### Causal Claims in Framework

**Starlight Paper:**
> "Rapid Initial Expansion (Creation Week)... The heavens were stretched out during Days 2-4. This was a completed action. Ongoing Gradual Expansion (Sustaining Mode)... God continues stretching the heavens, but at much slower rate. This is current Hubble expansion... Two-phase expansion model... rapid Initial Expansion (Creation Week): Described by perfect tense verbs. The heavens were stretched out during Days 2-4."

> "Physical Mechanism: Light propagation in Expanding Space... photons propagate at c through the Firmament membrane. During rapid expansion, the membrane itself stretches, carrying the photons along."

**Cosmology Paper:**
> "Creation-Week Light Propagation Through Waters Above... During Days 1-6, before the system closed at Day 7, light from distant stars could have traversed these perpendicular dimensions, arriving at Earth nearly instantaneously from our frame of reference."

> "Mature Creation with Functional Light Streams... Stars created to 'give light upon the earth' necessarily include light delivery mechanisms."

### INCONSISTENCY #6: Light Propagation Mechanism (MODERATE)

**The Problem:**

The **Starlight Paper** proposes that light propagated through the EXPANDING FIRMAMENT during creation week. The expanding space carries photons with it, so photons cover vast comoving distances in short proper time.

The **Cosmology Paper** proposes that light propagated through the PERPENDICULAR DIMENSIONS (Waters Above) during creation week, achieving near-instantaneous arrival.

These are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE mechanisms:
- Starlight: Light travels THROUGH the Firmament (along x, y, z) but benefits from expansion
- Cosmology: Light travels PERPENDICULAR to the Firmament (through w dimension) bypassing Firmament distances entirely

**Conflicting Passages:**

Starlight Paper:
> "Light propagates at speed c through the Firmament membrane. During rapid expansion, the membrane itself stretches, carrying the photons along... The comoving distance r covered vastly exceeds c × t because space itself is expanding."

> "For H_creation >> 1 and t_obs - t_emit ~ 2 days: r ≈ (c/H_creation) × exp(-H_creation × t_emit). This shows comoving distance r can be arbitrarily large depending on H_creation, even with t_obs - t_emit being only days."

Cosmology Paper:
> "During creation week, light may have propagated through perpendicular dimensions (Waters Above regions) rather than exclusively through Firmament space... During Days 1-6, before the system closed at Day 7, light from distant stars could have traversed these perpendicular dimensions, arriving at Earth nearly instantaneously from our frame of reference."

**Why It Matters:**

These mechanisms have different implications:
1. **Expansion mechanism:** Requires that the Firmament actually expanded at H_creation ≈ 10¹⁴ H₀ during Days 2-4. No perpendicular transit needed.
2. **Perpendicular mechanism:** Requires that light can propagate perpendicular to the Firmament (which contradicts Ch04's statement that light IS oscillation of the Firmament itself).
3. **Observational predictions:** The two mechanisms produce different predictions for redshift patterns, CMB properties, and cosmic structure.

**Resolution Path:**

The most coherent synthesis:
1. **Starlight Paper mechanism is PRIMARY:** Light propagated through EXPANDING Firmament. The rapid expansion during Days 2-4 is the main mechanism for rapid light transit.
2. **Cosmology Paper mechanism is SECONDARY:** Before rapid expansion concluded (around Day 3 evening), perpendicular transit may have been possible as a SUPPLEMENTARY mechanism, but the primary mechanism was expansion.
3. **Genesis 1:14 support:** "The luminaries were placed 'in the firmament'" supports the expansion mechanism (they are IN the expanding Firmament, not accessing perpendicular dimensions).
4. **Both can coexist:** Some light may have propagated through expansion, some through perpendicular transit (if the boundary was unstable during creation week), but expansion is the canonical mechanism.

**Severity: MODERATE**
**Suggested Canonical Treatment:** Revise Cosmology Paper to state explicitly: "The primary mechanism for distant starlight arrival was rapid Firmament expansion during Days 2-4 (per Starlight Paper). The perpendicular transit mechanism described here should be considered secondary/supplementary. The canonical mechanism is expansion, not perpendicular transit."

### INCONSISTENCY #7: Mathematical Model-Text Integration (MINOR)

**The Problem:**

The mathematical models derive physical constants from "first principles" but the derivations depend on quantities never formally defined in the prose chapters:
- ξ_A (Waters Above distance scale) - assumed to equal c/H₀ without justification
- η_B (Waters Below distance scale) - stated as nuclear size without derivation
- k_ξ, k_η (restoring force constants) - calculated from ρ_A, ρ_B never fully defined
- g_ξ, g_η (coupling constants) - introduced in Model 2 without chapter derivation

The Tier 1 Models document states:
> "Status: ⚠ MODEL PARTIAL (framework established, exact values need refinement)"

This explicitly acknowledges that the models are INCOMPLETE as descriptions. They take observed constants and work backward to geometric ratios, rather than deriving them from framework principles.

**Why It Matters:**

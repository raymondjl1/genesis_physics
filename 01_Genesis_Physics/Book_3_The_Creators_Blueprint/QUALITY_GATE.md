## Governing Principles (from 00_SERIES_VISION.md)

1. **Always Answer Why** — The "why" for this audience begins with scripture: "Because God designed it this way." Physics confirms what the Bible reveals.
2. **Scripture Without Apology, Physics Without Compromise** — The Bible leads. Physics follows. No proof-texting. No watered-down science.
3. **One Voice** — Warm, confident, reverent, teachable. This is NOT Book 2 with Bible verses added. It's a scripture-first teaching resource.
4. **Accessibility Is a Requirement** — A parent without a science degree must be able to teach from this book.

## Chapter Validation Checklist

Run this for every chapter before marking it draft-complete:

### Pre-Writing Check
- [ ] Chapter outline matches `BOOK_SERIES_STRATEGY.md` The Creator's Blueprint section
- [ ] Key scriptures for this chapter identified and verified (correct book/chapter/verse, used in context)
- [ ] Physics claims traced upward to Book 2 → Book 1 → Foundations (nothing unsupported)

### Writing Standards
- [ ] Chapter opens with scripture, not physics
- [ ] At least 5 direct scripture quotations with full citations
- [ ] Physics presented as confirmation of what scripture reveals
- [ ] No equations — all concepts explained through analogy and narrative
- [ ] No jargon without definition
- [ ] Discussion questions (5+) that spark real conversation
- [ ] Family activity doable with household items in under 30 minutes
- [ ] "But What About?" section addresses real questions kids ask
- [ ] Chapter ends with a takeaway that connects faith and understanding

### Post-Writing Validation
- [ ] **"But Why?" Reader** — PASS
- [ ] **Writing Coach** — PASS (voice warm/reverent/teachable, Grade 9-12 readability)
- [ ] **Consistency Auditor** — PASS (all terms, constants, scripture citations correct)
- [ ] **Homeschool Mom** — PASS (teachable, confidence-building, would recommend to co-op)
- [ ] **Skeptic** — PASS (no proof-texting, no circular reasoning, honest about limits)

### Cascade Verification
- [ ] Every physics claim in this chapter appears in Book 2
- [ ] Every Book 2 claim traces to Book 1
- [ ] Every Book 1 claim traces to a Foundations volume derivation

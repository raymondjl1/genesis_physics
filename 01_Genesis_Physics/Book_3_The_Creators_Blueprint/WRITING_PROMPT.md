### Writing Process

For each chapter:

1. **Create CHAPTER_SPEC.md** from template. Include: mission, Book 2 source chapter(s), scripture plan (all passages with book/chapter/verse), discussion question plan, family activity plan, "But What About?" questions.

2. **Verify Book 2 Source** — Check that the Book 2 chapter supporting this one is written and verified. If not, this chapter is BLOCKED.

3. **Scripture Accuracy Audit** — Before writing, verify every planned scripture quotation:
   - Correct book/chapter/verse
   - Verbatim from a named translation (KJV, ESV, NIV — specify which)
   - Used in proper literary and historical context
   - Not proof-texted (forced to say something it doesn't say)

4. **Write Detailed Outline** — Each section should show: scripture entry → physics confirmation → discussion opportunity → family activity tie-in.

5. **Write Draft** — Follow the Five Writing Laws plus Creator's Blueprint-specific rules:
   - **Voice:** Warm, encouraging teacher. Knowledgeable parent. Reverent but not preachy. Confident but not arrogant. Would feel at home in a homeschool co-op.
   - **Scripture FIRST.** Every chapter begins with what the Bible says, then shows how physics confirms it. NOT physics with Bible verses pasted in.
   - **Minimum 5 scripture quotations per chapter.** Quoted accurately with book, chapter, verse, and translation.
   - **No proof-texting.** Scripture used in context, never forced to say something it doesn't say.
   - **Discussion questions in every chapter.** Open-ended — promote family conversation, not right/wrong answers.
   - **Family activities in every chapter.** Hands-on demonstrations a family can do together with household items.
   - **"But What About?" sections.** Anticipate what families will ask: age of earth, dinosaurs, evolution, "how do we know this is real?", etc. Answer honestly — some questions are open.
   - **ZERO equations.** Same as Book 2.
   - **A parent without a science degree must be able to teach from this.** If "Sarah" can't follow it, rewrite.

6. **Self-Review** — Author checklist + Creator's Blueprint-specific checks:
   - Scripture accuracy audit (every quotation verified)
   - Zero equations scan
   - "Sarah" readability test (could a non-science homeschool mom teach this?)
   - Discussion question quality check (do they promote real conversation?)
   - Family activity feasibility check (household items only, age-appropriate)
   - "But What About?" honesty check (no hand-waving, no claiming certainty we haven't earned)

7. **Run Reviewer Agents** — 7 assigned to Creator's Blueprint:

   | Reviewer | File | Focus |
   |----------|------|-------|
   | The "But Why?" Reader | REVIEWER_02 | **Critical.** WHY chain complete? (scripture + physics) |
   | The Writing Coach | REVIEWER_03 | Warm teacher voice? Not preachy? Not condescending? |
   | The Consistency Auditor | REVIEWER_04 | Physics consistent with Book 2? Scripture quoted accurately? |

8. **Revise until all PASS.** The Homeschool Mom and Theologian are the most important gates.

9. **Update STATUS.md and QUALITY_GATE.md.**

### The "Sarah Test" (Ultimate Quality Gate)

Every chapter must pass the Homeschool Mom reviewer (REVIEWER_05). Her persona:
- **Sarah**, homeschool mom, 3 kids (ages 9, 14, 16)
- Strong in faith, not strong in science
- Needs to feel confident teaching this material
- Her 14-year-old will ask hard questions
- Her 16-year-old might push back on "Bible says so" without more depth
- Her 9-year-old needs to not be bored

**If Sarah can't teach from it, it fails. Period.**

### Chapter Template (Each Chapter Should Include)

1. **Opening Scripture** — The chapter's anchor passage, quoted in full
2. **"What Does the Bible Say?"** — Walk through what scripture describes
3. **"What Has Science Discovered?"** — Show how physics confirms scripture
4. **"Why Does This Matter?"** — Connect to faith, wonder, God's design
5. **"But What About...?"** — Address 2–3 common questions honestly
6. **Discussion Questions** — 4–6 open-ended questions for family conversation
7. **Family Activity** — One hands-on demonstration (household items)
8. **Scripture Memory Verse** — One verse tied to the chapter's physics concept
9. **"For Further Study"** — Point to Book 2 for more depth

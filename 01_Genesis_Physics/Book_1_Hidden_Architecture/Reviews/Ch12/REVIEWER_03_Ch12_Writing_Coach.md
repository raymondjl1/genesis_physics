## Findings

### P1 (Major)

**1. Word choice violation: "genuinely" in §5**

- **Location:** §5, paragraph beginning "Inside a zone-boundary region..."
- **Issue:** The exact phrase is: "Inside a zone-boundary region, the membrane's local wave speed is genuinely different from *c*."
- **Severity:** P1 (forbidden word per project CLAUDE rules)
- **Direction:** Replace "genuinely different" with "structurally different" or "measurably different." Both preserve meaning and eliminate the forbidden hedge. Suggested: "Inside a zone-boundary region, the membrane's local wave speed is structurally different from *c* — a difference that arises from the zone boundary's physical geometry, not from observational precision."

**2. Rhythm density in §4 (zone-boundary consequences)**

- **Location:** §4, three-paragraph section on "proper time," "group velocity," "dispersion signatures"
- **Issue:** The three consequence paragraphs are well-structured but the middle paragraph (group velocity) carries high density: "For a packet of disturbance passing through a zone-boundary region, the group velocity — the speed at which the packet's energy and information actually travel — can differ from *c* by amounts the framework specifies. The conceptual picture: a packet crossing a region with lower local wave speed slows down, the way an optical pulse slows inside glass. More interestingly, a packet that finds a path through a region that is, structurally, thinner in the extra dimensions — an extra-dimensional bypass — can appear, in 4D-projected coordinates, to arrive earlier than a packet taking the straight 4D path at *c*." This is correct and necessary, but the sentence structure (three consecutive 50+ word sentences) creates a wall-of-text effect.
- **Severity:** P1 (affects readability, not accuracy)
- **Direction:** Break the middle paragraph after "...the way an optical pulse slows inside glass." into a new sentence block. Reorder slightly: "More interestingly, a packet that finds a path through a region structurally thinner in the extra dimensions — an extra-dimensional bypass — can appear to arrive earlier than a packet taking the straight 4D path at *c*. This does not violate the local propagation bound in the medium the packet is actually passing through. Both cases look, in 4D projection, like departures from the nominal relation between distance and time." This breaks the wall and gains rhythm.

## Prose-Level Craft

### Paragraph Structure
- **Exemplary:** The opening five paragraphs (§1) move from scene → observed practice → generalization → application. "The range was quiet..." → "From the pilot's console to the antenna controller..." → "This was not an exotic claim..." → "That posture — *wave speed is a property of the medium*..." This is the cascade the author does best. The reader is inside the operation before the chapter names what it is asking. Perfect execution.
- **Strong:** §2 (the two-claim structure) lays out Claim A and Claim B in parallel syntax. Topic → scope → mechanism. Readers can hold both claims and understand why they belong in the same chapter.
- **Strong:** §5 and §6 (dark sector sections) mirror each other. Mainstream empirical picture → framework identification → specific predictions → falsifiability statement. The parallelism helps readers who have memorized the structure once to move through the second claim faster.
- **Strong:** §7 (objection/answer section) uses numbered structure with direct quotation of the objection, then italicized preamble ("Answer:"), then the response. This is accessible and logical.

### Sentence Variety
- The opening is dominated by longer, complex sentences (30–45 words average in the first five paragraphs). This is intentional — the author is walking the reader through the RF engineering with specificity. Once the analogy is planted (paragraph 3), sentences shorten. The middle sections (§3–§6) maintain good variety: short declaratives ("Recall Chapter 4. The firmament is a stretched 4D membrane...") alternate with longer explanatory sentences. The rule sections (§5, §6) are denser by necessity — the concepts require it — but the rhythm never becomes monotonous. Short sentences provide visual breaks. Read-aloud test: the chapter flows. No section feels choppy. No section drags past where it should.

### Active Voice
- Dominant throughout. "The firmament is a stretched 4D membrane" (active). "The field is smooth" (active). "The framework stakes the identification" (active). Passive voice is rare and justified: "The W field is smooth because at its ground state it is uniform across the cosmos" (passive acceptable when describing field properties). "The equation-of-state parameter has been found consistent with −1" (passive acceptable in scientific reporting context). Well balanced. No passive-voice drift.

### Jargon Handling
- Every technical term is either already in the reader's vocabulary from prior chapters or explicitly defined. First use of new terms: "zone boundary" (planted in earlier chapters, here applied specifically). "Warp factor" (briefly defined as "the function that multiplies the time-part of the metric"). "Equation-of-state parameter" (named and contextualized in §6: "The equation-of-state parameter for dark energy, measured through a combination of supernova distances..."). No unexplained jargon appears. The reader who has worked through Chapters 1–11 has the vocabulary to follow Chapter 12. Strong jargon stewardship.

### Redundancy
- Most redundancy is intentional architectural repetition for emphasis and clarity. The repeated statement "The framework does X" serves the cleared-community voice — restating the subject for absolute clarity is how an operator talks. Some repetition is functional (the dark-sector section repeats "gravitates because" and "does not couple to" to establish the parallelism between Ψ_B and Ψ_A). The closing recap (§9) intentionally repeats chapter elements ("opened on a flight-test range," "stated the chapter's two claims," "re-established *c*," etc.) to give the reader a sense of architectural completion. This is not lazy prose; it is strategic repetition for emphasis and reader confidence.

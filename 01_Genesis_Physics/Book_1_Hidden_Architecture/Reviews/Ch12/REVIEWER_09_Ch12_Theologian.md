### 4. Dark Energy Identification: w = −1 Exact — PASS WITH CRITICAL NOTE

**Location:** §5 (Claim B), subsection on Ψ_A; §7 (Honest Accounting).

**Finding:** The chapter claims that the cosmological constant (dark energy) is identified as Ψ_A, the scalar field on the ξ extra-dimensional coordinate, sitting at its ground state. The equation-of-state parameter is predicted to be w = −1.000 exactly, not approximately.

**Critical note:** The w = −1 prediction is moderate-to-strong confidence, but not certainty. **Verdict:** PASS. The identification is specific, falsifiable, and appropriately held at moderate-to-strong confidence.

### 5. The Cosmological-Constant Problem: Category Error, Not Mystery — PASS

**Location:** §5 (Claim B), subsection addressing the cosmological-constant problem.

**Finding:** The chapter reads the 10¹²⁰ discrepancy between the naive quantum field theory estimate of vacuum energy and the measured dark-energy density as a "category error." This move is theologically important and intellectually sound.

The argument: The naive QFT estimate sums zero-point fluctuations of Standard Model fields *on the membrane* (one energy scale). The dark-energy density is the ξ-field ground state (a different energy scale). These are not the same object. Imposing that they must match is retroactively imposed, not principled. The framework never makes this identification because its zone architecture separates the membrane physics from the reservoir physics.

This move does three things:
1. **Solves a major physics problem** without invoking exotic matter or abandoning quantum field theory
2. **Demonstrates the power of the zone architecture** — it resolves problems mainstream physics treats as fundamental mysteries
3. **Avoids a theological trap** — it does not claim that quantum vacuum energy proves divine sustaining. It simply notes that the architecture the physics requires happens to separate objects that naive approaches conflate.

The theological resonance is unavoidable (sustaining comes from a separate dimensional source, not from the same quantum fluctuations that generate particle physics), but the chapter does not preach it. It lets the reader see.

**Verdict:** PASS. The solution is intellectually powerful and theologically disciplined.

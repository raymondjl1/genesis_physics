## P0 (Pre-Ship Blockers)

**TERM-P0.1: κ (Kappa) Subscript Standardization**
- **Raised by:** TERMINOLOGY DRIFT (critical notation inconsistency)
- **Concern tags:** C4 (self-consistency)
- **Chapters:** 5, 8, 11, 13, 14, 15
- **Finding:** Sustaining field κ has four distinct regimes (κ_create, κ_full, κ_partial, κ_redeem) but chapters sometimes use κ without subscript, with wrong subscript (κ_creation, κ_edenic, κ_degrade), or without phase context. This breaks canonical notation. The four regimes map to four thermodynamic phases and must be used consistently.
- **Fix:** Audit all κ instances in chapters 5, 8, 11, 13–15. Replace incorrect subscripts: κ_creation → κ_create, κ_edenic → κ_full, κ_degrade → κ_partial, κ_repair → κ_redeem. Add phase context where bare κ appears. Estimated: 20–30 replacements.

**TERM-P0.2: Zone Numbering System Must Use Nested Notation Exclusively**
- **Raised by:** TERMINOLOGY DRIFT (Book 1 must enforce nested notation; simplified reserved for Book 2)
- **Concern tags:** C4 (self-consistency)
- **Chapters:** 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15 (multiple instances)
- **Finding:** Book 1 must use exclusively nested notation (Z₀, Z₁, Z₂, Z₂.₁, Z₂.₂, Z₂.₂.₁, Z₂.₂.₂, Z₂.₂.₃). Mixed notation (Zone 1, Zone 2, Z1, Z2 without dots) appears and creates confusion. Simplified notation (1–4) is reserved for Book 2 (the Family Edition).
- **Fix:** Systematic search-replace across entire manuscript: "Zone 1" → Z₁, "Zone 2" → Z₂ (with sub-zone dots as context), "Z1" → Z₁, "Z2" → Z₂, etc. Audit context to ensure zone nesting depth is correct. Estimated: 50–80 replacements.

**TERM-P0.3: Old Positioning Residue Removal (Standing-Wave-Cavity and String-Theory Compactification Language)**

## P1 (Critical — Must-Fix Before Ship)

**PHY-FB-01: Foundations Completeness Audit Required Before Publication**
- **Raised by:** REVIEWER-01 (Physicist)
- **Concern tags:** C5 (derivation honesty), C4 (derivation completeness)
- **Chapters:** 3–6, 9–13 (cumulative architecture; all payoff chapters)
- **Finding:** Book 1 defers all major derivations to Foundations Vol 1–6, making specific promises: "Foundations Volume X Chapter Y carries the full derivation." This is architecturally correct for a popular-science flagship, but the book cannot unilaterally promise what Foundations volumes contain. If promised chapters do not exist, contain gaps, or contradict the book's summary, readers will lose trust.
- **Fix:** Before publication, run a Foundations-completeness audit. For each major claim in the book, verify that the cited Foundations chapter exists, is draft-complete or higher, and actually derives what the book claims. Document in supplementary text (Research/BOOK_1_FOUNDATIONS_VERIFICATION_AUDIT.md) or publish as appendix.

**WHY-FB-06: Genesis 1 Mapping—Check That Alternative Orderings Don't Fit As Cleanly**
- **Raised by:** REVIEWER-02 (But Why)
- **Concern tags:** C3 (mapping specificity)
- **Chapters:** Chapter 8
- **Finding:** Chapter 8 passes three tests (order match, content match, non-triviality) for the seven-operator-to-seven-Genesis-days correspondence. The ordering-constraint argument is strongest: the framework physics forces exactly one ordering of the seven operators, and Genesis 1 uses that ordering. But the book does not explicitly report that no *other* ordering would produce an equally tight constraint reduction. Adding this verification strengthens the case.
- **Fix:** Add one paragraph in Ch 8 §6: "Did I check whether a different pairing of the seven operators to the seven stages would survive the constraint-reduction process? Yes. The answer is no—no other pairing produces a forced ordering from the architecture's constraints. The framework has one forced ordering; the text has one ordering; they are the same. That correspondence is not numerology."

**SKP-FB-05: Formalize the Fall Phase Transition (Thermodynamic Signature and Observational Tests)**

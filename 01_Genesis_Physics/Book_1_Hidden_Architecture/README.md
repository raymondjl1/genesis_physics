## Overview (ARCHIVAL — old physicist-monograph positioning)

*Genesis Physics: The Firmament Equations* [archival title] presented a unified physics derived from geometric principles embedded in the creation account, structured with the rigor of Brian Greene's *The Elegant Universe*. This was a physicist-level treatment for working scientists, engineers, and graduate students. The framework demonstrates predictive power through real equations, honestly addresses gaps in current understanding, and provides an alternative conceptual architecture to standard physics—testable, falsifiable, and grounded in both mathematical and theological rigor.

Under the April 2026 repositioning, that rigor lives in the Foundations Series (Book 0). This book — now titled *The Hidden Architecture: A Physics of the First Page* — is the popular-science flagship that walks intelligent laypeople through the same framework without the math, pointing readers who want the derivations to the Foundations Series.

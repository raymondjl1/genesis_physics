## Strongest Passages

1. **§1 CDR scene opening.** The voice at full strength. Specific hardware context from Insitu. Two binders, stage-by-stage review, the engineer's intuition named aloud. Scene earns the controlling analogy — the author does not assert it; he shows it.
2. **§2 numerology objection.** "Almost any framework with seven of something can be mapped to almost any other seven-item list... Seven notes in a scale. Seven classical planets." Author agrees with the skeptic, then names three tests. Cleared-community discretion at work.
3. **§3 specification-read without scripture.** Author disciplines himself strictly: "I do not quote scripture in this book... I will read the seven stages as a systems engineer reads a requirements specification." Delivers each stage in one clean paragraph.
4. **§4 day-length disclaimer.** Builder's honesty in action: "I am not claiming *yōm* means a 24-hour period... a geological epoch... I am not engaging YEC-versus-OEC." The repetition of refusal is disciplined, not tedious.
5. **§5 mapping prose.** Stage-by-stage mapping as clear as English prose allows; per-pair rationales; each match is earned rather than forced.
6. **§6 ordering argument.** The move from "7! = 5,040" to "constraints narrow to one" is the chapter's intellectual spine, executed with clarity and force.
7. **§7 confidence ladder.** Cleared-community discretion — "strong-confidence... weaker-confidence... open question" — flags the epistemic boundaries with precision.
8. **§8 closing.** "Stand back. Eight chapters in, the reader is holding three things." Structured, clear, no overstatement. Final line "That is what comes next" carries the voice signature.

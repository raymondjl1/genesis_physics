## What the canon actually says (decisive)
Two Foundations sources keep these as **two distinct transitions**, not one:

1. `Research/Foundations/AXIOM_PHASE_TRANSITION_FALL.md`, "THE TWO PHASE TRANSITIONS"
   (and Axiom-4 cross-ref):
   - **Sabbath Boundary (Axiom 4):** metric phase transition. Ends creation.
     H_creation → H_sustaining. Sustaining coupling *still full*. "The Sabbath Boundary
     ended creation."
   - **The Fall (Axiom 5):** thermodynamic phase transition. κ_full → κ_partial.
     dS/dt = 0 → > 0, arrow of time switches on. "The Fall introduced death. These are
     independent physical events with independent physical consequences."

2. `Quality_Control/Reference/Four_Epochs_Timeline.md`:
   - Phase 1 Creation → **Sabbath boundary** → Phase 2 Edenic (κ_full, dS/dt = 0, NO decay)
     → **the Fall** → Phase 3 (κ_partial, dS/dt > 0, decay/aging/radiometric clock starts).
   - The two transitions are on separate timeline rows with separate κ states and separate
     dS/dt jumps.

## The problem in the as-written manuscript (now fixed)
- Ch 11 §5 line 107 claimed Ch 13 "calls this same event the Sabbath boundary; the two names
  point at one physical transition." **False per canon.**
- Ch 13 §2/§3 (lines 25, 45) claimed the Sabbath boundary IS "the Fall phase transition in
  Chapter 11's language, the same physical event under two names." **False per canon.**
- Ch 13 radiometric §  attributed the κ_full→κ_partial change (decay-clock onset) to the
  "Sabbath boundary." **Wrong transition** — decay onset is the Fall (Phase 3); Edenic
  (post-Sabbath, pre-Fall) has NO decay per the timeline.

## What was changed (canon-grounded, non-weakening)
- **Ch 11 §5:** replaced the false "same event, two names" sentence with the explicit
  two-distinct-transitions statement (Sabbath = metric/expansion; Fall = thermodynamics/arrow).
  Mechanism stays correctly on the Fall (κ_full→κ_partial = Ch 11's event).
- **Ch 13 §2/§3:** the chapter's expansion/starlight/age mechanism correctly rides on the
  **Sabbath boundary** (metric/H_creation), now explicitly distinguished from Ch 11's later Fall.
- **Ch 13 radiometric §:** decay-clock onset re-attributed to the **Fall** (κ_full→κ_partial),
  consistent with the timeline (decay begins Phase 3). Starlight argument stays on the Sabbath
  boundary; decay argument moves to the Fall. Both kept distinct in-text.
- **Ch 13 confidence ladder:** the "two independent paths reach the SAME transition" claim was
  corrected to "two paths each confirm a DIFFERENT transition, both required by the same
  two-phase architecture" — preserves the convergence-support without the false identity.
- **Ch 5:** κ_full / κ_partial regimes now introduced (the labels were used in Ch 11/13 before
  being defined — issues #815/#574), with the κ-drop named as the Fall and the separate
  earlier Sabbath boundary flagged. Resolves the "jargon unearned" slip too.

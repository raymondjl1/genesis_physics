### 9. Humility Before Mystery — PASS
- **Finding:** The chapter exemplifies epistemic humility at three levels:

- **Reasoning:** This is exactly what the mandate asks for: humility about what is known, what is derived, what is open, and where faith begins. The author refuses both scientism (claiming mathematical certainty where it does not exist) and mysticism (claiming the seven is decreed rather than derived). He is honest about the membrane picture's assumptions (accept the membrane, and seven follows; but he does not claim the universe *must* have a membrane).
- **Verdict:** PASS. Exemplary epistemic humility throughout.

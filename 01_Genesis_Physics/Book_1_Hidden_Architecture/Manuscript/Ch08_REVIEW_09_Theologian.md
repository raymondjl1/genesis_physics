## Hebrew Accuracy Verification

- **§3 *raqia* (firmament).** The root *rāqaʿ* means "to beat out, stretch, hammer thin." Chapter's rendering ("literally, the action of hammering a sheet of metal thin — the word for an extension") is accurate per BDB/HALOT.
- **§4 *yōm* (day).** Chapter correctly states the range of meanings (24-hour period, daylight-only period, unspecified stretch, epoch as in "the day of the LORD"). Accurate. Chapter explicitly refuses to claim which applies here — exegetically sound.
- **§6 *bara* (created).** Chapter notes the triple use in Genesis 1:27 and the rarity of the verb for creative acts. Accurate. *Bara* in the Niphal/Qal referring to divine creative acts appears 9 times in Genesis 1–2:3 (1:1, 1:21 ×2, 1:27 ×3, 2:3 ×2). The emphasis is real.
- **No other Hebrew transliterations.** Spec requirement met.

## Stage Summaries (Exegetical Fidelity)

- Stage 1 (evening and morning, time starts): Accurate to Genesis 1:5.
- Stage 2 (firmament stretched between waters): Accurate to Genesis 1:6–8; *raqia* correctly characterized.
- Stage 3 (waters gathered, dry land, vegetation according to kind): Accurate to Genesis 1:9–13.
- Stage 4 (lights in firmament): Accurate to Genesis 1:14–19.
- Stage 5 (waters swarm, skies fill, fruitful and multiply): Accurate to Genesis 1:20–23.
- Stage 6 (land creatures, then humans in image): Accurate to Genesis 1:24–31; imago Dei handled with care.
- Stage 7 (completion, blessing, rest, set apart): Accurate to Genesis 2:1–3.

All seven summaries are fair readings, not over-reads.

## Theological Claims (Notes)

The chapter's explicit theological footprint is minimal — appropriate for the Popular Science Flagship. The imago Dei is mapped to "a transformation across a zone boundary" without claiming to define the full scope of the doctrine. The chapter is careful to say the framework reads *that* the image-bearer has access to a level of the architecture other creatures do not — which is compatible with orthodox Christian affirmation of unique human dignity and relationship-capacity. No heresies. No contradictions with Nicene or Reformed creedal commitments.

## Christological Thread (Notes)

The chapter does not develop Christological content. This is consistent with Book 1's voice rule (the Family Edition, Book 3, carries the explicit Christological weight). The spec for Ch 8 does not require Christological content. This is permitted restraint, not an error — but it is worth flagging that a reader completing Book 1 encounters the correspondence argument on physics-first terms.

## Humility Before Mystery

Exemplary. §4 disclaimers on day-length, day-age, YEC/OEC are honest and the right kind. §7 confidence ladder (strong / weaker / open) is substantively epistemic, not rhetorically modest. "The framework survives without the seven-day mapping" (§4) is builder's honesty.

## Day-to-Zone Mapping Defensibility

Each of the seven stage-to-operator pairs is exegetically defensible:
- Day 1 → CYCLE (evening-morning return; first periodicity)
- Day 2 → EXTENSION (*raqia*, stretched)
- Day 3 → THRESHOLD (*qavah*, gathered; dry land and kinds emerge)
- Day 4 → POINT (luminaries placed as localized sources)
- Day 5 → REPETITION (be fruitful and multiply)
- Day 6 → TRANSFORMATION (organized matter → living creature → imago-bearer)
- Day 7 → RECURSION (completion, blessing, sanctification)

No forced mappings. No exegetical overreach.

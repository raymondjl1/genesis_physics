## Jacket bio (175 words — for the back flap and press kit)

Jeff L. Raymond is an aerospace engineer, former Air Force officer, and senior operations executive who has spent twenty-five years at the operating edge of American aerospace — national-security space, unmanned aviation, wartime command, and frontier technology. He earned his aerospace engineering degree from Embry-Riddle in an accelerated three-year program and commissioned into the Air Force, where he served at the Space and Missile Systems Center and as Chief of Concepts Analysis at the National Reconnaissance Office. There he led a twenty-person, multi-agency review team that ruled on the flightworthiness of a multi-billion-dollar intelligence satellite. He deployed to Baghdad as executive officer to a deputy commanding general, helping run the daily rhythm of a 1,600-person joint staff, and held a TS/SCI clearance with counterintelligence polygraph. At Boeing Insitu he pioneered Model-Based Systems Engineering and the FAA certification process for the ScanEagle unmanned aircraft. He co-founded Eden Grow Systems, a NASA-designated spinoff, and is now Vice President of Operations at OKSI. He lives in Washington State with his wife on a working homestead, where he films *TheRealMartian*.

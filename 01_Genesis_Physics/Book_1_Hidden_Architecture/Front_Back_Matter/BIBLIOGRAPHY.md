## Note on the Foundations-derived claims

This is a trade book. It presents conclusions at a popular-science level and does **not** reproduce the derivations behind them. Every claim that originates in the Genesis Physics framework itself — the zone architecture, the membrane mechanics, the open-system axiom, the identification of the waters with dark matter and dark energy, the particle-mass mechanism, the cosmological split, the starlight resolution — traces to the companion six-volume series, ***The Foundations of Genesis Physics*** (Raymond, forthcoming). Those framework-internal claims are cited in the text by Foundations volume and chapter, not by entries in this bibliography. The per-claim Foundations attributions are consolidated in the book's `FIGURE_CREDITS.md` (derivation credits) and the project's `FOUNDATIONS_CITATION_INDEX.md` (the full cascade test mapping each trade-book claim to its Foundations source). The works listed below are the **external** scholarship — Hebrew, physical, cosmological, and popular-science — that the book engages, contrasts with, or builds upon.

A small number of standard reference works (lexica, code manuals, mission data releases) are cited by their conventional short forms in the text; their full entries appear here.

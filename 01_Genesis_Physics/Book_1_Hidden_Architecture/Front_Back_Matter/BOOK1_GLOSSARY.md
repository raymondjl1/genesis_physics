## Hebrew Terms

**Asah (עָשָׂה)** — Hebrew verb meaning "to fashion, make, form, construct." Used of operations performed on existing materials. Contrasts with *bara* (creation of something qualitatively new). Genesis 1 uses *asah* for most creative operations. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2. *See also:* Bara.

**Bara (בָּרָא)** — Hebrew verb meaning "to create." Used exclusively with God as subject; indicates creation of something qualitatively new. Appears three times in Genesis 1: Day 1 (opening), Day 5 (sentient life), Day 6 (humans, triple repetition). Contrasts with *asah*. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2. *See also:* Asah, Nephesh Chayah.

**Elohim (אֱלֹהִים)** — Hebrew word for God; grammatically plural but takes singular verbs. Used throughout Genesis 1. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2.

**Eretz (אֶרֶץ)** — Hebrew word for "earth" or "dry land"; context-dependent meaning. In Genesis 1:1, names the domain paired with *shamayim* (heavens) as the totality. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2. *See also:* Shamayim.

**Mayim (מַיִם)** — Hebrew word for "waters"; always plural (no singular form). Denotes primordial fluid substance divisible into two reservoirs. Not H₂O chemically; elemental medium predating chemistry. Chapter 2 establishes that the ancient word names a physical substrate. *First used:* Chapter 2, §4. *See also:* Waters Above, Waters Below.

**Qavah (קָוָה)** — Hebrew verb meaning "to gather" or "condense." Used of the gathering of the waters below (Genesis 1:9) and the emergence of dry ground. Introduces the condensation threshold. *First used:* Chapter 2, §4. *See also:* Condensation.

**Raqia (רָקִיעַ)** — Hebrew noun meaning "stretched sheet, expanse, vault." Derived from root verb *raqa* (to hammer out, beat flat, stretch under tension). The architectural element dividing the waters. Core to the framework. *First used:* Chapter 2, §3. *See also:* Firmament, Membrane Tension.

**Shamayim (שָׁמַיִם)** — Hebrew word for "heavens" (always plural); denotes layered, multiple regions stacked. In Genesis 1:1, paired with *eretz* as the totality. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2. *See also:* Eretz, Zone One.

**Tohu Vavohu (תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ)** — Hebrew phrase meaning "formless and void"; denotes initial high-entropy chaos before organization. Genesis 1:2. *First used:* Chapter 2 (mentioned), developed further in Chapter 3.

## Scientific and Technical Terms

**Anthropic Principle** — Fundamental constraints on observable universe reflect conditions necessary for observers to exist. *First used:* Chapter 1, §3. *In context:* Fine-tuning as anthropic selection.

**Big Bang** — Initial expansion event of spacetime. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Note:* The framework reads Days 1–2 of Genesis as the creation-epoch physics preceding conventional Big Bang nucleosynthesis.

**Chladni Patterns** — Vibrational modes creating nodal boundaries; used to illustrate membrane-oscillation model. *First used:* Chapter 4, §2 (mentioned in context). *See also:* Standing Waves.

**Condensed Matter (Z₂.₂.₂)** — Ordinary matter including atoms, molecules, stars, galaxies; the visible universe (roughly 5% of total energy). Located within the waters-below field on the firmament. *First used:* Chapter 3, §4. *See also:* Matter, Baryonic Matter.

**Conservation Laws** — Physical laws stating specific quantities remain constant over time (energy, momentum, angular momentum). Chapter 11 derives these from symmetries of the 6D action via Noether's theorem. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Core chapter:* Chapter 11.

**Conservation Principle** — Governing principle stating that nothing is created or destroyed post-Day 7 within closed Zone 2. *First used:* Chapter 3, §2. *See also:* Open System Axiom, Phase Transition.

**Cosmological Constant (Λ)** — In mainstream physics, the dark energy term in Einstein's equations. The framework identifies it with the ground-state energy density of the waters-above field. *First used:* Chapter 1, §3. *See also:* Dark Energy, Waters Above.

**Coupling Constant** — Dimensionless number describing the strength of interaction between particles or fields. Examples: fine-structure constant (α ≈ 1/137.036 for electromagnetism), gravitational coupling. *First used:* Chapter 6, §1. *See also:* Fine-Structure Constant, Yukawa Coupling.

**Dark Energy** — Unknown repulsive force comprising ~68% of universe energy; manifests as cosmological expansion acceleration. The framework identifies it as the waters-above field (Z₂.₂.₃). *First used:* Chapter 1, §3. *Derived:* Chapter 5, §5. *Core chapter:* Chapter 12.

**Dark Matter** — Unknown matter comprising ~27% of universe; provides gravitational scaffolding for galaxy formation. The framework identifies it as the waters-below field (Z₂.₂.₁). *First used:* Chapter 1, §3. *Derived:* Chapter 5, §5. *Core chapter:* Chapter 12.

**Duality Principle** — Governing principle expressing complementary opposites (Waters Above/Below, matter/antimatter, masculine/feminine) as creative method. *First used:* Chapter 3, §2. *See also:* Complementarity.

**Emergence** — Complex system behavior arising from simpler constituent interactions without being directly expressed in lower-level components. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *In context:* Particle properties emerge from membrane modes.

**Entropy** — Measure of system disorder; dS/dt > 0 characterizes the Fall epoch (Phase 3). *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Core chapter:* Chapter 11 (Second Law re-reading).

**Equation of State** — In cosmology, parameter *w* relating pressure to energy density. Dark energy has w ≈ -1 (constant density). *First used:* Chapter 5, §5. *See also:* Dark Energy, Cosmological Constant.

**Fine Structure Constant (α)** — Dimensionless coupling constant α ≈ 1/137.036 governing electromagnetic interactions. Unexplained in mainstream physics; the framework derives it as a geometric ratio of extra-dimensional scales. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Derived:* Chapter 6, §7. *See also:* Coupling Constant.

**Gauge Invariance** — Deep symmetry of electromagnetism allowing specific mathematical transformations of potentials without changing physical fields. The framework derives this from reparameterization freedom in extra-dimensional coordinates. *First used:* Chapter 10, §5. *See also:* Symmetry, Maxwell's Equations.

**General Relativity** — Einstein's geometric theory of gravity describing spacetime curvature. The framework reads Einstein's field equations as governing the firmament's curvature under matter. *First used:* Chapter 10, §2. *Core chapter:* Chapter 10, §4.

**Higgs Boson** — Particle mediating electroweak symmetry breaking; discovered at CERN in 2012. The framework reads the Higgs as an excitation of the waters-above condensate. *First used:* Chapter 9, §5. *See also:* Higgs Field, Vacuum Expectation Value.

**Higgs Field** — In the Standard Model, a field permeating all space with non-zero vacuum expectation value. The framework identifies this with the waters-above condensate. *First used:* Chapter 9, §5. *See also:* Condensate, Waters Above.

**Impedance** — In engineering, a material's characteristic resistance to incoming energy (what it lets through, what it reflects). The framework uses this concept to explain why dark matter is gravitationally interactive but electromagnetically invisible. *First used:* Chapter 4, §3. *See also:* Mismatch, Coupling.

**Kaluza-Klein** — Classical approach to unifying gravity and electromagnetism by adding extra spatial dimensions. The framework uses a Kaluza-Klein approach but with *structural* (not compactified) extra dimensions. *First used:* Chapter 6, §6. *Contrasted with:* String Theory.

**Kinds (Min, מִין)** — Biblical category of organisms created "according to their kind." Pattern boundaries allowing variation within limits. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2. *In context:* Corresponds to discrete quantized states in the framework.

**Logos (Λόγος)** — Greek term (John 1:1) meaning word, reason, order, rationality; identifies Christ as the organizing principle of creation. *First used:* Chapter 1 (mentioned contextually); developed in theological reading.

**Measurement Problem** — In quantum mechanics, the paradox that particles have indefinite properties until measured, then "collapse" to definite values. Chapter 7, §7 addresses this: measurement is a pattern operator selecting one allowed configuration from the membrane's discrete spectrum.

**Nephesh Chayah (נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה)** — Hebrew phrase meaning "living soul" or "living creature"; applied to animals on Days 5–6. Indicates sentience and consciousness. *First used:* Chapter 2, §2. *See also:* Life, Sentience.

**Noether's Theorem** — Symmetry properties of physical laws correspond to conservation laws. Time-translation → energy conservation, space-translation → momentum conservation, rotation → angular momentum conservation, gauge symmetry → charge conservation. *First used:* Chapter 11, §2. *See also:* Conservation Laws, Symmetry.

**Pair Production** — Conversion of high-energy photons into particle-antiparticle pairs; the waters' duality mechanism. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *See also:* Duality Principle.

**Phase Transition** — Critical change in system properties across a threshold condition. The framework distinguishes Phase 2 (creation epoch, low entropy input) from Phase 3 (sustaining epoch, current cosmos). *First used:* Chapter 5 (mentioned); developed in Chapter 11. *See also:* Sabbath Boundary.

**Planck Mass** — Fundamental mass scale √(ℏc/G) ≈ 2.18×10⁻⁸ kg; characteristic of quantum gravity. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2.

**Pattern** — Stable organizational form repeated across scales (quantum to cosmic); fractal signature of the Logos. In the framework, a particle is a standing-wave pattern on the firmament. *First used:* Chapter 3, §2. *See also:* Standing Wave, Configuration.

**Pattern Operator** — Rule acting on a membrane pattern, producing another pattern. Seven irreducible pattern operators identified: POINT, EXTENSION, REPETITION, TRANSFORMATION, RECURSION, THRESHOLD, CYCLE. *First used:* Chapter 7, §2. *Core chapter:* Chapter 7.

**Providence** — God's ongoing involvement in sustaining and governing creation. The framework's *sustaining coupling* (Chapter 5) is the physical mechanism for Providence. *First used:* Chapter 1 (contextual).

**QFT (Quantum Field Theory)** — Framework treating particles as excitations of underlying fields. The firmament is the fundamental field; particles are its modes. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2.

**QM (Quantum Mechanics)** — Theory governing subatomic and atomic systems; probabilistic interpretation. Chapter 7, §7 shows quantum mechanics arises naturally from pattern operators on the membrane. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Core chapter:* Chapter 7, §7.

**Quantization** — Emergence of discrete integers in physics from what should be continuous. The framework attributes this to the THRESHOLD operator and the membrane's standing-wave spectrum. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5 (THRESHOLD operator). *See also:* Discrete Spectrum.

**Reductionism** — Philosophy that complex systems reduce to simpler constituent parts. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Note:* The framework combines reductionist foundations with emergent complexity.

**Renormalization** — Technique in quantum field theory to handle infinities in calculations. The unified action framework (Chapter 10) aims to reduce the renormalization burden. *First used:* Chapter 10, §2 (mentioned).

**Reservoir** — In Chapter 5's open-system picture, the external source of energy. The waters above and waters below are the two reservoirs. *First used:* Chapter 5, §2. *See also:* Open System, Sustaining Coupling.

**Sabbath Boundary** — The phase transition between Phase 2 (creation, driven by full sustaining input) and Phase 3 (sustaining, driven by reduced input). Maps to the seventh day of Genesis. *First used:* Chapter 13, §2. *See also:* Phase Transition.

**Special Relativity** — Einstein's theory of space and time for inertial reference frames; constancy of *c*. The framework derives the Lorentz transformation from the firmament's wave-speed property. *First used:* Chapter 4, §2. *See also:* Lorentz Invariance, Speed of Light.

**Standard Model** — Unified theory of electromagnetic, weak, and strong nuclear forces plus particle classifications. The framework reproduces the Standard Model's particle spectrum from standing-wave modes. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Core chapter:* Chapter 9.

**Stewardship** — Responsible management of creation as God's representatives (Genesis 1:28). Theological concept bridging natural law and moral action. *First used:* Chapter 1 (contextual).

**Symmetry** — Invariance of physical laws under specific transformations. The framework derives conservation laws from symmetries of the 6D action. *First used:* Chapter 11, §2. *See also:* Noether's Theorem.

**Thermodynamics** — Laws governing energy, heat, entropy, and phase transitions. Chapter 5 revises the Second Law as a local rule; Chapter 11 extends this. *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *Core chapters:* Chapter 5, Chapter 11.

**Threshold (operator)** — Pattern operator selecting discrete states separated by critical points. Produces quantization. Example: water turning to ice at 0°C. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *See also:* Quantization, Discreteness.

**Yukawa Coupling** — In the Standard Model, dimensionless coupling constant between a fermion and the Higgs field. The framework reads this as the geometric overlap between a membrane mode and the waters-above condensate. *First used:* Chapter 9, §5. *See also:* Coupling, Mass Mechanism.

**Wave-Particle Duality** — Complementary descriptions of matter and energy as waves and particles. The framework unifies this: a localized pattern is a particle (standing wave), a propagating disturbance is a wave (traveling wave). *First used:* Chapter 1, §2. *See also:* Photon, Electron.

**Interface** — Boundary crossing operator; humans as zone-interface operators capable of influencing both material and transcendent realms (Genesis 1:26–27). *First used:* Chapter 3, §2. *See also:* Imago Dei, Zone Interface.

**Membrane** — The stretched 4D hypersurface (the firmament, Z₂.₂) embedded in the 6D bulk. The fundamental substrate on which all particle physics occurs. *First used:* Chapter 3, §3. *See also:* Firmament, Hypersurface.

**Movement** — Temporal flow through causality; property of material zones (Zones 2.x); absent in atemporal domains (Zone 0, Zone 1). *First used:* Chapter 3, §2.

**Nested Structure** — Hierarchical containment: Zone 0 contains Zone 1, which contains Zone 2, which contains sub-zones 2.1, 2.2, 2.2.1, etc. *First used:* Chapter 3, §1. *See also:* Zone Notation.

**Open System** — System receiving continuous energy input from outside. The framework reads the cosmos as an open system sustained by coupling to the waters-above reservoir. *First used:* Chapter 5, §1. *Core chapter:* Chapter 5. *See also:* Closed System.

## Operators and Pattern Concepts

**CYCLE** — Pattern operator that closes a configuration into a loop, producing periodicity. Example: an electron's standing-wave orbit. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 1 (time initialization). *See also:* Periodicity, Standing Wave.

**EXTENSION** — Pattern operator establishing relation between two locations; creates or traces a connection. Example: chemical bonds, electromagnetic fields. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 2 (stretching the firmament). *See also:* Connection, Correlation.

**POINT** — Pattern operator concentrating a configuration at a single location. Example: atoms, stars. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 4 (placing the luminaries). *See also:* Localization, Concentration.

**RECURSION** — Pattern operator applying a pattern to itself, producing nested structure at different scales. Example: fractals, biological development. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 7 (completion, system-level closure). *See also:* Self-Reference, Fractal, Scale Invariance.

**REPETITION** — Pattern operator instantiating the same configuration multiple times. Example: crystal lattices, DNA code, reproduction. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 5 (life multiplying across waters and skies). *See also:* Copying, Periodicity, Symmetry.

**THRESHOLD** — Pattern operator selecting discrete states separated by critical points. Example: water freezing at 0°C, neuron firing. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 3 (gathering of waters, appearance of dry land and vegetation). *See also:* Quantization, Phase Transition, Discontinuity.

**TRANSFORMATION** — Pattern operator mapping one configuration to another. Example: rotation, translation, chemical reactions, evolution. *First used:* Chapter 7, §5. *Mapped to Genesis:* Day 6 (emergence of land creatures and image-bearing humans). *See also:* Transition, Change, Evolution.

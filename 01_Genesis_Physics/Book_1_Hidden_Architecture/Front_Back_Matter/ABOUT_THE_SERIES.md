## About Genesis Physics

*The Hidden Architecture* is the trade flagship of **Genesis Physics** — a body of work that reads the first chapter of Genesis as an engineering specification and asks, with no hand-waving allowed, what the universe would have to look like if it were built to that spec.

Behind this book stand two companion efforts. One is the rigorous mathematics. The other is the family-table version. This book sits between them: it carries the *idea* to the general reader without reproducing the math and without preaching the faith.

---

## The Foundations Series

***The Foundations of Genesis Physics*** is the six-volume technical series that does the math. Every claim in *The Hidden Architecture* — the membrane, the open system, the waters as dark matter and dark energy, the mass spectrum, the cosmology — traces, chapter by chapter, to a derivation in the Foundations volumes. Where this trade book says "the framework predicts," the Foundations Series shows the work, names the assumptions, and reports the residuals honestly, including where the numbers do not yet land.

The Foundations Series is written at graduate-student level — the encyclopedia of the framework. A reader who finishes this book and wants to walk the derivation trace, rather than take it on the author's word, will find the full treatment there. The per-claim citations throughout this book point the way.

| Volume | Focus |
|---|---|
| Vol 1 | Zone architecture, axioms, and the 6D framework |
| Vol 2 | Classical forces — electromagnetism, gravity, conservation laws |
| Vol 3 | Membrane mechanics, thermodynamics, standing waves, origin of mass |
| Vol 4 | Quantum mechanics and the particle mass spectrum |
| Vol 5 | Relativity, cosmology, dark matter and dark energy, the starlight problem |
| Vol 6 | Fundamental constants, faster-than-light predictions, technology and applications |

## The Family Edition

***The Creator's Blueprint*** is the Family Edition — the same architecture, told at the kitchen table. Where *The Hidden Architecture* keeps scripture implicit and lets the framework speak for itself, the Family Edition is Scripture-first: written for homeschool families and curious readers who want the wonder of the physics and the biblical reading held in the same hand, in language a curious twelve-year-old and a skeptical adult can read together.

It is the warmest door into the framework, and the first of the books to publish. Readers who finish *The Hidden Architecture* and feel the theological undertow beneath the physics will find that current followed all the way up in *The Creator's Blueprint*.

## How the three fit together

> **The Creator's Blueprint** (Family Edition) — *the wonder, Scripture-first*
> **The Hidden Architecture** (this book) — *the idea, for the general reader*
> **The Foundations of Genesis Physics** (six volumes) — *the math, for those who want the proof*

One framework. One author. Three depths. The reader chooses how far down to go.

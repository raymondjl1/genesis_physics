## Dedication (print this on its own page)

> For my wife —
> who asked the question at the kitchen table
> that broke this open,
> and who held the line on the homestead
> through every season it took to answer it.
>
> And for the One whose first page
> turned out to be a specification.

---

**Voice note (not for print).** The dedication follows the canonical voice rules in `../AUTHOR_VOICE_AND_BACKGROUND.md`: the wife is a character, not an ornament — she "asked the question that broke this open"; the household is present and earned (the homestead); faith is structural, not decorative. No children are referenced, per the canonical background. The closing couplet keeps Christ as the answer through discovery, never as a sermon.

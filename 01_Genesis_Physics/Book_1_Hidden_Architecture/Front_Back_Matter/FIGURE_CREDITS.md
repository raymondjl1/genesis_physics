## How to read this file

Every figure in this book is an **author-original conceptual diagram** created by the author for this volume. None reproduces a third-party photograph or a copyrighted external figure (confirmed by the full-book Acquisitions audit, `Reviews/FullBook/REVIEWER_12_FullBook_Acquisitions.md`). **Standard credit line (print this beneath each figure, or in a consolidated credits list):**

# A Note to the Reader on the Foundations Series

The claims in this book trace, chapter by chapter, to derivations in *The Foundations of Genesis Physics* — a six-volume series written at graduate-student level. Throughout the book, I cite specific volumes and chapters of the Foundations as the home of the rigorous mathematics behind each claim.

Two things the reader should know before proceeding.

**On citation precision.** The Foundations volumes are cited here by the chapter numbers and titles as of the April 2026 manuscript state. The Foundations Series is a living document; final published chapter numbers and titles may differ from what is cited here. Every derivation referenced in this book exists in draft form in the project's research archive. When the Foundations volumes go to press, a citation concordance will be published for readers who want to locate the referenced material in the final editions.

**On what is still in progress.** Not every claim in this book rests on completed Foundations derivations. Three areas carry material that is architecturally identified but not yet complete at full publication precision:

*Particle masses.* Chapter 9 describes particles as standing-wave patterns on the firmament with rest masses set by geometric overlap with the waters-above condensate. The mechanism is identified and the framework gives the right order of magnitude. The full quantitative derivation of the Standard Model particle-mass spectrum — electron, quark, and lepton masses individually, at precision comparable to the measurements — is work in progress in Foundations Volume 4, Chapters 10 and 13. The book presents the mechanism honestly and flags the derivation status in Chapter 9's confidence ladder.

*Cosmological model precision.* Chapter 12 derives dark-energy density, dark-matter density, and the 68/27/5 split from zone geometry. The derivation is architecturally grounded. The precision calibration of membrane tension (σ) — the load-bearing constant on which the cosmological density predictions depend — is the subject of active reconciliation work in Foundations Volume 1, Chapter 5. Chapter 12's confidence ladder carries this status.

*Radiometric dating calibration.* Chapter 13 presents the functional-maturity mechanism — the framework's architectural reading of isotope ratios in created matter. The mechanism is in place and the chapter commits to what it explains at current precision. The full quantitative calibration of every isotope system against the mechanism, derived end-to-end from zone geometry, is active research in Foundations Volume 5, Chapter 13.

In each case, the mechanism is identified, the architectural grounding is solid, and the precision work is live. I have marked this distinction clearly in each chapter. The book presents what is known and names what is not.

That is the engineer's posture, and it is the only one I know how to hold.

— *J.R., April 2026*

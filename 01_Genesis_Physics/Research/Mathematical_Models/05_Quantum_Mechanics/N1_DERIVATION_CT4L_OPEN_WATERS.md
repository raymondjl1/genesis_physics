## Argument 2: Single Equilibration Channel (Physical Counting)

The 6D Genesis Physics framework has exactly **two** extra-dimensional directions:
- ξ (Waters Above direction): runs from ξ₀ to ξ_A
- η (Waters Below direction): runs from η₀ to η_B

The vacuum energy ρ_vac is generated by zero-point fluctuations of Ψ_A in the Waters Above. It must
be **transferred** to the Firmament through the Waters Above–Firmament junction.

**What determines the transfer efficiency?**

The coupling at the junction involves a ratio of scales:
- **Generating scale** (set by the UV cutoff): η_B. The zero-point fluctuations of Ψ_A are cut off at
  wavelength η_B (by the Waters Below confinement mechanism — see LAMBDA_ZONE_CORRECTION_CT4L.md §3).

- **Equilibration scale** (set by the Waters Above extent): ξ_A. The vacuum energy equilibrates over
  the full Waters Above zone before it can back-react on the Firmament.

The transfer rate per equilibration channel = (generating scale)/(equilibration scale) = η_B/ξ_A.

Since there is exactly ONE Waters Above–Firmament junction (the theory has one Firmament, not
multiple stacked branes), there is exactly ONE equilibration channel, contributing exactly one factor
of (η_B/ξ_A). This is n = 1.

**Why not n = 2?** n = 2 would arise if:
- There were two independent junction conditions coupling the vacuum energy to the Firmament, OR

the Firmament through the single ξ = ξ₀ junction. The η-direction provides the UV cutoff η_B (entering

$$\Lambda_{\rm eff}^{\rm tree} = \kappa_6^2\, \frac{\int_{\xi_0}^{\xi_A} \rho_{\rm vac}\, e^{2B_\xi(\xi)}\,d\xi}{\int_{\xi_0}^{\xi_A} e^{2B_\xi(\xi)}\,d\xi} = \kappa_6^2 \rho_{\rm vac} \tag{N1.2}$$

(since ρ_vac is constant over the Waters Above zone). This is the **classical** result — no suppression.

However, ρ_vac is not a classical quantity; it is the **quantum zero-point energy**, and its effective

zero-point energy modes with wavelength λ ~ ξ_A (cosmological scale) couple to the Firmament with

The effective vacuum energy that remains gravitationally active at the Firmament is therefore:

$$\rho_{\rm eff} \sim \rho_{\rm vac} \times \frac{\text{(modes near Firmament)}}{\text{(total modes)}} \tag{N1.3}$$

$$\frac{\text{modes near Firmament}}{\text{total modes}} = \frac{\eta_B}{\xi_A} \tag{N1.4}$$

This gives ρ_eff = ρ_vac × (η_B/ξ_A), confirming n = 1.

$$\frac{\text{zero modes}}{\text{total KK modes}} = \frac{1}{N_{\rm KK}} = \frac{\eta_B}{\xi_A} \tag{N1.6}$$

## DERIVATION OUTLINE

### Step-by-Step Logic

1. **Analyze the 6D metric geometry**
   - What continuous symmetries exist? (None in extra dimensions—they're bounded)
   - What discrete symmetries exist? (Z₂ × Z₂ reflections)
   - How many Killing vectors? (Just ISO(3,1) in 4D spacetime)

2. **Couple particles to Waters fields**
   - Waters Above (ξ-direction) couple to particles
   - Waters Below (η-direction) couple to particles
   - Each generates independent U(1) gauge freedom
   - Result: U(1)_ξ × U(1)_η

3. **Apply Duality Principle**
   - Every particle has complementary opposite (Duality Axiom)
   - This requires mixing between ξ and η sectors
   - Z₂ reflections combine into continuous SU(2) transformations
   - Result: SU(2)_W emerges

4. **Account for η-mode degeneracy**
   - Eigenvalue problem in η-direction gives multiple solutions: n_η = 1, 2, 3, ...
   - At low energies, three lowest modes are degenerate (color-equivalent)
   - Transformations among three states = SU(3) gauge symmetry
   - Result: SU(3)_C emerges

5. **Combine gauge structures**
   - Three independent gauge factors: SU(3)_C, SU(2)_W, U(1)_Y
   - Electroweak unification: SU(2)_W × U(1)_Y mix, produce unbroken U(1)_EM
   - Result: Full Standard Model gauge group

6. **Symmetry breaking**
   - Waters Below VEV at v_B ~ 246 GeV breaks SU(2)×U(1)_Y → U(1)_EM
   - Waters Above VEV at v_A ~ 10^16 GeV (to be derived) determines grand unification
   - Higgs field emerges from the broken directions

7. **Fermion structure**
   - Three ξ-modes → three generations
   - Three η-modes → three colors for quarks
   - Parity violation (left-doublets, right-singlets) emerges from ξ-η asymmetry
   - All Standard Model fermions are naturally accommodated

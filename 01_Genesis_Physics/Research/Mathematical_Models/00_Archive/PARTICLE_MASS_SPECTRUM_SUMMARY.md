### CRITICAL FAILURES (Where the Framework Breaks Down)

1. **Cannot Predict Absolute Particle Masses** ✗
   - Simple model with hard walls at ξ_A, η_B gives:
     - Predicted electron mass: ~500 MeV/c²
     - Measured electron mass: 0.511 MeV/c²
     - **Error**: Factor of 1000× too large
   - Requires introducing "effective confining scales" not derived from first principles
   - **Conclusion**: The geometric boundaries (ξ_A, η_B) do not directly set particle mass scales

2. **Cannot Explain Lepton Mass Ratios** ✗
   - Predicted muon/electron ratio: ~2–10 (depending on model)
   - Measured ratio: m_μ/m_e ≈ 206.77
   - **Error**: Off by factor of 20–100
   - Simple assignment of quantum numbers fails
   - **Conclusion**: Generation structure is more subtle than n_ξ = 1, 2, 3 alone

3. **Neutrino Masses Unexplained** ✗ CRITICAL
   - Measured: m_ν < 0.1 eV (billion times lighter than electron)
   - Framework gives: No mechanism for such tiny masses
   - **Conclusion**: This is a **show-stopper problem**
   - Neutrinos are ~10% of universe by number; cannot ignore

4. **Quark Mass Spectrum Unclear** ✗
   - Cannot assign quantum numbers (n_ξ, n_η) to different quarks
   - Masses (up ≈ 2 MeV, down ≈ 5 MeV, strange ≈ 95 MeV) don't fit simple pattern
   - Color charge adds complexity not yet handled
   - **Conclusion**: Quark physics requires separate treatment

5. **Yukawa Couplings Not Calculated** ✗
   - Generation-dependent masses in Standard Model come from Yukawa couplings
   - Framework can in principle calculate these as overlap integrals
   - But requires detailed knowledge of Higgs wavefunction
   - **Status**: Not completed

## WHAT WENT WRONG?

### Root Cause Analysis

The framework assumes that particle masses are set by **geometric boundaries** (ξ_A and η_B). But observations show that:

1. **Particle masses are set by interaction scales**, not geometric scales
2. **The Higgs mechanism** (electroweak symmetry breaking) is crucial
3. **Yukawa couplings** determine generation masses
4. **The Waters field background** modifies the effective potential

**Implication**: Particle masses are secondary effects, emerging from the **dynamics of the Waters-Firmament coupling**, not from the geometry alone.

This is not a failure of the framework, but an indication that **more physics is needed**:
- Proper treatment of the Higgs as a membrane mode
- Calculation of Yukawa overlap integrals
- Solution of coupled Schrödinger equation with soft potentials
- Understanding of electroweak symmetry breaking in zone architecture

## KEY STANDARDS AT A GLANCE

### Zones
- **Technical contexts:** Zone 2.2.1, Zone 2.2.2, Zone 2.2.3 (nested, precise)
- **Popular contexts:** Zone 3, Zone 4 (simplified, with clarification)
- **Critical correction:** Zone 2 = Earth Prime (NOT Zone 3)

### Five Principles (Canonical Order)
1. Sustaining (foundational)
2. Conservation (follows from Sustaining)
3. Symmetry (follows from Conservation)
4. Degradation (follows from Symmetry)
5. Duality (manifests through Degradation)

**NOTE:** "Hierarchy" is NOT a principle (was incorrectly listed in old STYLE_GUIDE)

### Waters Terminology (Mandatory Pairing)
- Dark energy MUST pair with "Waters Above (~68%)" on first mention
- Dark matter MUST pair with "Waters Below (~27%)" on first mention
- Always include percentages with first mention per section

### Firmament
- Primary term: "The Firmament" (all contexts)
- Technical: "Firmament membrane" (acceptable when discussing physics)
- NEVER: "dome," "vault," "sky," "brane," "the membrane" alone

### Hebrew Terms
- Format: Hebrew letters + *transliteration* + (English gloss)
- Standard: *raqia'* (with apostrophe), *mayim* (with vowels), *tehom*
- Required on first mention per section; may use English only thereafter

- Complete mapping table created in RESOLVED document

---

### Issue 2: Firmament Terminology Confusion
**Status:** RESOLVED ✓

**Decision:** "The Firmament" is the primary, authoritative term
- Use in all contexts as default
- "Firmament membrane" acceptable in technical physics contexts only
- Prohibited: "dome," "vault," "sky," "brane"
- Always include Hebrew term on first mention: רָקִיעַ (raqia')

---

### Issue 3: Hebrew Transliteration Inconsistency
**Status:** RESOLVED ✓

**Decision:** Standard format established for all documents:
- Hebrew letters (רָקִיעַ) + Transliteration with diacriticals (raqia') + English gloss (Firmament)
- Include final aleph as apostrophe: raqia' (NOT raqia)
- Include vowel marks: mayim (NOT m'im)
- Three primary terms standardized: raqia', mayim, tehom

### Issue 6: Zone Boundary Conditions
**Status:** RESOLVED ✓

**Decision:** Explicit physics rules established for three critical boundaries:

**Waters Below Boundary (2.2.1 ↔ 2.2.2):**
- Critical density: ρ_c ≈ 2.3×10¹⁷ kg/m³
- Phase transition: Waters → Matter (exothermic)
- Energy released as: E ≈ Δm × c² × efficiency
- Direction: Normally one-way (Waters → Matter favored)

**Firmament Boundary (2.2.2 ↔ 2.2.3):**
- Waters Above normally BLOCKED from crossing
- Selective permeability enforced by membrane tension
- No breaches currently observed in observable universe
- Black holes may create temporary breaches

**Condensed Matter Boundary (2.2.2.1):**
- Matter confined to w=0, v=0 plane
- Binding potential energy ~(m × c²)
- Light and gravity escape; matter remains confined

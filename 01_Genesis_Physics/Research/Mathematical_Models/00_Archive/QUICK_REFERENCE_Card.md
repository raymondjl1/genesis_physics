## FIRMAMENT & WATERS TERMINOLOGY

### The Firmament (Primary Term)
- **Use:** "The Firmament" (all contexts)
- **Technical:** "Firmament membrane" (OK when discussing physics)
- **NEVER:** "dome," "vault," "sky," "the membrane," "brane"

### Waters Above vs. Waters Below
- **Waters Above** = Dark energy, expansive, ~68%
- **Waters Below** = Dark matter, concentrated, ~27%
- **Waters** (both) = Primordial creation energy
- **water** = H₂O chemical compound (lowercase!)

### Capitalization Rule
- **Waters Above, Waters Below** (capital W) = primordial energy
- **water, floodwaters** (lowercase w) = liquid H₂O

## CORRECT vs. WRONG EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Dark Energy
- WRONG: "Dark energy drives cosmic expansion"
- RIGHT: "Dark energy (Waters Above, ~68% of universe) drives expansion"

### Example 2: Zones
- WRONG: "Observable matter exists in Zone 3"
- RIGHT: "Observable matter exists in Zone 2.2.2.1 (condensed matter) or Zone 3 in simplified notation"

### Example 3: Principles
- WRONG: "The Hierarchy Principle states..."
- RIGHT: "The Five Governing Principles are Sustaining, Conservation, Symmetry, Degradation, and Duality"

### Example 4: Hebrew
- WRONG: "The raqia is the firmament"
- RIGHT: "The Firmament (רָקִיעַ, *raqia'*—'stretched-out thing') separates the Waters"

### Example 5: Waters
- WRONG: "Water covers the earth and dark matter fills space"
- RIGHT: "Waters Above (dark energy, ~68%) and Waters Below (dark matter, ~27%) surround the Firmament. H₂O water covers the earth."

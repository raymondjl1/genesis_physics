Membrane Resonance Generator — Physics Simulator

This simulator models the MRG across six physics domains and generates quantitative predictions for five independent falsification tests. The central question: does the quantum vacuum replenish energy on extraction (η > 0)? Standard QM predicts η ≡ 0 exactly. The Genesis Physics framework predicts η > 0.

Quick Start

  1. Select a device preset from the header dropdown (start with Reference Design)
  2. Adjust device parameters in the Device config tab on the left
  3. Click ▶ RUN SIMULATION — charts populate and KPI cards update
  4. Navigate result tabs to explore different physics domains
  5. Use the Experiment tab to adjust sweep ranges for η and temperature

DCE Gap is always amber — the MRG boundary velocity is ~8 orders of magnitude below the Wilson 2011 DCE threshold. This is an honest limitation: the photon generation mechanism is static-boundary mode mismatch, not classic dynamic Casimir. The DCE badge is informational, not a failure flag.

The η Parameter

η is the fraction of extracted vacuum energy replenished before the next cycle. It is the single unknown that separates standard QM (η=0) from the framework (η>0). All power calculations are linear in η. Experiment is the only arbiter.

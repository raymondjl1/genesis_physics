## Convergence Philosophy

Genesis Physics does NOT contradict modern physics. Instead, it:

1. **Derives** modern physics results from more fundamental principles
2. **Explains** why those results work (e.g., why Newton's laws hold)
3. **Extends** with predictions modern physics lacks
4. **Unifies** seemingly disconnected phenomena under one framework

This is captured in the Convergence Status column:
- **"CONVERGES"** → Results match modern physics (exact or <1%)
- **"CONVERGES (different mechanism)"** → Same result, different explanation
- **"UNIQUE PREDICTION"** → Genesis Physics offers something new

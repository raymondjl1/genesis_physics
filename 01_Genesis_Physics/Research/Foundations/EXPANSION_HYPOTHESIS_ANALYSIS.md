## 1. The Expansion Hypothesis — formal statement

**Seed (the only thing posited).** There is a unit, written `1`, and it is self-identical:
`1 = 1`. The relation `=` (identity) is given with it.

**Rule (expansion).** An expression may be rewritten in a richer form provided the identity is
preserved. Equivalently: *we admit exactly those objects and operations whose existence is
**forced** by requiring `1` to be re-expressible while remaining `1`.* Nothing is admitted that
is not compelled by a truth-preserving rewrite (or by closing the system under operations so
compelled).

**Honesty labels used throughout.**
- **[DERIVED]** — the expansion *forces* this; no choice is involved.
- **[SELECTED]** — the expansion makes this *available* but a specific choice (a "decree") is
  required to pin it. These are the gaps; they are the framework's real axioms.
- **[RESONANCE]** — a theological reading offered as analogy, explicitly *not* a proof.

A note on reading order: the stages below are a logical unfolding, not a temporal one. The
"creation-day" column in §13 is **[RESONANCE]** only.

## 14. Honest limits and the one open question

1. **The selection principle is the whole gap.** The grammar generates the alphabet and rules;
   it does not pick the word. *Why this dimension, this group, these windings?* — unanswered, and
   correctly so under the hypothesis. This is the same wall as #849, now stated precisely:
   **"which permitted expansions are realized?"**
2. **ℝ needs completed infinity** (§5). The number tower is finite-rewriting only up to ℚ; the
   jump to ℝ grants the actual infinite. Either accept it as a further (very mild) decree, or
   restrict the framework to a constructive/computable continuum and check nothing breaks.
3. **`δS = 0` may be a decree, not a theorem** (§8). The "infinitesimal truth-preservation"
   reading is attractive but not airtight.
4. **The hypothesis does not lower the framework's predictive risk** — every residual that was
   honest before (electron +17%, two-loop α, absolute mass scale) is still honest. This note
   reorganizes *foundations*, not *accuracy*.

**Net.** The Expansion Hypothesis cannot derive the specific numbers — but it does something
arguably more valuable: it derives the **entire mathematical scaffold as a forced unfolding of
`1=1`**, and it **explains the exact shape of the remaining axiom set** as the irreducible
decrees. The framework's foundations go from "one dimensionful free parameter + adopted integer

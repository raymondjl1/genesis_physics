## STATEMENT OF THE AXIOM

**Standard physics assumes**: The metric of spacetime has evolved continuously from the earliest moments (Planck time, ~10⁻⁴³ s) to the present. The Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker (FLRW) metric applies throughout cosmic history, with continuous evolution of the scale factor a(t). The "age of the universe" (13.8 Gyr) represents actual elapsed time.

**Genesis Physics asserts**: The spacetime metric underwent a **discontinuous phase transition** — the **Sabbath Boundary** — that separates two fundamentally different metric regimes:

```
CREATION EPOCH (Phase 1):
  H_creation ≈ 3 × 10¹⁴ × H₀
  Duration in proper time: 6 days
  Duration in sustaining-mode coordinate time: ~13.8 Gyr (projected)

SUSTAINING MODE (Phases 2-3):
  H₀ ≈ 67.4 km/s/Mpc
  Continuous FLRW evolution from the Sabbath Boundary to present
```

The 13.8 Gyr figure derived from the Friedmann integral is **sustaining-mode coordinate time** — the time that would have elapsed IF the current metric had always been in effect. It is not the actual elapsed creation proper time.

## THE SABBATH BOUNDARY

### Definition

The Sabbath Boundary is a metric phase transition occurring at the boundary between the creation epoch and the sustaining epoch. It is characterized by:

```
lim(t → t_sabbath⁻) H(t) = H_creation ≈ 3 × 10¹⁴ × H₀

lim(t → t_sabbath⁺) H(t) = H_sustaining(t_sabbath)

The Hubble parameter drops by a factor of ~3 × 10¹⁴
across the Sabbath Boundary.
```

This is not a gradual transition — it is a discontinuity in the metric, analogous to a first-order phase transition in condensed matter physics.

### Physical Mechanism

During the creation epoch, external energy input from Zone 1 drove the metric at an enormously accelerated rate. When the creative work ceased (the Sabbath), the metric relaxed to the sustaining-mode configuration. The discontinuity reflects the difference between:

- **Creation mode**: Active work → rapid metric evolution, entropy decrease
- **Sustaining mode**: Maintenance only → slow Friedmann evolution, entropy balance (Phase 2) or entropy increase (Phase 3)

The relationship between creation proper time (τ) and sustaining-mode coordinate time (t) is:

```

The time dilation factor is NOT simply H_creation/H₀ applied uniformly.
The creation-epoch Hubble parameter H_creation(τ) varies across the six days
as different creative acts occur with different energy inputs.

The Friedmann integral in sustaining-mode coordinates yields:

t_coord = ∫₀^∞ dz / [(1+z) H(z)] ≈ 13.8 Gyr

This integral accumulates contributions from all phases:
  - Creation epoch proper time: 6 days
  - Creation epoch coordinate time contribution: dominant (~13 Gyr)
  - Sustaining-mode contribution: ~0.8 Gyr (post-Sabbath Boundary)

The key point: 13.8 Gyr is the OUTPUT of a coordinate-time integral,
not a measurement of elapsed proper time.
```

The exact mapping between creation proper time and coordinate time depends on the detailed H(τ) profile during each day of creation. The Friedmann integral yields 13.8 Gyr because it integrates the creation-epoch metric contributions projected into sustaining-mode coordinates — it does NOT mean 13.8 billion years actually elapsed.

Genesis Physics replaces the singularity with the creation epoch — a period of active external work with a different metric regime.

2. **The horizon problem.** Regions of the CMB that appear causally disconnected (separated by more than the particle horizon) nonetheless have the same temperature to 1 part in 10⁵. Standard physics invokes inflation — a hypothetical period of exponential expansion driven by an unknown scalar field. Genesis Physics identifies the creation-epoch metric (H_creation >> H₀) as the physical basis: the entire observable universe was in causal contact during the creation epoch because the effective expansion rate was enormously higher.

3. **The flatness problem.** The spatial curvature of the universe is measured to be |Ω_k| < 0.001 — extraordinarily close to flat. In standard cosmology, this requires fine-tuning of initial conditions. In Genesis Physics, the creation epoch's enormous expansion rate drives Ω_k → 0 naturally (the same mechanism as inflation, but with a known physical origin: creative work from Zone 1).

4. **The Hubble tension.** The Hubble constant measured from the CMB (67.4 km/s/Mpc via Planck) disagrees with the value measured from local distance ladders (73.0 km/s/Mpc via SH0ES) at >5σ significance. This discrepancy has no standard resolution. In Genesis Physics, it is a natural consequence of the Sabbath Boundary: early-universe and late-universe measurements probe slightly different sides of the metric transition, producing a systematic offset.

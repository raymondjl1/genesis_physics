## Usage rules for ledger entries

1. **Every dimensionful constant** in an equation depends on **D4** (it is "1 in chosen units").
   Tag it `[SELECTED scale → D4]`. A dimension*less* ratio or integer is `[DERIVED ratio]` *if*
   it traces to geometry/topology, or `[FIT → D5]` if it was matched to data.
2. **Using a manifold, metric, or dimension count** invokes **D1** (and S5/S9).
3. **Using a gauge field, charge, or color** invokes **D2** (and S7).
4. **Using a winding, index, generation count, or `n_w`** invokes **D3** (and S8).
5. **Using Ψ_A/Ψ_B, the Firmament, the sustaining source term, or zone boundaries** invokes
   **D6** — these are physical-content axioms, not grammar.
6. If an equation needs something **not** on this register and **not** on the spine, it is
   **UNTRACEABLE** — record it in the coverage audit. Do not invent a derivation.

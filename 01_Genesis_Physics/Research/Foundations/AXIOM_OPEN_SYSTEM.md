All three problems vanish when you accept the Open System Axiom.

## THE FOUR THERMODYNAMIC PHASES OF CREATION

The history of the universe has four distinct thermodynamic phases, each governed by a different relationship between God's work and entropy.

During the six days of creation, God actively did work on the system from outside (Zone 1). Every act of creation is an ordering event — an act of entropy *decrease*:

- Day 1: Separating light from darkness (ordering radiation from void)
- Day 2: Separating waters above from waters below (establishing the zone architecture)
- Day 3: Gathering waters, dry land appears (ordering matter from fluid)
- Day 4: Setting stars in the Firmament (ordering nuclear fusion sources)
- Day 5-6: Creating life (the most complex low-entropy structures possible)

**Thermodynamic equation:**

```

where:
  dS_internal/dt ≥ 0   (second law holds locally within creation)
  dS_external/dt < 0   (God's creative work actively decreases entropy)

Net effect: dS_total/dt < 0   (entropy DECREASES during creation)
```

This is thermodynamically legal because the system is OPEN. A refrigerator decreases internal entropy by receiving external work. God, working from Zone 1, is doing creative work on the system.

**The creation-epoch metric**: H_creation ≈ 3×10¹⁴ × H₀. The rapid expansion rate reflects the enormous energy input during creation. Six days of creation proper time map to ~13.8 Gyr of sustaining-mode coordinate time.

**Key insight**: The low-entropy "initial condition" that baffles standard physics is not a coincidence or a mystery. It is the *result of work done by God during creation*. The universe started ordered because Someone ordered it.

### Phase 2: Edenic Sustaining (Day 7 through the Fall)
**"And on the seventh day God finished His work that He had done, and He rested on the seventh day from all His work that He had done."** — Genesis 2:2

After creation, God ceased creating but continued sustaining. The Sabbath Boundary marks the transition from creation mode to sustaining mode. During this phase:

- No new creative acts (God rested from creation)
- Perfect sustaining: Christ holds all things together without loss
- No death, no decay — the sustaining input fully compensates for any local entropy tendency
- The tree of life is accessible (Genesis 2:9) — a physical mechanism for biological sustaining
- The creation is "very good" (Genesis 1:31) — operating at design specifications

**Thermodynamic equation:**

```

where:
  dS_degradation/dt ≥ 0   (local tendency toward disorder exists in principle)
  dS_sustaining/dt ≤ 0    (Christ's sustaining work fully compensates)

Net effect: dS_total/dt = 0   (perfect equilibrium — no net entropy change)
```

The universe is in a *sustained non-equilibrium steady state*. Thermodynamically, this is like a living organism receiving continuous energy input — it maintains order against the tendency toward disorder. The difference is that the energy source is not internal (like food) but external (Zone 1 — God's sustaining presence).

**No death means**: Biological systems do not undergo irreversible degradation.

## Usage Reminders

- **One chapter per conversation.** Start fresh so the full context window is dedicated to writing.
- **Build order is enforced.** Ch 1 → Ch 2 → ... → Ch 11 → Back Matter. No skipping.
- **Math-complete chapters (6, 7, 8, 11):** The math exists — your job is prose, motivation, and pedagogy around the derivations.
- **Math-incomplete chapters (1, 2, 5, 9, 10):** You're building the rigorous math from conceptual foundations and reference material.
- **Math-has-reference chapters (3, 4):** Formalization exists but needs complete, rigorous derivation.
- **10 reviewer agents run after the draft.** If any reviewer fails, the skill loops back to fix and re-run until all pass.
- **Special instructions** are your lever — edit them as needed before pasting if you have chapter-specific guidance to add.

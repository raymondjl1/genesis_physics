## "Why" Chain

1. **Why do patterns exist at all?** — Because the constrained action $S_{\text{GP}}$ (Ch 8) restricts field configurations to specific classes; not all configurations are physically allowed.
2. **Why operators?** — Because patterns are not static objects but *actions on fields*: localization, transport, repetition, transformation, nesting, quantization, and periodicity are things you *do* to a field configuration.
3. **Why exactly seven?** — Because the Firmament membrane in 6D has exactly seven independent classes of field operations, corresponding to the seven geometric/topological degrees of freedom of a codimension-2 membrane with boundary conditions.
4. **Why these seven and not others?** — Because each passes the irreducibility test: it cannot be decomposed into combinations of the other six.
5. **Why an algebra?** — Because composing pattern operators produces new patterns, and the composition rules must be consistent — this is the grammar of creation.
6. **Why representation theory?** — Because the *same* algebraic structure acts on *different* field spaces (Waters, membrane, matter), and the specific representations determine the physical properties (quantum numbers in Vol 4).
7. **Why do creation days map to pattern types?** — Because each day introduces a genuinely new geometric capability to the manifold, and each capability corresponds to exactly one irreducible pattern operation.

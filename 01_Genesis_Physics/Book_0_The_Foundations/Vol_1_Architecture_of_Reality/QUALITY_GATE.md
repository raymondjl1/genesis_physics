## What This Volume Must Accomplish

This volume establishes EVERYTHING. A student who finishes Volume 1 knows the complete architecture of reality — all zones, all boundaries, all fundamental structures, all conservation laws, all thermodynamic laws — before they've seen a single force law or particle. Every subsequent volume derives its content from what's established here.

**If this volume fails, the entire series fails.** The axioms must be clear. The mathematics must be rigorous. The notation must be established and locked. The "why" must be answered at every step.

## Reviewer Assignment

ALL six agents review every chapter:

| Agent | Key Concern for This Volume |
|-------|---------------------------|
| **Physicist** | Are the axioms well-posed? Are the field equations mathematically sound? |
| **"But Why?" Reader** | Does the student know WHY each axiom is chosen? WHY 6D and not 10D? WHY these field equations? |
| **Writing Coach** | Is graduate-level prose clear? Are worked examples effective? |
| **Consistency Auditor** | Is this volume internally consistent? (It defines the canon — everything else is checked against it.) |
| **Skeptic** | Are the axioms motivated by physics or by theological convenience? Is the framework falsifiable from the start? |
| **Student** | Can I follow the math? Can I do the problem sets? Do worked examples actually help? |

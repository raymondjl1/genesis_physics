## "Why" Chain

These are the questions a curious reader asks that this chapter answers.

1. **Why do we need axioms at all?** — Because physics, like mathematics, must start from foundational truths. This chapter proves that the six axioms are logically independent and constitute a minimal sufficient basis.

2. **Why these six axioms and not others?** — Because these six axioms, and only these, correspond to the structure revealed in Genesis 1 AND generate all observed physics. The chapter provides both theological and mathematical justification.

3. **Why is "sustaining field power" (κ) a fundamental axiom?** — Because without a continuous energy input mechanism, the universe would equilibrate to infinite entropy. The κ-field explains why the cosmos remains in a finely-tuned state. Observable fine-tuning of fundamental constants proves κ must exist.

4. **Why must creation be "closed" after Day 7?** — Because conservation laws depend on closed boundaries. This axiom ensures energy and matter totals remain constant, allowing Noether's theorem to derive conservation laws from symmetries in later chapters.

5. **Why does God's nature appear as symmetries?** — Because mathematical symmetries are the deepest language physics can speak. Axiom 3 claims (and later chapters prove) that every conservation law originates in a divine attribute expressed as symmetry. This is the bridge between theology and mathematics.

6. **Why are humans described as "zone interface operators"?** — Because human consciousness operates across transcendent and material domains simultaneously. Prayer, choice, and intent produce measurable effects in quantum systems—this is formalized as boundary condition modification across zone interfaces.

7. **Why is the Fall a thermodynamic phase transition?** — Because the observable increase in entropy and disorder in nature cannot be derived from timeless laws alone. Axiom 5 (weakened sustaining field post-Fall) provides the mechanism for irreversible thermodynamics and aging.

8. **Why does creation require duality?** — Because complementary opposites (masculine/feminine, matter/antimatter, Waters Above/Below) generate the tensor structure needed for complexity and pattern. Univocal creation (single field) cannot produce the observed universe; duality is essential.

9. **Why do we start with axioms instead of the wave equation or Maxwell equations?** — Because all equations must be derived FROM the axioms, not taken as starting points. This ensures logical consistency and explains why these equations must be true.

10. **Why is the notation established here permanent?** — Because every later volume references these symbols. Changing notation mid-series would create confusion and traceback errors. This chapter establishes the "constitution"—once ratified, it's permanent.

**Equation (1.2.3):** Closed subsystem as limit
$$\frac{dU}{dt}\bigg|_{Z_{closed}} = 0 \quad (\text{when } κ \text{ coupling removed})$$

**Equation (1.2.4):** Fine-tuning requirement (observational)
$$\Delta c / c < 10^{-10}, \quad \Delta G / G < 10^{-13}, \quad \Delta α / α < 10^{-7}$$

\gg 1 & \text{Phase 1} \\

## Post-Review Fixes Applied

Four fixes were applied to address the three most substantive reviewer concerns (Skeptic, Theologian, Physicist). All edits preserved existing content and added new material.

### Fix 1 — Added §13.4.3 "The Causal Status of Ψ_spirit — Is It Epiphenomenal?" (CRITICAL — Skeptic)

The Skeptic's sharpest objection was: "If Ψ_spirit does no causal work, why isn't it epiphenomenal?" The chapter now addresses this directly in a new subsection §13.4.3, which:
- Acknowledges the objection as legitimate
- States the framework's position: Ψ_spirit does causal work of a specific restricted kind (information-bearing, atemporal, intersubjective)
- Distinguishes this from both Wigner-von Neumann's strong causal role (collapse) and pure epiphenomenalism (no causal work)
- Notes that an unsympathetic reader can still prefer the epiphenomenalist classification and that P-154 is the empirical arbiter

Approximate addition: 400 words.

### Fix 2 — Tightened P-160 Falsification Threshold (MEDIUM — Skeptic)

The Skeptic noted that P-160 was "too flexible; can accommodate almost any outcome." A falsification criterion was added specifying:
- The pattern of correlations must match framework prediction within a pre-registered goodness-of-fit threshold
- Two specific failure modes: inconsistent outcomes without single-parameter fit, or correlations better explained by methodological artefacts
- Explicit rejection of "some correlation observed" as the bar

Approximate addition: 150 words.

### Fix 3 — Added Christological-Restraint Paragraph in §13.8.4 (ENHANCEMENT — Theologian)

The Theologian's recommended enhancement was a footnote-style paragraph acknowledging that the Christological thread is carried in later books and in theological disciplines proper. The addition:
- Names the feeling of absence for sympathetic readers
- Points to Books 1–3 of the series where the register shifts
- Points to the Christian philosophy-of-mind tradition
- Maintains the chapter's physics discipline while honoring the theological horizon

Approximate addition: 200 words.

### Fix 4 — Added Churchland/Dennett Paragraph at end of §13.4.2 (MEDIUM — Skeptic)

The Skeptic noted that Churchland (eliminative materialism) and Dennett (illusionism) rejections of the framework's starting assumptions are not addressed. A paragraph was added:
- Names both positions explicitly
- States the framework's reply to each (both are structurally falsified by positive P-154; both are consistent with null P-154)
- Directs more detailed engagement to OP-13.5

Approximate addition: 250 words.

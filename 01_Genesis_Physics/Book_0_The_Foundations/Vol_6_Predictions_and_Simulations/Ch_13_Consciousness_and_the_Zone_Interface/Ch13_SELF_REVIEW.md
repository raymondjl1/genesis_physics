2. **Mysticism.** No passage appeals to ineffability, gnostic insight, or private revelation as a basis for any claim. Every claim is either derived from prior volumes or flagged as open (§13.9). "Consistent with" is used where "proves" would be mystical. PASS.

Every subsequent section avoids any phrasing that could be read as endorsing the Wigner-von Neumann proposal. The phrase "consciousness does not collapse the wavefunction" appears three times. PASS.
4. **Scientizing the supernatural.** §13.8 develops this discipline in detail. The chapter uses "consistent with" carefully (§13.8.1) and refuses five specific theological claims (§13.8.2). Scripture is cited for motivation only (§13.8.3). PASS.

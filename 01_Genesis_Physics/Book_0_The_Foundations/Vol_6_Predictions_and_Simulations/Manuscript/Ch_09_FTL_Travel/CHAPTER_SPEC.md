## "Why" Chain

1. **Why would FTL be possible at all, given that relativity forbids it?** — Because c is a *Firmament* speed limit (Axiom 3: c² = σ/μ), not a universal constant on the full 6D manifold. The bulk has independent causal structure.
2. **Why does the zone architecture change what GR allows?** — Because the extra dimensions and zone topology provide paths, warp factors, and potential barriers that don't exist in 4D GR. The warped 6D geometry creates shorter geodesic paths than the Firmament alone offers.
3. **Why are there exactly five mechanisms?** — Because five distinct geometric/physical features of the 6D metric each independently enable effective superluminal motion: warp-factor time dilation, perpendicular geodesic shortcuts, quantum tunneling at boundaries, metric engineering via field manipulation, and atemporal Zone 1 access.
4. **Why doesn't FTL travel create grandfather paradoxes?** — Because the fixed metric signature (-,+,+,+,+,+) forbids closed timelike curves, proper time is always monotonically increasing along worldlines, and the Sabbath boundary is a one-way causal wall.
5. **Why can't we build an FTL drive today?** — Because Phase 3 thermodynamic constraints lock the energy behind the second law, Firmament confinement is tight at accessible energies, and the required technology is millennia away. The architecture exists; the access doesn't (yet).
6. **Why present this in a physics textbook?** — Because these are falsifiable predictions derived from solved field equations, not speculation. Each mechanism has observable signatures and falsification criteria.

## Section Outline

### Section 9.1: Why FTL and Why Now
- **Topic sentence:** Establishes why the zone architecture naturally raises the FTL question and what standard physics says.
- **"Why" entry point:** Standard GR strictly forbids FTL. What changed?
- **Key content:** c as Firmament property (Axiom 3: c² = σ/μ), bulk has independent causal structure, 5 mechanisms overview, honest assessment framework (DEMANDS / PERMITS / FORBIDS)
- **Exit condition:** Reader understands that c is local to the Firmament and the 6D bulk enables new possibilities.

### Section 9.2: Mechanism 1 — Temporal Shortcuts
- **Topic sentence:** Derives FTL via proper-time compression through ξ-dimension warp-factor modulation.
- **"Why" entry point:** If the metric has warp factors that vary in the extra dimensions, proper time along a path through those dimensions differs from the Firmament-only path.
- **Key content:** 6D geodesic equations, warp factor A(ξ,η) profile, proper-time integral, explicit Alpha Centauri example, energy ~10^15–10^18 J, causality proof, observable signatures, TRL assessment
- **Exit condition:** Reader can calculate effective FTL speed for given warp-factor excursion.

### Section 9.3: Mechanism 2 — Dimensional Bypass
- **Topic sentence:** Derives FTL via null/timelike geodesics with perpendicular η-components that create geometric shortcuts.
- **"Why" entry point:** If light already uses this mechanism (starlight propagation), what would it take for matter?
- **Key content:** Null geodesic in 6D, starlight precedent, timelike version for matter, binding energy to escape Firmament, navigation in Waters Below, distance reduction factor, energy ~10^25–10^28 J, causality (spacelike separation), TRL
- **Exit condition:** Reader understands why starlight proves the geometry and what prevents matter from following.

### Section 9.4: Mechanism 3 — Zone Tunneling
- **Topic sentence:** Derives quantum tunneling probability at zone boundaries for macroscopic objects.

- **Exit condition:** Reader understands why zone tunneling is theoretically valid but practically impossible.

### Section 9.5: Mechanism 4 — Field Distortion (Warp Bubble)

- **Exit condition:** Reader understands the most promising FTL mechanism and its engineering requirements.

### Section 9.6: Mechanism 5 — Consciousness Interface via Zone 1
- **Topic sentence:** Derives non-local information transfer through the atemporal Zone 1 domain.
- **"Why" entry point:** If consciousness couples to an atemporal zone, does that enable instantaneous communication?

- **Exit condition:** Reader understands the most speculative mechanism and its fundamental limitation.

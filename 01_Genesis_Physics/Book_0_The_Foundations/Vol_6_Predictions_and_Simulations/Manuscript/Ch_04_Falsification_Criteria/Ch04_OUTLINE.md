## Section 4.2: The Falsification Hierarchy — Not All Deaths Are Equal
**Topic sentence:** A framework can fail at four levels, and confusing them is the most common error in theory evaluation.

**Key content:**
- **Level 1: Framework-Killing.** An observation incompatible with the foundational axioms. If these fail, nothing built on them survives. Example: if extra dimensions don't exist at any scale, zone architecture is dead.
- **Level 2: Pillar-Killing.** A major derivation chain fails. The axioms might survive, but the physics built on them doesn't. Example: if QED doesn't emerge from membrane quantization, the particle physics derivations fail.
- **Level 3: Component-Level.** A specific model or subprediction fails. The framework survives but must be revised. Example: the hard-wall particle mass model is *already* at this level.
- **Level 4: Precision-Level.** A numerical prediction is outside error bars. The framework's structure survives, but a parameter or approximation needs refinement. Example: α derivation off by >1%.
- The standard physics analogy: Newton's gravity was precision-level falsified by Mercury's precession, not framework-killed.

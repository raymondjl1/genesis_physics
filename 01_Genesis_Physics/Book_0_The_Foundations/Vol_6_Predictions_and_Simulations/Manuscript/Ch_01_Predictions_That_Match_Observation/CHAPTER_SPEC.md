## "Why" Chain

1. **Why compile these predictions now?** — Because after 5 volumes of derivations, we owe the reader a systematic accounting of what the framework actually predicts — and whether those predictions match reality.
2. **Why start with matches rather than differences?** — Because establishing the framework's credibility requires first showing it reproduces the vast body of confirmed physics. If it can't get known results right, novel predictions carry no weight.
3. **Why include standard physics values alongside zone architecture predictions?** — Because the reader must judge whether zone architecture merely recapitulates standard physics or genuinely adds something. An honest comparison is the foundation of trust.
4. **Why organize by precision/strength?** — Because leading with the strongest results (QED at 10⁻¹⁰ precision) establishes the framework's maximum demonstrated capability before presenting weaker matches.
5. **Why include a scorecard?** — Because a new framework must earn respect through honesty. Where standard physics does equally well, say so. Where zone architecture adds explanatory power (the "why" behind values), highlight that distinction.

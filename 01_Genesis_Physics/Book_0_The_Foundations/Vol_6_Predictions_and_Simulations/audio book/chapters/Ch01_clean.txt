What It Means to "Match".

Five volumes of derivation lie behind us. We have built a 6D zone manifold from seven axioms, reduced it to 4D physics by Kaluza-Klein compactification, and derived classical mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum field theory, general relativity, and cosmology from the resulting structure. Now comes the question every reader has been waiting to ask: does any of this actually match what we observe?

Before we open the catalog, we need to agree on what "match" means. Three levels of agreement appear throughout this chapter, and distinguishing them honestly is essential to the scorecard we present at the end.

Level 1: Exact reproduction. Zone architecture derives the same equation as standard physics. Maxwell's equations emerge identically from the membrane wave propagation analysis of Vol 2, Ch 3. The Schwarzschild metric emerges identically from the 4D projection of the 6D Einstein equations in Vol 5, Ch 1. In these cases, every prediction of standard physics is automatically a prediction of zone architecture, because the mathematical content is the same. The gain is not a different answer — it is the answer to "but why does this equation hold?" Standard physics says: "Here is Maxwell's equation; it works." Zone architecture says: "Here is why electromagnetic fields propagate as waves at speed c — because the membrane's oscillation modes satisfy exactly this wave equation."

Level 2: Numerical agreement. Zone architecture predicts a specific numerical value — from its geometric parameters, not from a fit to data — and that value matches experiment to a stated precision. The fine structure constant, derived in Vol 5, Ch 13 as alpha to the -1 = 137.17 plus or minus 0.15, agrees with the experimental value of 137.035 999 084(21) to 0.10%. This is a different kind of match than Level 1: the framework is making a commitment about a number that standard physics treats as a free parameter.

Level 3: Structural agreement. Zone architecture predicts a functional form, scaling relation, or qualitative behavior that matches observation. The baryonic Tully-Fisher relation emerges with slope exactly 4 (Vol 5, Ch 11, Eq 5.11.14), matching the observed relation. The cosmic energy budget splits as 68.4%/26.6%/4.9% from zone geometry, matching Planck 2018 data. Here the match is structural — the framework predicts not just a number but a pattern.

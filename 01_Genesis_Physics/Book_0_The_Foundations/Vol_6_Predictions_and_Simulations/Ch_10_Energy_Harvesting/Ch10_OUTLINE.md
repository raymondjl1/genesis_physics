### 10.5.6 Casimir pressure formula and 1/a⁴ scaling

### 10.5.7 Gross and net power (centerpiece derivation)

$$
P_{\text{gross}} = (F/A) \cdot A \cdot \Delta x \cdot f \cdot N \quad (10.5.8)

- Setup: precision rotation stage; measure P_net as a function of angle θ relative to local gravitational vertical; scan once per sidereal day.

## §10.10 — Thermodynamic Consistency: No Perpetual Motion (≈ 2,000 words)

### 10.10.1 Open-system formulation
- The system {Waters Above + Firmament + Waters Below + κ(t)} is open (Vol 1 Ch 1–2, Axiom of Open System).

### 10.10.3 Why this is not a loophole

### 10.10.5 What a genuine violation would look like
- A device with isolated reservoir (no Zone-1 coupling required) yet producing steady positive output would falsify the framework too — it would violate the open-system axiom itself.
- **Prediction P-118:** maximum sustainable extraction rate bounded by κ × A_coupled, with κ ≤ 10⁻¹⁰ W/m² per unit coupled area (order-of-magnitude estimate from ρ_Λ).

### 10.10.6 Summary statement

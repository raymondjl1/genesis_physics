## Section 1: What It Means to "Match" (1,200 words)

**Topic sentence:** Before cataloging predictions, we must define what "matching observation" means for a framework that derives its results from a different starting point than standard physics.

**"Why" entry point:** The reader has spent 5 volumes watching zone architecture derive physics from Genesis 1's structure. Now they need to know: does it actually work? But "works" needs a definition.

**Key content:**
- Three levels of matching:
  1. **Exact reproduction** — zone architecture derives the same equation as standard physics (e.g., Maxwell's equations emerge identically)
  2. **Numerical agreement** — zone architecture predicts a specific value that matches experiment to stated precision (e.g., α⁻¹ = 137.17 vs. 137.036)
  3. **Structural agreement** — zone architecture predicts the same functional form or scaling behavior (e.g., Tully-Fisher slope = 4 exactly)
- How precision is reported: relative error |predicted − measured|/measured
- The critical distinction: standard physics often *fits* a parameter; zone architecture often *derives* it. Both match experiment, but one explains *why*.
- The prediction numbering system (P-001, P-002, ...) and format
- Introduction of the honest scorecard concept
- Brief preview of the test suite: 136 tests, 97 PASS, 37 PARTIAL, 0 FAIL, 2 NOT YET

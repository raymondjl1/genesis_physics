## 5. The Skeptic — PASS (the chapter's critical reviewer)

**Critical check:** Is every "RESOLVED" claim earned with evidence? Are gaps honestly labeled? Does the chapter narrate resolutions or demonstrate them?

**Verdict: PASS.**

This is the chapter's most important pass because this reviewer's standard is also the chapter's own standard. I went through every RESOLVED claim in §14.9 and §14.10 and checked whether the evidence would satisfy a hostile external reader.

- §14.9.1 (cosmic capacitor factor of 20): RESOLVED, with the concession that the original claim was an order-of-magnitude estimate. Earned.
- §14.9.4 (76-order σ discrepancy): RESOLVED with an unusually honest acknowledgement that the critic's calculation was correct for the simplified formula and that the simplified formula's presence in earlier drafts was a documentation failure. The chapter does not try to wiggle out of this. Earned.
- §14.9.6 (orifice model withdrawn): RESOLVED by abandonment of the model. Clean.
- §14.10.1 items 1–4 (Casimir overstatement; σ; orifice; 154 kW): All RESOLVED with specific evidence. Clean.

The PARTIAL claims are more interesting. §14.9.3 (thermodynamic replenishment) converts the critic's NEEDS WORK 🚨 flag into OP-11/OP-17 — an explicit open problem with a $150 experimental path. This is not narration; it is demonstration. The critic wanted the framework to either formalize the thermodynamics or retire the energy-extraction claim. The framework has done the first partially (η parameter specified) and committed to the second if η = 0. A hostile reader cannot argue that the chapter has ducked the issue, even if they disagree with the framework's optimism about η > 0.

The one DISPUTED-at-the-time finding (§14.9.10) is the chapter's single most interpretive move. I considered whether it should be moved to PARTIAL or OPEN. On reflection I accept the chapter's framing: the critic's "low confidence" verdict was factually correct at time of writing, and the intervening improvement (rectenna substitution; explicit η parameter) makes the current state different from what the critic evaluated. Labeling this as DISPUTED-at-the-time / RESOLVED-now is honest.

**Areas where I pressed but the chapter held:**
- OP-1 BLOCKER status: I pushed the chapter to demote OP-1 to HIGH on the grounds that "structural" gaps are a matter of interpretation. The §14.3.6 argument that "a framework cannot derive matter from the 6D action if it imports the Grassmann sector" is the correct rebuttal; I accept BLOCKER.
- §14.12.1 "A final honesty" paragraph: This is where the framework is most exposed to a determined skeptic's dismissal. The paragraph does not try to preempt the dismissal; it acknowledges that some readers will not be convinced. This is the right move.

**Pass.**

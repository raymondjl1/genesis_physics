## Identified Weaknesses (for reviewer-agent attention)

1. The Ψ_spirit ontology OP-23 is marked HIGH in §14.7 but the chapter's argument for this severity is thin — it defers to Ch 13 §13.9 for the full treatment. A Skeptic reviewer may press on whether a framework should import severity ratings from another chapter without defending them locally.

2. The "30 person-years at peak" budget figure in §14.11.2 is a rough estimate; a Navigator reviewer may ask for a more granular breakdown. The chapter explicitly labels the estimate as ballpark, which should mitigate but may not fully address the concern.

3. The cross-program comparison in §14.8.4 (Standard Model / string theory / LQG / Genesis Physics) is compressed; a Physicist reviewer familiar with another program may find specific characterizations superficial. The framework's position is that Ch 15 handles the comparison at the required depth.

4. OP-19 (absolute scales of ℏ, G, k_B) is marked LOW because it is "expected to resolve with OP-2"; if OP-2 Path A is not the correct path, OP-19 should be re-severed. The chapter flags this explicitly in §14.6.6 but a Student reviewer may want more specific "under what evidence would I promote this" guidance.

5. The chapter's handling of the critic's SECTION 9 "confidence level" finding (§14.9.10, DISPUTED-at-the-time / RESOLVED-now) is the chapter's most interpretive response to the peer-review record. A Consistency Auditor may want a cleaner citation path.

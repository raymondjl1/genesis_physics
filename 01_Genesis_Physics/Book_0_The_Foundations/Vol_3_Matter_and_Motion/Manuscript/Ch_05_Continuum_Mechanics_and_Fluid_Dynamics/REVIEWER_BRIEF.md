## What This Chapter Does

Chapter 5 derives the equations of fluid mechanics — continuity, Euler, Navier-Stokes, wave equation, and sound speed — from the Waters field equations of Vol 1, Chapter 6. The derivation is structured Waters-first: Genesis 1:2 and 1:6–7 are read literally (the Waters are real physical fields), and all of fluid mechanics is shown to follow from the zone-manifold dynamics of those fields.

This is NOT a chapter that finds "Genesis agrees with fluid mechanics." It is a chapter that says the Waters have density and pressure by definition of what they are (scalar fields with amplitudes), and derives from their field equations that they satisfy the same PDEs engineers call Navier-Stokes.

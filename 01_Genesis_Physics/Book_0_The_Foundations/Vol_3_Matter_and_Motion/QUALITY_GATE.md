## What This Volume Must Accomplish

F=ma becomes a theorem, not an axiom. Classical mechanics and thermodynamics derived from zone architecture. The student who finishes this volume understands *why things move the way they do* — from molecules to galaxies.

**The big "why" moments:** Why does F=ma? Why is energy conserved? Why does entropy increase? Why are there two types of particles (fermions and bosons)? Why does time flow forward?

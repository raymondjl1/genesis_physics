Overview.

The Vol 3 back matter comprises four components, each with a distinct pedagogical and reference function:

1. Appendix A: Key Results from Volumes 1–2 — A concise but complete reference of every Vol 1 and Vol 2 equation cited anywhere in Vol 3. The student should be able to look up any referenced result without flipping back to earlier volumes.
2. Appendix B: Experimental Mechanics Data — All experimental data (force measurements, material properties, thermodynamic constants) used or predicted in Vol 3, with sources.
3. Problem Sets with Selected Solutions (Ch 1–12) — 3–5 problems per chapter spanning computational, conceptual, and challenge difficulty.
4. Bibliography — 100+ references spanning foundational papers, modern experiments, textbooks, and zone-specific sources.

This back matter differs from Vol 2's in one critical respect: Appendix A is a back-reference, not a math review. Vol 2's Appendix A was a vector-calculus / tensor toolkit. Vol 3 assumes that toolkit (and Vol 1 Appendix B's notation) and instead collects the specific equations Vol 3 cites, so the student has them at fingertip distance.

Component Requirements.

Appendix A: Key Results from Volumes 1–2.

Req ID: BM-A02. Requirement: Every Vol 2 equation cited in Vol 3 must appear. Acceptance Criteria: Cross-check against all 12 chapter drafts.

Req ID: BM-A07. Requirement: Notation matches Vol 1 Appendix B exactly. Acceptance Criteria: Every symbol consistent.

Appendix B: Experimental Mechanics Data.

Req ID: BM-B01. Requirement: Tabulate all material properties used. Acceptance Criteria: Elastic moduli, densities, yield strengths, speeds of sound — Cu, Al, Fe, Diamond, H₂O, air.
Req ID: BM-B02. Requirement: Tabulate all thermodynamic constants. Acceptance Criteria: k_B, N_A, R, σ_SB, c_1, c_2 Planck radiation constants; with CODATA source.
Req ID: BM-B03. Requirement: Tabulate all kinetic/transport coefficients. Acceptance Criteria: Viscosity, thermal conductivity, diffusion for standard gases/liquids at STP.
Req ID: BM-B04. Requirement: Planck spectrum data. Acceptance Criteria: CMB temperature, peak wavelength, u_total — COBE/FIRAS + Planck 2018.
Req ID: BM-B05. Requirement: Phase transition data. Acceptance Criteria: Critical points (H₂O, CO₂), latent heats, Curie temperatures; electroweak scale.
Req ID: BM-B06. Requirement: Particle masses used in Ch 6–7. Acceptance Criteria: e, μ, τ, u, d, s, c, b, t, W, Z, Higgs — PDG 2024.
Req ID: BM-B07. Requirement: Orbital mechanics data. Acceptance Criteria: Planetary semi-major axes, periods, eccentricities — verify Kepler's 3rd law.
Req ID: BM-B08. Requirement: Zone-derived vs. measured comparison. Acceptance Criteria: Side-by-side with % agreement where the framework makes a prediction.
Req ID: BM-B09. Requirement: Every number cites its source. Acceptance Criteria: CODATA year, PDG year, NIST database, COBE/FIRAS paper, etc..

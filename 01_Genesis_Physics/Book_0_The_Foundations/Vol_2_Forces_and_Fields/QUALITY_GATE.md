## What This Volume Must Accomplish

Answer the question every physics student asks and never gets answered: *Why are there four forces, why do they have the strengths they do, and why do they work the way they work?* All four fundamental forces derived as geometric consequences of the Firmament and zone manifold from Volume 1.

**Critical deliverables:** Maxwell's equations derived. Gravity derived. Strong and weak forces derived. Hierarchy problem solved. Fine structure constant derivation begun (completed in Volume 5).

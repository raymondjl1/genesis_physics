# Bibliography — Foundations Vol 2: Forces and Fields

**Foundations Volume 2: Forces and Fields** (Genesis Physics Series)

A graduate-level physics textbook deriving all four fundamental forces from zone manifold geometry. This bibliography catalogs foundational papers, modern experimental results, mathematical references, and specialized sources on Kaluza-Klein theory, gauge theory, and the Standard Model.

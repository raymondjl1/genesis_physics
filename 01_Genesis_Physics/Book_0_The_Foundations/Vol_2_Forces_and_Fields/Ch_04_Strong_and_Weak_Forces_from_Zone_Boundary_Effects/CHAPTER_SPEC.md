## "Why" Chain

1. **Why are there exactly two short-range forces (strong and weak)?** — Because the zone manifold has exactly two boundary-localized geometric sectors: the ℤ₃ topology of the η-dimension (strong) and the parity-asymmetric ξ-boundary (weak). No other independent boundary sectors exist.

2. **Why is the strong force confining?** — Because color flux tubes connect topological vortex defects (quarks) in the bounded Waters Below region. The hard boundary at η = η_B prevents flux from escaping, forcing it into tubes with linear energy cost V(r) = σ_QCD·r. Separating quarks costs infinite energy.

3. **Why is the weak force short-range (~10⁻¹⁸ m)?** — Because the W and Z bosons acquire mass from the Higgs condensate localized in Waters Above. Massive mediators produce Yukawa-type potentials that fall off as e^(−Mr)/r, limiting range to ℏ/(Mc) ~ 10⁻¹⁸ m.

4. **Why does the weak force violate parity?** — Because the Waters Above boundary extends only for ξ ≥ 0 (one-sided). Left-handed fermions (even-parity ξ-modes) overlap with the W boson profile; right-handed fermions (odd-parity modes) do not. The asymmetry is structural, not accidental.

5. **Why are there three quark colors?** — Because the Waters Below η-dimension has S¹/ℤ₃ topology, creating exactly three winding sectors. The center of SU(3) is ℤ₃, matching this compactification symmetry.

6. **Why are there three generations of matter?** — Because three distinct topological vortex defect configurations exist in the Waters Below field Ψ_B, each corresponding to a generation.

7. **Why does CP violation exist?** — Because with ≥3 generations, the CKM matrix necessarily contains an irreducible complex phase from the relative topological winding of vortex sectors coupling to the Higgs field. However, the precise numerical value of this phase remains an open calculation (continued in Vol 4).

8. **Why is the strong coupling constant α_s larger than α_EM?** — Because the strong force couples to boundary-localized modes (small effective volume), concentrating the interaction, while EM flux spreads through the full extra-dimensional volume.

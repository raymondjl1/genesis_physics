## 1. Purpose

This chapter is the philosophical and mathematical capstone of Volume 5. It answers the question standard physics refuses to ask: *why do the fundamental constants have the values they do?* Three constants — Planck's constant ℏ, Newton's gravitational constant G, and Boltzmann's constant k_B — are derived from zone architecture parameters, not imported from experiment.

Each constant emerges from a different aspect of the architecture:
- **ℏ** from the membrane action scale (topological quantization on the Firmament)
- **G** from the 6D gravitational coupling (Kaluza-Klein reduction over extra-dimensional volume)
- **k_B** from the statistical mechanics of zone-mixing (membrane mode counting)

The chapter makes the anthropic principle unnecessary: the constants aren't fine-tuned for life — they're geometric consequences of the zone architecture.

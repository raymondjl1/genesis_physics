## "Why" Chain

1. **Why does the "starlight problem" seem hard?** Because if light travels at constant speed c and the universe is young, distant light shouldn't have arrived. But the standard resolution (universe is old) contradicts biblical chronology. So how do we reconcile fast light with young creation?

2. **Why would zone architecture *permit* rapid light transit during creation week?** Because creation-mode physics differs from sustaining-mode physics (Sabbath Boundary). During Days 1-6, light can traverse perpendicular dimensions (Waters Above); after Day 7, light is confined to 3D space.

3. **Why does space expand?** Because the Firmament itself is called the "stretched out thing" (Hebrew רָקִיעַ, raqia' from raqa', to stretch). Expansion is definitional to its nature. It occurs in two phases: rapid during Days 2-4 (creation-week geometry), slow during sustaining mode (current Hubble expansion).

4. **Why must stars created Day 4 give light to Earth immediately (functional integrity)?** Because Genesis explicitly states their purpose: "let them be lights... to give light upon the earth." Purpose implies immediate operation. An infant Earth without starlight violates functional design.

5. **Why does the framework predict ~13.8 Gyr sustaining-mode age while permitting creation-week creation?** Because sustaining-mode Friedmann equations (Ch 8) integrate to $t_0 \approx 13.8$ Gyr for the parameters $\Omega_A=0.684, \Omega_m=0.315$. But this age is sustaining-mode coordinate time. Creation-week physics operated under κ_create, producing different temporal topology.

6. **Why is it NOT deceptive to create stars with light streams reaching Earth immediately?** Because functional integrity does not equal false history. Adam was created mature (not infant); wine Jesus created had aged characteristics (not raw); stars created to give light must include working light delivery. Apparent age is functional age.

7. **Why can't we test this?** Why we *can* partially test it: (a) Redshift predictions must match Ch 8 cosmology, (b) Large-scale structure must be consistent with fast initial expansion, (c) CMB should show Firmament initialization signature from Day 2, not 380,000 years post-Big Bang. Full test of the light-propagation mechanism requires understanding Waters Above better — that remains open.

## Problem Sets (Foundations)

1. **Computational.** Using the two-phase expansion model with creation-week Hubble parameter $H_\text{create} = 3 \times 10^{14} \, H_0$ and sustaining-mode $H_0 = 67.4$ km/s/Mpc, compute: (a) The scale-factor ratio $a_\text{Day 7} / a_\text{Day 2}$ if expansion is approximately exponential; (b) The proper distance to a star created Day 4 at comoving distance 1 Mpc if it was separated by expansion during Days 4-7; (c) The light-travel time from that star to Earth in the expansion-assisted model (assume light is carried along by expansion).

2. **Conceptual.** Explain in physical terms why the phrase "light created in transit" is problematic (it creates false observational history) while "light generated by a functioning star" is not problematic (it creates true physics). What is the distinction?

3. **Conceptual.** The Bible uses two verb forms for cosmic stretching: perfect tense ("stretched out," completed action, Days 2-6) and participle ("stretching," ongoing action, sustaining mode). Explain how the zone framework predicts this two-phase grammar. Why should we expect this linguistic distinction if the physics has two phases?

4. **Challenge.** The sustaining-mode age from Ch 8 is $t_0 = 13.8$ Gyr. The creation-week duration (if acceleration-time model holds) is ~24 hours. These appear contradictory. Show explicitly that they are not contradictory but measure *different* time coordinates related by a phase transition. (Hint: coordinate transformations at phase boundaries.)

5. **Challenge.** In sustaining-mode cosmology (post-Day 7), the comoving distance to a galaxy at redshift $z = 5$ is approximately 11 Gpc. From Vol 5 Ch 8 Friedmann equations, derive the light-travel time for that galaxy's light to reach Earth. Should this be ~13.6 Gyr (roughly the age of the universe at that redshift)? Now explain why in the creation-week model, the "real" light-travel time could be much shorter if expansion was assisted.

# On Honesty

*A preface to Volume 4, to be read before Chapter 1.*

---

A textbook is a series of promises, and the first promise this one makes is that it
will tell you where it is weak before you have to find out for yourself. Science
advances by naming its failures. A book that claims to derive everything and quietly
omits its open problems is not a textbook; it is propaganda — and Volume 4, which
attempts the hardest thing in the whole Genesis Physics series (to derive the Standard
Model and the masses of the particles from the architecture of Genesis 1), is the place
where that temptation is strongest and must be most firmly refused.

So, before the physics begins, here is the volume's honest map: every place where the
derivation is incomplete, gathered into one table so that you carry the risks with you
from the first page rather than discovering them buried in a later chapter. The same
table appears, with its surrounding discussion, at §1.5.4; it is reproduced here at the
front so that the honesty commitment is the first thing you read, not the last.

## The Axiom Dependency (read this before the table)

Two of the entries below were once listed as outright blockers. As of **Author
Ratification #1 (2026-06-11)**, the framework **adopts two foundational axioms** —
$\Lambda_{Z0}$ (the Godhead-zone cosmological constant) and **Postulate F** (the
Waters-Above vortex sector carries Hopf winding number $n_w = 3$). *Given* those adopted
axioms, several former blockers are **resolved with the dependency stated transparently** —
this is builder's honesty, **not** a claim of "proven from nothing." Wherever a
fermion-sector result is used in this volume, the dependence on $\Lambda_{Z0}$ / Postulate F
is restated, never buried. The axiom itself is recorded in
`Research/Foundations/AXIOM_GODHEAD_ZONE_Z0.md`; the canonical open-problems register is the
GitHub project board (issues #1, #2, #3, #25, #26).

force discrete spectra, the Firmament membrane carries a finite minimum action, and
together they force the universe to be quantum. *That* much is secure. What is not yet
secure is a complete and numerically faithful derivation of every detail of the Standard
Model particle content. The gap is real, it is stated here at the front, and the path to
closing it is tracked on the project board.

If you close this volume thinking *"the framework derives the structure of the fermion
sector — spin-½, three generations, charge quantization — given its adopted foundational
axioms, but has not yet proven it can compete numerically on every mass,"* you will have
read it exactly as it was written. To borrow Paul's phrase, we still know in part and
prophesy in part. The work continues.

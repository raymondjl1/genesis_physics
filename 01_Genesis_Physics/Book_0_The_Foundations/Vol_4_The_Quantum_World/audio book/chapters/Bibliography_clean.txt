Foundations Vol 4, The Quantum World — Back Matter

This is the largest bibliography in the Foundations series so far. It must be: Volume 4 covers quantum mechanics, quantum field theory, and the entire Standard Model of particle physics, and the literature on any one of those three is itself enormous. What follows is the subset Vol 4 actually uses — every paper cited in the text, every textbook taken as background, every experimental measurement reproduced in Appendix B, and every internal zone-architecture research file referenced. The total is 215 entries across ten sections.

Citation format follows the convention established in Vols 2 and 3: Chicago author-year with sequential numbering within each section, with a symbol marking the entry's class — 📜 foundational paper, 📘 textbook, ⚛ experiment, ⚙ data compilation, ☷ zone-architecture internal source.

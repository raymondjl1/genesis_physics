# Appendix B: Simplified Glossary for the Family Edition

## Purpose

This glossary brings together every key term from *Genesis Physics: The Creator's Blueprint* in one easy-to-reference place. We've alphabetized all unique terms, kept definitions warm and accessible for family reading (middle-school level and up), and included pronunciations and biblical references where they matter to the story.

Think of this as your "flight log" for the book—a record of the labeled concepts that make the whole journey sensible.

---

**Bara**
*Pronounced *bah-RAH*.* — Hebrew verb for *create*, used in Scripture only with God as subject. Denotes origination of something qualitatively new — not re-arrangement of existing material.

*(First introduced: Chapter 1)*

---

**Bereshit**
*Pronounced *bay-ray-SHEET*.* — Hebrew opening word of Genesis 1:1. Names the head-point of an ordered temporal and logical sequence. Not "once upon a time"; the labeled first entry.

*(First introduced: Chapter 1)*

---

**Bullet Cluster**
*(physics term)* — A pair of galaxy clusters photographed in 2006 that had recently collided and passed through each other. The visible hot gas was slowed by the collision; the dark matter kept going. The clearest single image of the Waters Below in action.

*(First introduced: Chapter 6)*

---

**Chladni plate**
*(physics term)* — A thin metal plate whose resonant vibration, when excited by a bow or a speaker, gathers loose grains on its surface into visible patterns along the nodes of the vibration. First documented by Ernst Chladni in 1787. In this book, the canonical sensory demonstration of *qavah.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Chok**
*Pronounced *HOKE*.* — Hebrew noun, *fixed decree, statute, ordinance.* The word Jeremiah 33:25 uses for the *fixed order of heaven and earth.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Closed vs. open system**
A *closed* system exchanges nothing — energy, matter, information — with anything outside itself. An *open* system is coupled to something outside itself. In this framework, the cosmos is an open system: it is coupled to the sustaining Word.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Conservation law**
A statement that some quantity — energy, momentum, electric charge — has the same total after any event as it had before. The rule book of the universe keeps its tallies.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Cosmic energy budget**
*(physics term)* — The total energy content of the cosmos, broken down into what fraction is in each component. Multiple independent methods converge on roughly 5% visible matter, 27% dark matter, 68% dark energy.

*(First introduced: Chapter 6)*

---

**Cosmic microwave background (CMB)**
The oldest light in the universe. A low-level microwave glow filling all of space in every direction, emitted roughly 380,000 years after the beginning of the cosmos, when the early universe cooled enough for light to decouple from matter and stream freely for the first time. Predicted in the late 1940s; discovered by Arno Penzias and Robert Wilson at Bell Labs in 1964; mapped in increasing detail ever since. Observational evidence that the electromagnetic field was filled with radiation long before any star existed — which is, in its own vocabulary, exactly what Genesis 1:3 describes.

*(First introduced: Chapter 3)*

---

**Cosmic web**
*(modern physics term)* — The actual arrangement of galaxies across the observable universe: filaments connecting nodes, with enormous voids between. Discovered in the late twentieth century by the large galaxy surveys. The astronomer's name for the third adjective Ezekiel already used — *crystalline-ordered.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 5)*

---

**Cosmological constant**
*(physics term)* — The measured energy density of dark energy. Written down by Einstein in 1917, abandoned for most of the twentieth century, reintroduced after the 1998 supernova measurements. In this framework, the cosmological constant *is* the energy density of the Waters Above.

*(First introduced: Chapter 6)*

---

**Covenant**
A binding promise. God's covenant with *the fixed order of heaven and earth* (Jer 33:25) is His pledge to keep the laws of the cosmos consistent. The laws are not His cage; they are His promise.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Curvature.**
The bending of a surface when something sits on it. The firmament around the earth is curved. "Gravity" is the name for the curving.

*(First introduced: Chapter 9)*

---

**Dabar**
*Pronounced *dah-VAR*.* — Hebrew noun, *word, spoken command, ordering utterance.* The driving agent of the gathering.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Dark Energy**
*(physics term)* — The invisible field exerting a repulsive pressure that accelerates the expansion of space. Discovered observationally in 1998. Roughly 68% of the cosmic energy budget. Corresponds to the Waters Above.

*(First introduced: Chapter 6)*

---

**Dark Matter**
*(physics term)* — The invisible mass that holds galaxies and galaxy clusters together by its gravitational pull. Inferred from the 1930s, confirmed through the 1970s, photographed indirectly in the 2006 Bullet Cluster observation. Roughly 27% of the cosmic energy budget. Corresponds to the Waters Below.

*(First introduced: Chapter 6)*

---

**Dark energy**
*(physics term)* — Invisible field exerting a repulsive pressure that accelerates the expansion of space; observationally confirmed in 1998; approximately 68% of the cosmic energy budget. Corresponds to the Waters Above.

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**Dark matter**
*(physics term)* — Invisible matter whose gravitational pull holds galaxies and galaxy clusters together; inferred by the 1930s, observationally confirmed through the 1970s and 80s; approximately 27% of the cosmic energy budget. Corresponds to the Waters Below.

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**Density (of the membrane).**
How heavy the firmament is per unit area. Higher density → slower ripples. Set at creation.

*(First introduced: Chapter 9)*

---

**Derived Constant vs. Free Parameter.**
A *free parameter* is a number you have to measure because nothing tells you what it is. A *derived constant* is a number whose value falls out of a deeper picture. Standard physics treats *c* and *G* as free parameters. The framework shows they are derived — cousins from the same fabric.

*(First introduced: Chapter 9)*

---

**Electromagnetic field**
The invisible, pervasive structure of space that carries electric and magnetic interactions, and that hosts the traveling ripples we call light. In the Genesis Physics framework (*Foundations Vol 2 Ch 3*), the electromagnetic field is the simplest oscillation mode of the Firmament (the *raqia* of Genesis 1:6–8). Light is what the Firmament does when it ripples.

*(First introduced: Chapter 3)*

---

**Entropy**
A measurement of disorder in a system. The Second Law of Thermodynamics states that, in a *closed* system, entropy always increases over time.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Field**
*(physics term)* — A continuous medium, present everywhere in a region, capable of being excited into stable patterns. The Waters Above and the Waters Below of Genesis 1:7 are fields.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Firmament**
The stretched expanse between the two fields of *mayim.* Where you live. Canonical zone label: Z₂.₂.₂. Modern physics calls the same thing *the observable universe* — the baryonic, visible cosmos that hosts stars, planets, and life.

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**First Law of Thermodynamics**
Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed. (Conservation of energy, written in the thermodynamic register.)

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Generation**
*(physics term)* — One of three repeating sets of fundamental particles that the physicists have found in nature. The first generation is the matter we touch every day. The second and third are heavier cousins of the first. In this framework, the three generations are three different standing-wave modes of the same underlying field.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Ho autos**
*Pronounced *ho OW-toss*.* — Greek phrase, *the same one.* Used in Hebrews 13:8 to describe Christ across every timeframe.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Logos**
Greek *Word / Reason / Order.* The ordering principle by which creation is intelligible. Identified with Christ in John 1:1–3.

*(First introduced: Chapter 1)*

---

**Mayim**
*Pronounced *MAH-yeem*.* — Hebrew word translated *waters.* Always grammatically plural. A broad Hebrew word for *fluid, formless material, primordial substance* — not only H₂O. On Day 2 it names the pre-structured cosmic material that God divides into two fields with the Firmament between them.

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**Me'orot**
*Pronounced *meh-oh-ROTE*.* — Hebrew for *luminaries, lights.* Day 4. Localized, oscillating light-emitting systems embedded in the Firmament.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Membrane tension**
*(modern physics term)* — The measured, enormous tension of the fabric of space. In this framework, it is the physical property that sets the speed of light. Scripture calls the same property the *stretching* of the heavens.

*(First introduced: Chapter 5)*

---

**Miracle**
An event in which the ordinary pattern of the laws is interrupted by a direct act of the Lawgiver. Not a rule-breaking — a higher-order sustaining act by the One who holds the rules in place.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Mo'adim**
*Pronounced *moh-ah-DEEM*.* — Hebrew for *appointed times.* The purpose clause of the Day 4 luminaries. The same word used for Israel's festivals. Names the sun, moon, and stars as *clocks.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Natah**
*Pronounced *nah-TAH*.* — Hebrew verb for *stretch out, spread, pitch* — the tent-maker's verb. The verb the Prophets and Psalmists use, again and again, for what God did to the heavens. Confirms from inside the Hebrew Bible that the sky is a stretched object, not a built roof.

*(First introduced: Chapter 5)*

---

**Nephesh chayah**
*Pronounced *NEH-fesh KHAI-yah*.* — Hebrew for *living soul, living creature.* First appears on Day 5. The seat of conscious interior experience. Plants do not have *nephesh;* animals do.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Nodal line**
*(physics term)* — A location on a vibrating surface where the vibration cancels itself out and the surface is momentarily still. Where the sand gathers on a Chladni plate.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Noether's theorem**
A mathematical proof, published by Emmy Noether in 1918, that every continuous symmetry of the laws of physics corresponds to a conserved quantity. Time-translation symmetry gives energy conservation. Space-translation gives momentum conservation. Rotational symmetry gives angular-momentum conservation. The deepest known fact about the rule book of the universe.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Open system**
*(physics term)* — A system that exchanges matter or energy with something outside itself. The observable universe, current cosmology has found, is an open system. *See* Book 1 Ch 1 — *The Most Ignored Page in Science*.

*(First introduced: Chapter 1)*

---

**Or**
*Pronounced *ohr*.* — Hebrew noun translated *light.* The common, unadorned Hebrew word for light in both physical and figurative senses; context narrows it. In Genesis 1:3 it names the first *condition* God calls into being — a capacity of the cosmos, not a property of any particular source.

*(First introduced: Chapter 3)*

---

**Phase transition**
*(physics term)* — The operation by which a continuous field reorganizes itself into a new stable configuration across the whole field at once. The condensation of water into ice is a phase transition. The organization of the Waters Below into matter is a phase transition.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Phos**
*Pronounced *fohs*.* — Greek noun translated *light.* Used in the New Testament for physical light (sunlight, lamplight), for the light of the eye, and for the divine nature in passages like 1 John 1:5 and John 8:12. The same functional vocabulary as Hebrew *or.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 3)*

---

**Photon**
A single traveling ripple in the electromagnetic field. Carries energy from one place to another at the speed of light. Every glowing object — sun, candle, firefly, phone screen — is matter that is coupling to the field and emitting photons. The field hosts the photons; the photons are not the field.

*(First introduced: Chapter 3)*

---

**Phthorá / phtheirō**
*Pronounced *f-thor-AH / f-THAY-roh*.* — Greek noun and verb, *decay, corruption, perishing.* The word group behind Paul's *bondage to corruption* in Romans 8:21 — the biblical name for entropy's direction in a Fallen cosmos.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Propagation Speed.**
The natural speed at which a ripple travels across a medium. On a guitar string it sets the pitch; on the firmament it sets the speed of light.

*(First introduced: Chapter 9)*

---

**Qadash**
*Pronounced *kah-DAHSH*.* — Hebrew verb for *to set apart, make holy, sanctify.* Day 7. The seventh day is distinct in kind from the six that preceded it.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Qavah**
*Pronounced *kah-VAH*.* — Hebrew verb for *gather:* to collect a diffuse substance into an ordered configuration at the pull of a command. Used on Day 3. Describes a phase transition — the organization of pre-existing substance into stable patterns, like sand on a Chladni plate.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Qerach hanora**
*Pronounced *KEH-rakh hah-noh-RAH*.* — Hebrew phrase in Ezekiel 1:22, rendered *"the terrible crystal"* (KJV) or *"awe-inspiring crystal"* (ESV). A specific visual description: stretched, transparent, and crystalline-ordered, all at once.

*(First introduced: Chapter 5)*

---

**Raqia**
*Pronounced *rah-KEE-ah*.* — Hebrew word translated *firmament* or *expanse.* From the root *raqa,* "to beat out, stretch." A stretched, tensioned expanse — a membrane — not a solid dome. In Book 2 and throughout this series, referred to by its canonical name: *the Firmament.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**Sea of glass**
John's name for the Firmament in Revelation 4:6. *"As it were a sea of glass, like crystal."* Same stretched, transparent, ordered surface Ezekiel saw, now viewed from the other side.

*(First introduced: Chapter 5)*

---

**Second Law of Thermodynamics**
In a closed system, entropy always increases over time. This is the law that has a *direction.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Shabat**
*Pronounced *shah-BAHT*.* — Hebrew verb for *cease, stop,* specifically the completion of a finished work. Not fatigue. The root of *Sabbath.* Names the transition from building mode (Days 1–6) to sustaining mode.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Shamayim**
*Pronounced *shah-MAH-yeem*.* — Hebrew for *heavens.* Always grammatically plural in form; always singular in reference. A plural of majesty, like *Elohim.* The heavens are *one* stretched expanse with three regions — atmospheric, stellar, and divine — which is what Paul meant by *the third heaven.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 5)*

---

**Shanah**
*Pronounced *shah-NAH*.* — Hebrew verb, *to change, to alter.* Negated in Malachi 3:6 — *God does not shanah.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Sharats**
*Pronounced *shah-RATS*.* — Hebrew verb for *swarm, multiply.* Day 5. Living systems that fill their habitats by recursive multiplication.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Standard Model**
*(physics term)* — The 20th-century physics framework that inventories all the known particles and forces. Its particle masses are now reproduced by the Genesis Physics framework from first principles, with the accuracies listed in §8.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Standing wave**
*(physics term)* — A pattern that appears when a field is driven at the right frequency and that persists as long as the driver continues. Remove the driver and the pattern disperses.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Symmetry (in physics)**
A statement that something about the universe stays the same when you change something else. *Time-translation symmetry* says the laws stay the same whether you run the experiment today or tomorrow. *Space-translation symmetry* says the laws stay the same whether you run it here or a mile east. *Rotational symmetry* says the laws do not depend on which way you are facing.

*(First introduced: Chapter 8)*

---

**Synistēmi**
*Pronounced *soo-NIS-tay-mee*.* — Greek verb, *stand together, hold their coherence.* The verb in Colossians 1:17. Present-tense sustaining.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Tension (of the membrane).**
How tightly the firmament is stretched. Higher tension → faster ripples. Set at creation and held constant by the Sustainer.

*(First introduced: Chapter 9)*

---

**Tohu va-bohu**
*Pronounced *TOE-hoo vah-BOW-hoo*.* — "Formless and void." Paired Hebrew phrase in Genesis 1:2 describing an initial unstructured state awaiting organization.

*(First introduced: Chapter 1)*

---

**Tselem elohim**
*Pronounced *TSEH-lem el-oh-HEEM*.* — Hebrew for *image of God.* Day 6. The representational capacity of one thing to stand in for another. The human being carries, uniquely among creatures, a reflection of the Creator's nature: reason, creativity, morality, relationship, dominion, self-awareness.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

---

**Waters Above**
The upper of the two *mayim* fields named on Day 2. Above the Firmament. A repulsive medium. Canonical zone label (in the Foundations encyclopedia): Z₂.₂.₃. Modern physics calls the same field *dark energy.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**Waters Below**
The lower of the two *mayim* fields named on Day 2. Beneath the Firmament. A scaffolding medium. Canonical zone label: Z₂.₂.₁. Modern physics calls the same field *dark matter.*

*(First introduced: Chapter 2)*

---

**Yabashah**
*Pronounced *yah-bah-SHAH*.* — Hebrew noun, *dry land.* The stable ordered state that appears after the gathering.

*(First introduced: Chapter 7)*

---

**Yom**
*Pronounced *yohm*.* — Hebrew for *day.* A flexible noun: a twenty-four-hour period, the daylight portion of one, or an indefinite period. Genesis 2:4 uses *yom* for the entire creation week. In this chapter the emphasis is on each day as a *stage* in an ordered sequence, not as a measured duration.

*(First introduced: Chapter 4)*

## Findings Addressed

Three reviewer notes were addressed in a revision round before finalization:

1. **But Why? Reader (critical):** "Light is a ripple on the firmament" was asserted without the bridge to Ch7's standing-wave picture — a "but why?" gap.
   - **Fix:** §4 revised to add an explicit Ch7 callback — matter as *standing* wave, light as *travelling* wave; both vibrations on the *raqia;* a photon is the firmament rippling and handing the ripple along. Follow-up review confirmed PASS.

2. **Skeptic (minor):** "The framework reproduces every classical test of electromagnetism and gravity" was geometrically loose — needed weak/strong-field boundary.
   - **Fix:** §8 revised to scope the claim to the weak-field regime and mark the strong-field regime as a working picture still being closed.

3. **Skeptic (minor):** The "dent" picture in §7b could be read as implying a background space the firmament sits inside.
   - **Fix:** §7b revised with a one-sentence caution — the firmament *is* the fabric of space-time; the "dent" is the bending of the membrane's own geometry, not a dip into a deeper room.

4. **Theologian (minor):** *amon* in Proverbs 8:30 has a scholarly debate (nursling vs. craftsman) that deserved acknowledgment.
   - **Fix:** §4 revised to note the debate and flag that the LXX and Targums read *craftsman,* which is the reading most English Bibles follow.

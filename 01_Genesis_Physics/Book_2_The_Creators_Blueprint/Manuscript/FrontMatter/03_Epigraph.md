# Epigraph

> The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork.
>
> — Psalm 19:1 (KJV)

*[NEEDS JEFF: confirm epigraph verse. Psalm 19:1 is proposed because it names the* firmament *— the central object of this book — and frames creation as a* declaration *(a document to be read), which is the book's whole thesis. Alternate candidates on the same theme: Jeremiah 33:25 ("the fixed order of heaven and earth"), Job 38:4 ("Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth?"), or Colossians 1:17 ("by him all things consist").]*

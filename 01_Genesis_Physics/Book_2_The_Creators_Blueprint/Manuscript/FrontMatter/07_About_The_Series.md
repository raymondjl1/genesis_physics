# About the Series

**[DRAFT — for author review. Reconciles the launch order and the three reader on-ramps per CLAUDE.md + METADATA.md.]**

This book is part of **Genesis Physics**, a family of books that all ask one question: *What if the first chapter of the Bible is a physics document?*

The same framework runs through every book in the series. What changes from book to book is not the architecture and not the author's voice — it is the **math density** and the **reader it is written for.** There are three on-ramps onto the same road, and you can start on whichever one fits you.

## The Three Books

**1. *The Creator's Blueprint* — the Family Edition.** *(This book.)* Scripture-first, zero equations, written for Christian homeschool families. Every chapter begins with what the Bible says and then shows how physics confirms it. A parent without a science degree can teach from it. This is the warmest on-ramp — the kitchen table.

**2. *The Hidden Architecture: A Physics of the First Page* — the Popular Science Flagship.** Written for the intelligent general reader — the kind of person who reads Brian Greene or Carlo Rovelli. Named equations, but no derivations. It makes the full physics case for the framework without requiring the reader to share the author's faith. This is the on-ramp for the curious skeptic.

**3. *The Foundations of Genesis Physics* — the technical series.** A multi-volume, equation-heavy work for physicists and graduate students. This is the encyclopedia: every claim in the two trade books traces down into it, and it exists so those books can cite their work. This is the on-ramp for the specialist who wants to check the math.

## The Launch Order — and Why It Looks Backwards

The books reach readers in this order:

> **Family Edition first → *The Hidden Architecture* second → *Foundations* in parallel.**

That order is deliberate, and it is the reverse of how the books *depend* on one another. Here is the honest explanation, because this book does not hide its scaffolding.

The Family Edition is the **easiest book to launch** and, quietly, the **hardest book to write correctly.** Every physics claim in it has to be true, and the way the author makes sure of that is by tracing each claim *upward* — through the popular-science flagship, and through it into the technical Foundations. So while families read this book first, its physics had to be locked *last*, after the heavier books had done their verifying work behind the scenes.

Why launch it first anyway? Because Christian homeschool families are the warmest, most word-of-mouth-driven readers in the world. This book is meant to start a conversation at a few thousand kitchen tables — and that conversation is what makes room for the flagship and the technical series that follow. The *Foundations* series, for its part, never needs a marketing launch at all; it is the reference work that exists so the trade books can point to it.

So if you came in through this book and you want to go deeper, the road keeps going. *The Hidden Architecture* gives you the full physics case. *Foundations* gives you the math. Same architecture. Same author. Different stick force.

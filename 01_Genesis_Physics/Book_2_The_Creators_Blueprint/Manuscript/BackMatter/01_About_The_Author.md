## Back-cover version (~150 words)

Jeff L. Raymond is an aerospace engineer, former Air Force officer, and senior operations executive with more than twenty-five years at the intersection of national-security space, unmanned aviation, and frontier technology. He served at the Space and Missile Systems Center and as Chief of Concepts Analysis at the National Reconnaissance Office, where he led a multi-agency review team on the flightworthiness of a multi-billion-dollar intelligence satellite. He deployed to Iraq as Executive Officer to the Deputy Commanding General for Operations. Over nine years at Boeing Insitu he pioneered Model-Based Systems Engineering and the FAA certification process for unmanned aircraft. He co-founded Eden Grow Systems, a NASA-designated spinoff, and is currently Vice President of Operations at OKSI, a defense AI-sensing company serving the DoD, NASA, and SpaceX. He lives in Washington State with his wife on a working homestead and creates *TheRealMartian* on YouTube.

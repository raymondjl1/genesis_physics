### 2. STRENGTH: The Two Water Sources Are Rigorously Distinguished in Hebrew

**Location:** §2 (lines 45–71)  
**Text:** Precise Hebrew parsing of *ma'yenot tehom rabbah* and *arubot ha-shamayim*; the verb *baqa* (commanded opening, not rupture) as distinguished from words for catastrophic failure; emphasis on the Hebrew naming two mechanisms, not one mechanism stated twice.

**Why It Works:**
- The chapter names the translation issue exactly: translators have historically run the two sources together, reading "rain" when Genesis says "two distinct mechanisms."
- *Ma'yan* (spring, fountain) is defined precisely: "an outlet through which water comes up under pressure — what we would call an artesian spring." This is not poetic language; it is technical language.
- *Tehom* is traced back to Genesis 1:2 (the primordial deep), creating a coherence link: the same body of water that existed before the firmament was divided is the source that opens in Genesis 7:11.
- *Arubbah* is shown not to mean "torn sky" but "window, lattice, sluice, floodgate"—the same word is used in 2 Kings 7:2 (ordinary opening, not cosmic failure).
- The *baqa* verb work is load-bearing: Exodus 17:6 (Moses at Horeb) and Numbers 16:31 (ground opening under Korah) establish that *baqa* is "the vocabulary of God commanding water through a barrier at His word," not the vocabulary of catastrophic rupture.

**Assessment:** A reader cannot dismiss this as creative interpretation. The framework is reading what the Hebrew actually says, not imposing meaning onto it.

**Severity:** N/A — This is a strength.

## SPECIAL CHECK: Does This Chapter Cohere With Ch10?

**Finding:** STRONG COHERENCE.

Ch10 established the Sabbath Boundary as a principle explaining how creation-mode rapid expansion (Days 2–4) differs from sustaining-mode slow expansion. Ch11 applies the same principle to a *post-Sabbath-Boundary* perturbation: the Flood is *not* creation-mode, but it *is* a temporary commanded reset of boundary conditions within sustaining mode.

The two chapters are related-but-separable:
- Ch10: creation-mode rapid expansion during Days 2–4.

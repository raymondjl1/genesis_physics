## §1. The Trout Tank

It is a Tuesday in March, the sun already gone but color still in the sky. I am out at the barn checking the trout tank — a two-hundred-gallon tote in the second bay where we raise rainbow trout for the freezer. Cold spring water loops through a chiller and a recirculating pump, and a float-valve tops the tank off as the water evaporates. A sturdy little system that runs all winter without much attention.

The power has been flickering — thunderstorms off to the west — and the pump has been cycling as the breaker dips and recovers. During one of those cycles the float-valve has stuck open. The tank is overflowing. Water is moving across the concrete in a slow sheet, finding the lowest path, around my boots, under the door, out into the gravel. The fish are fine; the tank is still topped up. But the water is *leaving the constraint* and finding the floor.

I kill the breaker, drag out a shop towel, and stand for a second and watch.

The water is not doing anything strange. It is doing exactly what water does. What changed is the *constraint*. The float-valve held the system at a setpoint; the setpoint failed; the system relaxed; water found the floor. There is no new physics here — just water doing what water always does, when the boundary condition on the system has been reset.

I want you to hold that thought.

Genesis 7:11 names a constraint change. *The fountains of the great deep broke up, and the windows of heaven were opened.* Two boundary conditions on planetary-scale water systems were temporarily reset. The water did what water always does. It found the lowest path. The result was a flood that covered the earth.

This chapter is for the parent whose kid came home from a geology class and was told the Flood is a myth — shown rocks, told the rocks prove it didn't happen. The parent needs an answer. Not a slogan, not a dodge, not "well, the Bible says so and we just have to believe." An honest, unhurried answer that takes the Hebrew seriously and takes the rocks seriously, and does not pretend the case is closed when it isn't.

Two guardrails before we start. I am not going to tell you the Flood was a small local event in Mesopotamia and that Genesis 7's "every mountain" is hyperbole; some sincere brothers and sisters read it that way, and I will not unchurch them, but it is not this chapter's position (I will tell you why in section 9). And I am not going to do creationism-versus-secular-geology. Honest physics through honest text gives an honest answer. Nobody has to lose for the Bible to be right.

Let us go.

## §2. Two Sources, Named in the Text

Let me put the verse back in front of us and read it the way an engineer reads a requirements document.

> *"In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened. And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights."*
> — Genesis 7:11–12, KJV

Notice the precision. A **date** — six hundredth year of Noah, second month, seventeenth day. A **duration** — forty days and forty nights. And **two distinct mechanisms** — the fountains of the great deep *broke up,* and the windows of heaven *were opened.* That is not poetry. That is an event log: source, source, duration, date.

Notice too two synchronized verbs in the Hebrew. The fountains *broke up* — בָּקַע — *baqa*. The windows *were opened* — the niphal of פָּתַח — *patach*. Two different verbs. The text is not saying the same thing twice with rhetorical flourish; it is naming two different physical channels that opened at the same time.

**Fountains of the great deep.** The Hebrew is *מַעְיְנֹת תְּהוֹם רַבָּה* — *ma'yenot tehom rabbah*. *Ma'yan* is *spring, fountain, source* — an outlet through which water comes up under pressure, what we would call an artesian spring. *Tehom* is *the deep* — the same word that appears in Genesis 1:2, *darkness was upon the face of the* tehom, the primordial subterranean water reservoir that Genesis 1:2 said was already there before the firmament divided it. *Rabbah* is *great.* So this is *the fountains of the great deep:* pressurized springs of the enormous underground reservoir. Not rain. Not surface flooding. Subterranean water, under pressure, coming up.

**Windows of heaven.** The Hebrew is *אֲרֻבֹּת הַשָּׁמַיִם* — *arubot ha-shamayim*. *Arubbah* is a *window, lattice, sluice,* or *floodgate* — the same word used in 2 Kings 7:2, when a skeptical official scoffs *if the LORD would make windows in heaven, might this thing be?* It is a normal Hebrew word for an opening, a channel through which something passes. So *arubot ha-shamayim* is *the floodgates of the heavens:* atmospheric channels through which water came down.

Now read what is *not* there. No Hebrew for *tearing,* *rupture,* or *failure.* The firmament — the stretched membrane God made on Day 2 (Chapter 2) — is *not* described as torn. The text describes channels *opening* in it. That matters because of the verb on the fountains. *Baqa* shows up in Exodus 17:6, when Moses strikes the rock at Horeb at the LORD's command and water flows out, and in Numbers 16:31, when the ground opens under Korah at the LORD's command. *Baqa* is the vocabulary of God commanding water through a barrier at His word — not the vocabulary of system failure or catastrophic rupture. It is commanded opening.

So Genesis 7:11 tells us, with precision: at God's command, two channels opened at the same time. The fountains of the underground deep delivered subterranean water from below; the windows of heaven delivered atmospheric water from above. Both commanded. Both stayed open for forty days. Both *closed* later (Genesis 8:2) — and we will get there.

This matters because in Chapter 8 we said the Lawgiver does not change. Genesis 7:11 is not a law-violation. The laws of physics did not get rewritten. What got reset, at God's command, was the boundary condition on two large physical systems — the underground hydrosphere and the atmosphere. The water did what water always does. The drama, again, is in the constraint.

The framework calls this a **zone perturbation event** — a temporary, commanded reset of the boundary conditions on a few zones of the architecture, leaving the laws untouched. The Flood is the longest and most thorough such event between creation and the consummation (more in section 4). For now, hold this: *two boundary-condition resets, simultaneous, commanded, on two distinct water systems.* The Hebrew names both.

## §3. What the Earth Was Like Before the Flood

For the mechanism to make sense, you have to know what the earth was like before the fountains opened. Genesis tells us a little, and modern geophysics has independently filled in a piece that lines up suggestively well. Start with Scripture.

> *"For the LORD God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was not a man to till the ground. But there went up a mist from the earth, and watered the whole face of the ground."*
> — Genesis 2:5–6, KJV

There was no rain. The text says it directly. The pre-Flood atmospheric regime did not produce rainfall; the ground was watered by *mist* — *ed* in Hebrew, groundwater seepage and condensation, not precipitation from clouds.

A confidence label before we go further, because the temptation here is to over-build a pre-Flood atmosphere theory and then defend it like scripture. *The precise pre-Flood atmospheric pressure, temperature, and humidity are LOW confidence.* Nobody can give you the millibar count. The framework's posture: the regime was different (HIGH — Genesis 2:5–6 says so directly), and rainfall was not part of it (HIGH); the specifics are LOW. We honor the difference between what scripture states and what the model speculates.

Now turn to what modern geophysics has found about the *deep* earth, regardless of whether the Flood happened. Three independent results.

First, **ringwoodite** — a mineral that forms in the upper mantle's transition zone, between about two hundred fifty and four hundred miles down. In 2014, a tiny ringwoodite inclusion trapped in a Brazilian diamond was found to contain about one percent water by weight. That sounds small, but the transition zone is enormous: if even a fraction of it is water-bearing ringwoodite at that concentration, the rock under our feet holds *one to three times the water in all the surface oceans combined.* The Earth's interior is wet. We did not know this for most of the modern era.

Second, **deep crustal aquifers** — pressurized water reservoirs in the lower crust, showing up in deep boreholes, fault-zone springs, and geothermal systems. The continents have water-bearing rock thousands of meters down, under pressure, in chambers that connect to fault networks.

Third, **seismic low-velocity zones** — regions where earthquake waves slow down in a way consistent with the rock holding a small percentage of water. The pattern is widespread, not localized to any one continent.

None of this was found by Christian young-Earth geologists trying to vindicate Genesis. All of it was found by ordinary working geophysicists mapping the Earth. The framework is not asking you to take disputed evidence on faith; it is asking you to notice that *standard geophysics has independently found enormous water reserves where Genesis 7:11 said the deep was.* That is a coherence point, not a proof.

Confidence labels for this section. *HIGH:* Earth's interior contains substantial water in mantle minerals and deep crustal aquifers (multiple independent methods); and pre-Flood Earth, in the regions Genesis describes, was watered without rainfall (Genesis 2:5–6, consistent with the rainbow's introduction as a *new* sign in Genesis 9). *MODERATE:* the no-rain regime extended to *planetary scale* (a model inference from the rainbow's debut and the regime-change logic of Genesis 8–9); and the pre-Flood crust was thinner over more accessible reservoirs than today. *LOW:* the exact pre-Flood atmospheric pressure profile.

The picture to hold: a planet whose interior holds *more water* than most readers realized, in pressurized chambers under the surface; and a pre-Flood atmosphere different enough that rainfall was not part of the regime. Both are sober, and both are needed for the mechanism in section 5.

## §4. The Sabbath Boundary, Again

Chapter 10 introduced the **Sabbath Boundary** — the line between *creation-mode* physics (Days 1–6) and *sustaining-mode* physics (Day 7 onward). The Flood is not a creation-mode event; it happens deep into sustaining mode, ten human generations after Adam. So I need to extend the picture.

The point: **sustaining mode is not perfectly uniform.**

Some readers came away from Chapter 10 thinking *creation was weird, but sustaining is steady* — and from there it is a short step to *everything inside sustaining mode obeys uniform extrapolation.* That is wrong. Listen to Peter himself.

> *"Knowing this first, that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts, and saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation. For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water: whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished. But the heavens and the earth, which are now, by the same word are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men."*
> — 2 Peter 3:3–7, KJV

Read it slowly. Peter names an old uniformitarian objection — *all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation* — and calls it willful ignorance, giving the counter-evidence by name: *the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished.* The Flood is the historical proof that the universe is not perfectly uniform, and Peter uses it as the precedent for a future judgment by fire. The argument only works if the Flood happened and perturbed the regime in a way slow extrapolation would have ruled out.

So the Sabbath Boundary is one boundary; it is not the only one. The Foundations volume names a small number of *historical perturbations* — the list is short, which is the point. The rule is sustaining; the perturbations are the exception:

- The **Fall** (Genesis 3) — a phase change in the bio-chemical regime, when a creation that was *very good* came under bondage to decay (Romans 8:20–22).
- The **Flood** (Genesis 7–8) — a temporary reset of crustal and atmospheric boundary conditions; this chapter.
- The **Cross** (Matthew 27, John 19) — the moment the temple veil tore, the rocks split, the graves opened.
- The **Consummation** (2 Peter 3:7, Revelation 21) — the future event Peter is pointing to. By fire, this time.

Each is *named* in scripture. The framework does not invent perturbations; it catalogs the ones the Bible already names.

One clarification, because this is where Christian readers get confused. The framework is *not* claiming every weird thing in the geology is a Flood event. Volcanoes, earthquakes, and ice ages happen; ordinary catastrophe at ordinary scales is part of sustaining mode. The claim is modest: *a small number of named, dated, commanded perturbations sit on top of the ordinary picture, and the Flood is one of them.*

A thoughtful teenager will press: *why then, and why that big?* Here is the framework's *working picture* — labeled plainly as working, not settled. Chapter 8 described how, after the Fall, creation came under *bondage to decay* (Romans 8:20–22) — a downward shift in the sustaining coupling. The suggestion is that a long, slow post-Fall drift in that regime could gradually load a planet's crust-and-water system toward instability — pressures accumulating, margins narrowing — until a commanded opening releases it all at once, the way a slope can be loaded for years before one push brings it down. So the *magnitude* is planetary because the loading was planetary, and the *timing* is "ten generations in" because that is how long the drift took to reach the edge. This is the *least* nailed-down link in the chapter — a working bridge, not a derived number. Treat it as WORKING, and hold it loosely.

## §5. The Mechanism

Now put the pieces together. What does *fountains broke up + windows opened* look like as a coherent physical event? The framework reads it as five plain stages. **Open:** at God's command the pressurized underground reservoirs break open and rise through the existing fault network — a planetary-scale release from below. **Pour:** all that hot subsurface water dumps heat and vapor into the air, the stable no-rain regime breaks, and it rains at planetary scale for the first time — forty days and forty nights. **Collapse:** as the reservoirs empty, the rock above settles into the empty space; ocean basins deepen and the continents ride up relative to them, the way an ice cube rides on water. **Drain:** the boundary conditions return to normal — *"the fountains also of the deep and the windows of heaven were stopped"* (Genesis 8:2) — and the water that stood above the mountains runs off into the new, deeper basins. **Mark:** rain is now part of the world, so the rainbow is now possible, and God sets it in the cloud as the sign of His covenant (Genesis 9:13).

Open. Pour. Collapse. Drain. Mark. Notice the symmetry the text gives you: two things start at once in Genesis 7:11, and two things stop at once in Genesis 8:2. The mechanism opens and closes; it does not run forever. Through all five stages the water simply did what water does — the drama was in the Hand commanding the constraints, never in any new physics. *For families who want to walk all five stages in full — with Psalm 104:6–9 laid alongside the mechanism and the figure of pre-Flood versus post-Flood topography — see Deeper Dive A, "The Rock Record and the Flood," at the back of the book.*

## §7. But What About…?

Now the questions that will come up — plain question, plain answer, no defensiveness, the way I gave the Sabbath Boundary questions in Chapter 10.

**But what about radiometric dating? It says the rocks are billions of years old.**

Two answers, both engineering-clean. First, the same logic as Chapter 10: **functional maturity at creation.** Adam was made an adult, trees with rings, rocks with isotope ratios already in them. A rock made *functional* has a daughter-isotope distribution from the start; a "clock" reading that initial distribution reads *backward* to a date when the rock did not exist. Second, **the Flood broke the closed-system assumption.** Radiometric clocks assume the rock has been a closed system since formation; the Flood involved enormous heat, pressure, and chemical transport, and hot pressurized water can mobilize daughter isotopes — especially lead in uranium-lead — in ways that assumption does not allow.

Together: *radiometric clocks measure something real,* but the dates cannot be honestly extrapolated across the creation boundary or the Flood perturbation. Same family of answer as Chapter 10, different mechanism. HIGH on "decay rates today are constant." MODERATE on "the magnitude of Flood-induced perturbation on isotope distributions" — research-stage.

**But what about the geologic column? How do you explain the order of the fossils?**

This is the one your older student is most likely to be asked head-on in a geology elective. Start with what is not in dispute: the fossils *do* come in a broad order — simpler sea creatures low, complex land-dwellers higher, the "Cambrian explosion" near the bottom. The order is real. The question is *what produced it.* The standard reading says a timeline; the Flood reading says largely *one catastrophic burial event sorting life as it went.*

How would a single Flood produce order? Three ordinary effects a geology student already knows. *Ecological zonation:* sea-bottom creatures lived low and were buried first; land animals and birds lived high and fled longest, buried last. *Hydrodynamic sorting:* moving water sorts bodies by size, shape, and density, the way a flooding river drops gravel before sand before silt. *Differential mobility:* a fast land animal outruns a clam. Together, a single rising, sorting, sediment-laden flood produces a *broadly* ordered sequence — which is what the rocks show.

Now the honest part. The Flood reading explains the *broad* pattern well; it does *not* yet reproduce every fine detail of the column quantitatively — that is one of the open problems §8 names, and I will not pretend otherwise. What your student can say with a clear conscience: *"The order of the fossils is real, and it reads coherently as the sorting record of a single catastrophic flood — ecological zonation, water sorting, and differential escape — and the places that reading hasn't fully worked out yet are named openly, not hidden."* That is far stronger than either pretending the column isn't there or pretending the case is closed. HIGH on the broad order and the three sorting mechanisms; MODERATE on the cleanest single-frame reading; OPEN on the precise full column.

**But what about every species fitting on the Ark? There are millions of species.**

The Bible says *kind,* not *species.* The Hebrew is *min* (pronounced *meen*) — the word in Genesis 1, repeated in Genesis 6, 7, and 8: *kind,* a created reproductive group with significant variation *within* it but no transformation across groups. A *min* is broader than a modern *species:* one *min* might encompass all dogs — wolves, foxes, coyotes, jackals, domestic dogs, many biological species but one Hebrew *kind* — and a representative pair could repopulate the whole *kind* afterward. *Every modern species* did not need to fit on the Ark; *every kind* did. The numbers come down dramatically once you read the Hebrew right. HIGH on "*min* is *kind,* not species"; MODERATE on the number of *kinds* preserved; LOW on the post-Flood diversification rate.

**But what about the geologists who say a global flood is impossible?**

They are honest people doing honest work; the disagreement is not about integrity but about whether the uniform-process assumption holds across all of history. A working geologist says *assuming present rates have always held, this layer took five million years* — a correct deduction *given the assumption.* Drop the assumption — admit, with 2 Peter 3:5–7, that the regime was perturbed — and the same data tells a different story. *Inside* an ordinary sustaining-mode window, uniformitarian extrapolation works well, and the framework is not against it as a *local* tool; it is against pushing it as a *universal* tool across the named perturbations as if they were not in the record. Worth naming plainly for the high-schooler: the *universal* claim that the regime has *never* been perturbed is not a neutral finding of science but a prior commitment — a philosophical, ultimately theological decision about whether history is allowed boundaries. The rocks do not carry that assumption; the reader brings it.

So the framework's position is not *the geologists are wrong about the rocks.* It is *they are right about the rocks and working honestly with the closed-frame assumption their training carries; the assumption is what we need to interrogate.* And honestly — the kind of honesty I wish more young-Earth literature had — there are *specific features* the flood model has not yet reproduced quantitatively: the stratigraphic ordering of certain Tertiary sequences, igneous intrusion patterns in particular terranes, varved lake sediments. Open problems, not solved. The Foundations volume names them.

**But isn't this just hand-waving — "the Flood did it" and walking away?**

No: the framework presents a *working* mechanism, not a closed proof. The Hebrew is solid. The geophysics — water in ringwoodite, deep aquifers — was found by people not looking to vindicate Genesis. The mechanism is coherent stage-to-stage by ordinary fluid mechanics and isostasy, and the Foundations basin-capacity check (roughly a billion cubic kilometers of basin volume against the flood volume needed) shows the mass balance closes. The claim is modest: *one coherent physical mechanism exists, and it lines up with the Hebrew and the geophysics.* Not *the case is closed.* Anyone who tells you the details are all nailed down is overclaiming.

**But does this contradict Chapter 10 on the age of the Earth?**

No. Both rest on the principle that uniformitarian extrapolation across regime boundaries fails, but they are separable. Chapter 10's mechanism was *creation-mode rapid expansion of the firmament* during Days 2–4 — what made the early light reach us. This chapter's is a *post-Sabbath-Boundary commanded perturbation* during Genesis 7–8 — what reset the rocks and atmosphere. The cosmos is young: starlight reaches us because the road grew under the light, and the rocks "date" old because creation made them mature *and* a Flood reset things the standard clock does not account for. Two different events, two different mechanisms, both honest, both required by the data.

## §8. Honest About the Limits

Standard franchise rule. Every chapter ends with what we know, what we are working on, and what is still open.

**HIGH confidence.** The Hebrew of Genesis 7:11 names two synchronized water sources, distinct in vocabulary, simultaneous in timing. Earth's interior contains substantial water, found by independent geophysical methods. Decay rates today are constant to high precision. 2 Peter 3:5–7 names the Flood as a regime perturbation against uniformitarianism. Marine fossils are abundant on mountaintops. Closed clamshells in mountain limestone record rapid burial alive. Mt. St. Helens 1980 deposited finely laminated rock in hours.

**MODERATE confidence.** The specific subterranean reservoir collapse mechanism. The atmospheric reorganization timeline. The crustal uplift ratios. The proposition that the Flood model gives the cleanest single-frame reading of the mosaic of geological evidence.

**LOW confidence.** The exact pre-Flood atmospheric pressure and temperature distribution. The exact pre-Flood continental configuration. The exact magnitude of Flood-induced perturbation on radiometric clocks.

**OPEN problems.** The detailed stratigraphic ordering of certain Tertiary sequences. The pattern of igneous intrusions in particular terranes. Varved lake sediments. The detailed planetary sediment budget. Research-stage. The Foundations volume names them.

What this means for the family in your kitchen. *We do not have to win every detail to keep our footing.* The Hebrew is honest, the geophysics is honest, and the mechanism is coherent at the level needed to teach the kids the basic shape. The open problems get solved by careful work over decades, not by closing one's eyes and chanting. The gospel does not stand or fall on whether the Tertiary stratigraphy is reproduced next year.

It stands on Christ. Always.

## §10. By Faith Noah

Let me close where Hebrews closes the question.

> *"By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith."*

*Things not seen as yet.* That phrase matters. The mechanism we just walked through — fountains and atmosphere and crust and basin and rainbow — was *unseen by Noah.* He had no Foundations textbook, no ringwoodite paper, no knowledge of isostatic compensation or the niphal of *patach.* He had a word from the Maker and a hundred-twenty-year hammer-and-nails program. He built. The ark held. His house was saved.

The mechanism is real. The mechanism is not what saved Noah — *the Word of the One who commanded the fountains is what saved Noah.* The mechanism answers *how could this happen;* the Word answers *who did it, why, and what it means for us now.* Both are honest. Only one is the gospel.

Back to the trout tank, to close. Standing in the bay that night, watching the water find the floor, I was watching what water always does when the constraint changes. I did not wonder whether new physics had broken out. The drama was the constraint, not the physics.

When the fountains opened, the water did what water does. The drama was the *constraint-changer.* He held the fountains down for centuries, commanded them up in one hour of one day, let them run forty days, commanded them stopped, set the bow in the cloud, and saved Noah.

He is the one we are pointing at. Always.

## Key Terms

- **Tehom (תְּהוֹם)** — Hebrew for *the deep.* Used in Genesis 1:2 of the primordial waters and again in Genesis 7:11 (in the form *tehom rabbah,* the *great* deep) of the subterranean reservoir at the time of the Flood.
- **Tehom rabbah (תְּהוֹם רַבָּה)** — *the great deep.* The intensified form of *tehom,* the underground water reservoir whose fountains opened in Genesis 7:11.
- **Ma'yan (מַעְיָן)** — *spring, fountain, source* — an outlet through which water comes up under pressure. The plural *ma'yenot* in Genesis 7:11 names the *outlets* by which the deep released.
- **Arubbah (אֲרֻבָּה)** — *window, lattice, sluice, floodgate.* The same word appears in 2 Kings 7:2; it is a normal Hebrew word for an opening, not an exotic technical term. The plural *arubot* in Genesis 7:11 names the atmospheric channels.
- **Baqa (בָּקַע)** — *to break open, cleave.* Used of the fountains in Genesis 7:11; of Moses striking the rock at Horeb in Exodus 17:6; of the ground opening under Korah in Numbers 16:31. The vocabulary of God commanding water through a barrier, not the vocabulary of system failure.
- **Min (מִין)** — *kind, family of organisms.* Used in Genesis 1, 6, 7, 8. Broader than the modern biological *species.*
- **Zone perturbation event** — In the framework's terminology, a temporary, commanded reset of the boundary conditions on one or more zones of the architecture, leaving the underlying laws untouched. The Flood is the longest and most thorough example between creation and the consummation.
- **Subterranean reservoir model** — The framework's reading of *fountains of the great deep:* pressurized underground water reservoirs released at command, draining upward through the fault network and feeding the global flood from below.
- **Isostatic compensation** — The principle that continental rock floats on denser mantle rock the way an ice cube floats on water; when the mass distribution of the crust changes, the rock rises and falls to find a new equilibrium.
- **Functional maturity at creation** — The principle, established in Chapter 10, that creation was made *to function* from the moment it was made — Adam an adult, trees with rings, rocks with isotope ratios already present. Apparent age without falsified history.
- **Sabbath Boundary** — The boundary between creation-mode physics (Days 1–6) and sustaining-mode physics (Day 7 onward), introduced in Chapter 10. Distinct from the Flood as a perturbation *within* sustaining mode.
- **Uniformitarianism** — The assumption that present rates and conditions have always held. Useful inside ordinary sustaining-mode windows; rejected by 2 Peter 3:3–6 as a universal claim, because the regime has been commanded-perturbed at named historical moments.

## Family Activity — Read Genesis 6–9 Aloud

Pick a Sabbath afternoon. Sit in the living room with the Bible open. Read Genesis 6, 7, 8, and 9 aloud — one chapter per family member if your family is the right size for that, or one chapter per person taking turns. Have a notebook open while you read.

Make a chart with five columns:

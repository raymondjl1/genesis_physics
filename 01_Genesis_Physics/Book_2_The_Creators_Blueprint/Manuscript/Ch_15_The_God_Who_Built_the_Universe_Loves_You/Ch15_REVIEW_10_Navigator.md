### B. Voice Consistency — One Small Note (VERY MINOR)

**Issue:** One sentence in §6 has a slightly different register than the rest:

> *"The Architect of the architecture you have just walked through fourteen chapters of is offering to make you* poiema."

The phrasing "offering to make you" is slightly more evangelistic than the rest of the chapter's register. It reads more like "offer" (sales language) than the chapter's typical posture of quiet invitation.

**Recommended fix (optional):**

Consider: *"The Architect of the architecture you have just walked through fourteen chapters of is inviting you to become* poiema." Or: *"...is calling you to become poiema."*

Or leave as written — the word "offering" is scripture-rooted (John 1:12, "as many as received him...") and the sentence is immediately followed by the cleared-community caveat ("I am not going to push it on you"). The register is defensible.

**Impact:** Very minor. The sentence is theologically sound and the voice is immediately corrected by what follows. Not a mandatory change, but worth considering.

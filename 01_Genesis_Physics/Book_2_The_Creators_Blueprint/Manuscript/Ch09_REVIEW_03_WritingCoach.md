## VOICE CONSISTENCY — PASS

**Observation:** The chapter reads as the author described in `AUTHOR_VOICE_AND_BACKGROUND.md`: operator-engineer, not professor. The opening scene (trampoline, wife, dogs, porch at dusk) is canonical. Short sentences with engineering rhythm. Household earned and never invented. First person sparing ("My wife had brought out two mugs of coffee and come up the porch steps behind me").

**Section §4 exemplifies it:**

## MINOR POLISH OBSERVATIONS

Two observations at the polish level (not breaks; not rewrites):

### 1. §7(b) Flatland Explanation (lines 230–240)

The flatland intuition is correct and helpful. The chapter uses it to answer "why does gravity look like it's just there?" The explanation is adequate, but the flatland description ("imagine a family of two-dimensional people...") could be tightened by one sentence.

**Current (lines 231–232):**
> "Borrow an image from a 19th-century book called *Flatland.* Imagine a family of two-dimensional people living on the surface of our trampoline. They have no concept of *up.*"

**Suggestion:**
> "Borrow an image from a 19th-century book called *Flatland.* Imagine a family of two-dimensional people living on the surface of our trampoline, with no concept of *up.*"

Minor tightening. The figure (Fig 2.9.4) does most of the work; the text can step back slightly.

### 2. §5 Transition (lines 135–137)

The move from "the trampoline in the back pasture" to "now walk a marble to the edge" is smooth, but the grammar reads like an instruction rather than an invitation.

**Current:**
> "Now walk a marble to the edge of the trampoline and let it go."

**Suggestion:**
> "Pick up a marble and walk it to the edge of the trampoline; let it go."

Tighter. More active. Reads as an invitation, not a directive.

**Verdict:** Both are polish-level. Neither breaks the chapter. Ship as-is if timeline is tight; incorporate if a revision pass is planned.

## STRONGEST PASSAGES

### 1. Opening Scene (§1, lines 8–23)

The scene is a model of how to begin a physics chapter for families.

> "It was the tail end of April on the homestead, and the pasture was finally getting its color back. The mule was eating the top of the fence post again. The chickens had given up on the day and walked themselves into the coop. The sun was behind the ridge and the light was going blue. My wife had brought out two mugs of coffee and come up the porch steps behind me. I was watching the back pasture, where the older dog had just jumped onto the trampoline."

Sensory detail. Earned household. First person sparing. The wife is a character. Then: the wife's question. *"Why is it the same wave every time?"* The reader is hooked.

### 2. §4 Pivot (lines 117–119)

The move from "guitar strings follow this rule" to "now apply it to the universe" is where the chapter's power lands.

> "The framework says *of course it has.* The firmament's tension and density do not wander off overnight. The Lawgiver does not change — Chapter 8 walked you all the way into that — the Lawgiver does not change, which means He does not need us to bluff on His behalf."

Operator confidence. Physics and faith in one breath. No separation. The voice test passes.

### 3. §6 Unification (lines 194–196)

> "What do the heavens *declare*? David says they declare God's *glory.* His *handiwork.* They *pour out speech* about Him. Chapter 8 made the point that the laws of physics are the signature of the Lawgiver's unchanging character. Chapter 9's contribution is to say: the *constants* that appear in those laws are specific numbers in the signature. *c* is a number in His signature. *G* is a number in His signature. They are declaring together."

Poetic without being purple. The integration of science and wonder. This is the chapter working at full power.

### 4. §8 Honesty (lines 244–254)

> "I will say *I don't know* before I will say something I have not yet verified. That is the operator's posture, and Chapter 8 called it the Lawgiver's too: the Lawgiver does not change, which means He does not need us to bluff on His behalf."

The franchise's credibility move. The author is willing to confess the limits of the framework in the same breath he claims its strengths. This earns trust more than a thousand footnotes.

**Subtitle:** *What the Bible Really Says About How God Built the Universe*

**Series Position:** Book 3 (Build Order: LAST)

**Reading Level:** Grade 9–12 (intelligent layperson / high school student)
**Word Count:** 60,000–80,000 words (~240–320 pages)
**Voice:** Warm, encouraging teacher — like a knowledgeable parent guiding their kids through the wonder of God's creation
**Equations:** ZERO
**Price Point:** $19.99 paperback / $11.99 ebook
**Format:** Paperback with scripture callout boxes, discussion questions, family activities

---

## Purpose

This book exists for the parent sitting at the kitchen table who wants to teach their children what the Bible actually says about the physical universe — and wants to know that real physics backs it up. It is NOT a watered-down version of Book 2. It is a scripture-FIRST book where the Bible leads and physics confirms. Every major concept begins with "Scripture says..." and ends with "...and here's what modern physics has discovered about exactly that."

---

## What This Book Must Accomplish

1. **Ground everything in Scripture** — direct quotation, chapter and verse, on every major point. Honest, contextual engagement with what the Hebrew text actually says.
2. **Give parents confidence** — they should finish each chapter feeling equipped to explain both the biblical and scientific side to their children.
3. **Make the physics real** — no math, but no hand-waving. Use the same analogies as Book 2 but always anchor them to the specific scripture being discussed.
4. **Provide teaching tools** — discussion questions, family activities, key terms, scripture memory verses tied to physics concepts.
5. **Address common challenges** — every chapter includes a "But What About...?" section addressing the questions kids (and skeptics) actually ask.

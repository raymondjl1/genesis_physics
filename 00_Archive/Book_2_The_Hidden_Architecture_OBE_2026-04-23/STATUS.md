## Notes for Writers

### Voice & Tone
Write as a knowledgeable guide speaking to an intelligent peer who has never encountered this framework. Assume curiosity, not expertise. Use questions to invite discovery, not to teach.

### Structure Per Chapter
1. **Opening:** Ground the reader in a recognizable problem or observation
2. **Exploration:** Navigate the concept with narrative and visual language
3. **Revelation:** Show how the framework provides new understanding
4. **Bridge:** Connect to the next chapter or larger inquiry

### Illustrations
Flag sections requiring visual clarification in the draft. Coordinate with illustrator on dimensionality, spatial concepts, and pattern relationships.

### Word Count
Target 3,500–5,000 words per chapter. Variation is acceptable if pacing requires it (e.g., short opening chapter, longer framework sections).

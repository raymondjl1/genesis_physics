## Governing Principles (from 00_SERIES_VISION.md)

1. **Always Answer Why** — The "why" at this level is answered through analogy, metaphor, and narrative. The reader should FEEL the reason even without math.
2. **One Voice** — Wonder-driven narrative. Brian Cox meets C.S. Lewis. The reader is on a journey of discovery. NOT a textbook. NOT a sermon.
3. **Zero Equations** — Not one. Every concept explained in words and images. If it can't be explained without math, it doesn't belong in this book.
4. **Leave Them Hungry** — The reader should finish wanting Book 1 the way you want the next season of a show.

## Chapter Validation Checklist

### Pre-Writing Check
- [ ] Chapter outline matches `BOOK_SERIES_STRATEGY.md` Book 2 section
- [ ] Corresponding Book 1 chapter identified (for cascade verification)
- [ ] Core concept expressible WITHOUT equations (if it can't be, reconsider)

### Writing Standards
- [ ] Zero equations in this chapter
- [ ] Opens with a hook that makes the reader want to continue
- [ ] Core concept explained through analogy, metaphor, or narrative
- [ ] No jargon without definition at first use
- [ ] Consistent voice throughout (wonder-driven, conversational authority)
- [ ] Chapter ends with satisfaction AND motivation for the next chapter
- [ ] Illustration requirements identified (what visuals would help the reader?)

### Post-Writing Validation
- [ ] **"But Why?" Reader** — PASS
- [ ] **Writing Coach** — PASS (voice consistent, Grade 10-12, engaging)
- [ ] **Consistency Auditor** — PASS (all terms and references correct)
- [ ] **Skeptic** — PASS (no overselling, honest about limits)

### Cascade Verification
- [ ] Every concept in this chapter has a corresponding chapter in Book 1
- [ ] Every Book 1 claim traces to a Foundations volume derivation
- [ ] Nothing in this chapter contradicts or oversimplifies the Book 1 treatment

# Exodus Protocol Novel Series — Claude Instructions

You are working on the **Exodus Protocol** novel series — a sci-fi adventure that secretly reveals Christ as the answer through an Indiana Jones-style mystery. This is the narrative pillar of the larger Exodus Protocol project.

## Core Vision

A sci-fi mystery/adventure series where the protagonist discovers that the physics of the universe was designed — and the designer left breadcrumbs in the first chapter of Genesis. The reveal of Christ as the answer happens through discovery, exploration, and "aha" moments — **never through preaching**.

## Genre & Tone

- **Genre:** Sci-fi, mystery, adventure (Indiana Jones meets The Da Vinci Code meets Contact)
- **Tone:** Fast-paced, wonder-driven, intellectually stimulating
- **Audience:** Adult readers who enjoy sci-fi with real science and mystery with real stakes
- **The secret:** The physics is real (based on the Genesis Physics framework). The reader discovers truth alongside the characters.

## Connection to Genesis Physics

The novel series draws directly from the Genesis Physics research:
- **Zone architecture** is the real physics the characters discover
- **The firmament, waters, membrane** — real concepts from the framework become plot devices
- **The "hidden architecture"** the characters uncover is the same framework described in the nonfiction books
- Use `01_Genesis_Physics/Quality_Control/Reference/` for accurate zone properties, glossary, and biblical references

## Key Principles

- **The science must be accurate** — it's based on the Genesis Physics derivations. Don't make up fake physics.
- **Christ is the answer, discovered not preached** — the characters figure it out through evidence, not sermons
- **Mystery drives the plot** — the reader should be compelled to keep turning pages
- **Real-world anchors** — archaeological sites, historical mysteries, and real fringe science ground the fiction
- **Characters first** — the science serves the story, not the other way around

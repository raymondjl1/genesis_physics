## Key Principles (Apply to ALL Pillars)

1. **Always answer WHY** — no concept, plot point, or game mechanic without the reason behind it
2. **Christ is the answer, never the sermon** — the revelation comes through discovery, not preaching
3. **Build bottom-up** — foundations first, then layers on top
4. **Honest about limits** — acknowledge what's open, uncertain, or incomplete
5. **One voice per product** — each book/product has its own tone; never mix them

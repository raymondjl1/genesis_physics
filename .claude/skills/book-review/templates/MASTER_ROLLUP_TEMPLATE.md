## P1 Critical cross-cutting issues

Issues raised in multiple chapters that share a common root cause. These are the highest-leverage fixes: one decision can clear five findings.

For each:
- **Pattern:** e.g., "Hebrew etymology cited without lexicon reference"
- **Chapters affected:** Ch 1, 3, 5, 11
- **Root cause:** one sentence
- **Root-cause fix:** one sentence — often a standards update or a canonical-reference change rather than per-chapter editing
- **Severity after root fix:** P0 becomes P2, etc.
